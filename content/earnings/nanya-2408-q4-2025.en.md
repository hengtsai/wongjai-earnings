## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue Up NT$11.3B While Costs Barely Moved</h3>
<p>Q4 revenue jumped NT$11.3B (+60% QoQ) to NT$30.09B, yet cost of revenue moved only from NT$15.31B to NT$15.33B. Virtually all incremental revenue flowed straight to gross profit, vaulting gross margin from 18.5% to 49.0% in a single quarter. While the filing does not break down price vs. volume, flat costs alongside surging revenue point to DRAM price increases as the primary driver (author's inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: 39% Operating Margin as Scale Diluted OpEx</h3>
<p>Operating income reached NT$11.78B for a 39.1% operating margin, up from 6.0% in Q3 and a -NT$2.81B loss a year earlier. R&D rose 27% QoQ to NT$2.13B, yet the OpEx ratio fell from 12.5% to 9.9%. With NT$1.60B in non-operating income and a 17.1% effective tax rate, net income hit NT$11.09B (36.9% net margin).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Q4 EPS Alone Surpassed Full-Year Profit</h3>
<p>Q4 EPS of NT$3.58 exceeded full-year EPS of NT$2.13 because the first nine months had accumulated a -NT$1.45 per-share loss. Full-year 2025 revenue surged 95% YoY to NT$66.59B and net income reached NT$6.61B, reversing 2024's -NT$5.08B loss. Book value per share recovered from NT$50.78 in Q3 to NT$54.99.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Cash Flow Swung Sharply Positive, Debt Paydown Began</h3>
<p>Q4 operating cash flow of NT$15.50B against just NT$2.24B in CapEx produced NT$13.26B in quarterly free cash flow, wiping out the 9M deficit of -NT$8.1B and turning full-year FCF positive at NT$5.12B. Long-term debt fell NT$6.3B and short-term debt dropped NT$2.55B in Q4, while cash still rose to NT$58.07B (~NT$42.8B net cash). Inventories declined 15% QoQ, pointing to brisk shipments (author's inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Stock Tripled in 3 Months, Creating Polarized Multiples</h3>
<p>The stock rallied from NT$89.1 after the Q3 call to NT$275 on Jan 19, dipping 1.1% on day one post-call to close at NT$272. That puts P/E at ~128x on full-year EPS of NT$2.13 versus just ~19x on annualized Q4 EPS of NT$14.32—reflecting how heavily earnings were concentrated in Q4. The post-call filing disclosed only preliminary results without guidance, leaving sustainability of DRAM pricing as the key variable.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: Proprietary DRAM Technology and Conservative Balance Sheet</h3>
<p>Backed by Formosa Plastics Group, Nanya Tech manufactures specialty and commodity DRAM using proprietary technology and has long maintained a net cash position. During the loss-making first nine months of 2025, it tapped long-term debt to sustain CapEx, then immediately paid down borrowings once Q4 profits surged, restoring net cash to NT$42.8B. Its vulnerability lies in sharp earnings cyclicality tied to DRAM pricing and smaller scale than the top three memory makers (author's inference).</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue Surge**: Q4 revenue jumped 60% QoQ to NT$30.09B while cost of revenue stayed nearly flat.
- **Margin Inflection**: Gross margin reached 49.0% and operating margin 39.1%, jumping 31pp and 33pp QoQ.
- **Full-Year Turnaround**: Full-year EPS reached NT$2.13, with Q4 alone delivering NT$3.58.
- **Cash Flow Turnaround**: Q4 FCF hit NT$13.26B, borrowings fell NT$8.85B, and net cash stood at ~NT$42.8B.
- **Polarized Multiples**: Full-year P/E is ~128x versus ~19x on annualized Q4 earnings after the stock tripled in 3 months.

> 💡 **Bottom Line**: Nearly all of Nanya Tech's Q4 revenue growth dropped straight to profit—earning more in one quarter than the entire year and kicking off debt paydown—but profitability hinges entirely on DRAM pricing, making sustainability of Q4's lofty gross margin the critical test.
