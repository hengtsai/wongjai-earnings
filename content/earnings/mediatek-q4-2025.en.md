## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Gross margin hits 19-quarter low at 46.1% as memory costs weigh on earnings</h3>
<p>Q4 gross margin fell to 46.1%, the lowest since 2020 Q4, driven mainly by steep DRAM and NAND supplier price hikes and rising foundry costs. CEO Rick Tsai said: "Cost headwinds bottomed out in Q4. Through strict capacity allocation and dynamically passing costs through to end customers, MediaTek is confident in anchoring full-year 2026 gross margin within 46% ± 1.5%."</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Dimensity 9500 pushes mobile revenue above $10B, flagship share tops 30%</h3>
<p>Mobile SoC quarterly revenue jumped to NT$88.6B (+18% QoQ, +8% YoY), lifting full-year mobile revenue past the $10B milestone. Powered by its all-big-core energy-efficient architecture, Dimensity 9500 surpassed 30% share in flagship Android models in China, breaking Qualcomm's high-end monopoly and sharply strengthening brand pricing power.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Mass-producing DGX Spark with NVIDIA as data center ASIC enters breakout year</h3>
<p>MediaTek's deeply co-developed AI chip with NVIDIA (GB10) has entered mass production and begun contributing revenue. Tsai confirmed: "Cloud data center ASIC revenue will exceed $1B in 2026 and scale toward multi-billion dollars in 2027." This marks MediaTek's successful ascent from a pure edge-mobile chipmaker into a top-tier silicon architect capable of custom CSP cloud compute designs.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Wi-Fi 7 triples and 5G modems double on strong edge connectivity demand</h3>
<p>Within Smart Edge Platforms, Wi-Fi 7 chip revenue jumped 300% YoY and 5G Thin Modem revenue doubled, winning spots in tier-one U.S. tablet and laptop supply chains. Despite seasonal softness in consumer electronics, a higher mix of premium connectivity products effectively smoothed mobile cyclicality.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: First to complete TSMC 2nm SoC tape-out, keeping pace with leading rivals</h3>
<p>MediaTek officially announced the tape-out of its chip built on TSMC's 2nm (N2) advanced process, with pilot production slated for 2H 2026 and full commercial mass production in 1H 2027. This secures leading-edge node parity alongside Apple and Qualcomm for the flagship Dimensity 9600 and next-generation AI accelerators.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Memory price hikes keep pressuring gross margin</b>: If DRAM/NAND prices keep surging and phone OEMs cannot pass costs on, MediaTek's 2026 gross margin could break below the 45% psychological threshold.</li>
<li><b>Qualcomm price retaliation in mid-to-high tiers</b>: If Qualcomm launches a price war in mid-tier Snapdragon chips to defend against Dimensity share gains, MediaTek's shipment momentum and margins could be squeezed.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Lengthening global smartphone replacement cycles</b>: If global macro recovery disappoints, mass-market upgrade demand could soften, dampening orders for mainstream Dimensity chips.</li>
<li><b>Competition for CoWoS advanced packaging capacity</b>: If cloud hyperscalers crowd out TSMC advanced packaging allocation, 2H ASIC deliveries could face delays.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>AI PC & smart cockpit takeoff</b>: Volume shipments of edge AI PC processors co-developed with NVIDIA could open a multi-billion-dollar blue-ocean market ahead of schedule.</li>
<li><b>High dividend yield downside protection</b>: Consistent 80%–85% cash payout ratio implies a 4%–5% dividend yield, attracting long-term institutional capital.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Record full-year revenue**: FY25 revenue approached NT$596B with EPS of NT$66.16, as flagship Dimensity 9500 solidified its Android high-end footprint.
- **Gross margin bottoming out**: Though 46.1% marked a 19-quarter low, management showed strong pricing and capacity discipline, guiding 2026 gross margin to stabilize around 46%.
- **Entering the multi-billion data center blue ocean**: The NVIDIA-partnered GB10 chip propels ASIC revenue past $1B in 2026 and toward multi-billion dollars in 2027.
- **Leading 2nm tape-out execution**: First to complete TSMC 2nm SoC tape-out, staying neck-and-neck with U.S. giants and shedding the "second-tier chipmaker" label.
- **Significant valuation re-rating potential**: At 16.5x Forward PE with a >4% cash dividend yield, MediaTek combines defensive support with AI transformation upside in semiconductors.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: MediaTek established high-end mobile credibility with Dimensity 9500 and teamed up with NVIDIA to unlock a second multi-billion-dollar growth curve in data center ASICs, priming the stock for a re-rating as gross margins bottom.
