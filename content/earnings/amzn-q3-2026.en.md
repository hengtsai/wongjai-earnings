## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: AWS accelerates for four straight quarters to a $184B run rate</h3>
<p>AWS revenue growth climbed from 20% in Q3'25 to 24%, 28%, 37% and 39%, with operating income of $17.8B, up 55% YoY, or 63% of company operating income. Spending by Fortune 500 customers on Bedrock rose nearly 7 times YoY and token usage nearly 17 times. Jassy said <b>AWS third-quarter revenue grew more than 39% YoY (a $184B annualized run rate), and the company expects revenue growth above 40% in both 2027 and 2028</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Retail revenue slows, but margins improve markedly</h3>
<p>North America revenue grew only 6% and online stores 2%, mainly because Prime Day moved to Q2 this year. North America operating margin was 7.7% (4.5% last year) and International 3.7% (2.9% last year). But other operating expenses were $2.88B in last year's Q3 versus only $130M this year, inflating the YoY gain. Jassy said <b>broader selection and faster delivery are helping Amazon win more retail share while margins keep improving</b>; in Q3, 1.5 billion items were delivered same-day or next-day.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: $77.3B of quarterly capex, now funded with debt</h3>
<p>Q3 purchases of property and equipment were $77.3B, up 120% YoY and above operating cash flow of $42.4B, for quarterly free cash flow of -$33.5B. Trailing 12-month net capex rose $94.8B YoY, which the press release said <b>primarily reflects investments in artificial intelligence</b>. Amazon has issued $115.1B of long-term debt this year ($48.1B in Q3 alone), and long-term debt rose from $65.6B to $173.1B in nine months, for net debt of about $58.9B. Full-year cash capex is maintained at about $220B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Net income driven by investment gains and losses, with more volatility</h3>
<p>EPS fell 3% YoY because last year's Q3 included a $9.5B pretax gain on Anthropic; this year's Q2 net income of $62.6B and EPS of $5.75 also came mainly from non-operating items. Nine-month non-operating income was $68.2B, 46% of pretax income. Other assets rose $236.2B in nine months, and the investment portfolio is now large enough to swing EPS, so earnings should be judged mainly on operating income.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Ads briefly slow as new businesses start charging</h3>
<p>Advertising revenue grew 12% YoY ex-FX; the company said this reflected timing effects such as Prime Day and that it <b>expects fourth-quarter advertising revenue growth to return to the high teens</b>. Zoox began charging for rides in Las Vegas, the Amazon Leo satellite network is expected to start initial service by year-end and has signed with AT&T, and pharmacy customers grew more than 60% YoY. Jassy said <b>there has never been a business with over a trillion dollars in revenue, and Amazon may have two within the next 5 to 10 years</b>.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q4 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: AWS scale + its own logistics network</h3>
<p>AWS has a $184B annualized run rate and an operating margin near 39%, and locks in enterprise customers with in-house Trainium chips and the Bedrock model platform. In retail, third-party sellers account for 61% of units, its own logistics network keeps expanding same-day and next-day delivery, and ads and subscriptions monetize the traffic, with the three reinforcing one another.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Negative cash flow, now relying on debt</b>: Trailing 12-month free cash flow is -$42.4B, long-term debt rose $107.5B in nine months, and interest expense is already 3 times last year's.</li>
<li><b>Investment portfolio volatility</b>: Nine-month non-operating income of $68.2B was 46% of pretax income, so a pullback in AI company valuations would erode EPS.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Slower retail growth</b>: North America revenue +6% and online stores +2%; Prime Day timing explains only about 4 percentage points.</li>
<li><b>Supply chain and costs</b>: Guidance risks now include memory chip supply volatility, and depreciation up 25% YoY will keep pressuring AWS margins.</li>
<li><b>Capex definition</b>: Estimated Q4 spending is far below Q3; whether it keeps rising in 2027 depends on the call and next quarter's report.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>AWS acceleration</b>: Four straight quarters of acceleration to 39%, with the company expecting more than 40% in both 2027 and 2028.</li>
<li><b>New business optionality</b>: Leo satellites, Zoox robotaxis and the pharmacy are all charging now, providing long-term growth sources.</li>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **AWS is the main engine**: Up 39% YoY with a $184B run rate and 63% of company operating income, and expected to grow more than 40% in each of the next two years.
- **Record operating margin**: Operating income of $28.1B (+61%), a 13.9% operating margin, with North America margin up from 4.5% to 7.7%.
- **EPS looks like it fell**: Net income of -2% reflects a high base from last year's Anthropic investment gain; this year non-operating was actually -$1.2B.
- **Cash flow turns negative**: Quarterly capex of $77.3B, trailing 12-month free cash flow of -$42.4B, and $115.1B of debt issued this year.
- **Solid Q4 guidance**: Revenue of $240–247B and operating income of $28.5–33.5B, with ad growth expected to recover.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Amazon's core business has never been more profitable and AWS has re-accelerated, but AI capex now exceeds operating cash flow and is being funded with debt. Next to watch: whether AWS's 40% growth can keep up with depreciation and interest.
