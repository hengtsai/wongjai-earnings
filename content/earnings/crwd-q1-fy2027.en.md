## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Record Q1 Net New ARR</h3>
<p>Q1 net new ARR was $256M, up 32% YoY for a Q1 record, and ending ARR was $5.51B, up 24% YoY, both above guidance. Founder and CEO George Kurtz called this quarter a <b>Mythos moment</b> where cybersecurity meets frontier AI, describing CrowdStrike as <b>AI security infrastructure</b>, with expansion by existing customers, new customer wins, and partner engagement all rising together.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Profit and Cash Flow Expand</h3>
<p>Non-GAAP operating income was $326M, up 62% YoY, and operating margin rose from 18.2% to 23.5%; on a GAAP basis the company swung to a profit, with net income attributable of $27.8M. Operating cash flow of $591M and free cash flow of $468M were both records. CFO Burt Podbere said the company is <b>expanding profitability and cash flow while accelerating growth</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: AI Security Partnerships with OpenAI and Anthropic</h3>
<p>CrowdStrike launched the QuiltWorks security coalition, bringing in OpenAI and Anthropic to address frontier AI risks through Falcon; the press release said it is the only cybersecurity company selected for both AI labs' security programs, and it also launched Charlotte AI AgentWorks.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Full-Year Guidance Raised</h3>
<p>FY27 ending ARR guidance was raised from $6.47–6.52B to $6.53–6.56B, and guidance for revenue of $5.91–5.96B, Non-GAAP operating income of $1.45–1.48B, and EPS of $4.88–4.96 was also raised; the midpoint of net new ARR growth guidance rose 5.2pp to 27.7%. Podbere said the Q2 pipeline is at a record and customer retention remains strong.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: 4-for-1 Split Announced</h3>
<p>The board approved a 4-for-1 stock split: shareholders of record on 6/25 receive 3 additional shares for each share held, with split-adjusted trading starting 7/2. A split does not change the company's value, but it lowers the per-share price, which can improve liquidity and retail participation (our inference). On the first day after earnings the stock fell from $747.6 to $719.1 (pre-split), down 3.8%.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Moat and FY27 Guidance

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Falcon single-agent platform</h3>
<p>CrowdStrike covers endpoint, cloud, identity, data, and SIEM with a single lightweight agent and the cloud-native Falcon platform; module adoption keeps rising, and Falcon Flex lets customers expand usage flexibly. It has been named a Leader in Gartner's endpoint protection Magic Quadrant seven times. The weaknesses are an extremely high valuation and the ongoing need to maintain customer trust after the July 2024 outage.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **ARR acceleration**: Net new ARR of $256M (+32%), a Q1 record; ending ARR of $5.51B (+24%).
- **Beat across the board**: Revenue of $1.386B, Non-GAAP operating income of $326M, and EPS of $1.10 all topped the high end of guidance.
- **Record cash flow**: Operating cash flow of $591M; free cash flow of $468M.
- **AI security push**: QuiltWorks partnership with OpenAI and Anthropic; launched Charlotte AI AgentWorks.
- **Guidance and split**: Full-year guidance raised; 4-for-1 split announced.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: CrowdStrike's FY27 Q1 delivered record net new ARR and cash flow, a raised full-year outlook, and AI security as a new growth story; but at a P/E near 150x, beating across the board still earned only a small drop in the stock, and what matters next is whether ARR acceleration can continue.
