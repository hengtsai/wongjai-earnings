## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue above the top of guidance</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue of NT$142.10B exceeded the NT$130.1–140.0B guidance top end given in July, up 7.8% YoY. Management attributed the 5.5% QoQ decline mainly to <b>"some customers pulling in orders into the first half"</b>, while YoY growth came from <b>"market share gains in AI flagship smartphone and AI tablet chips"</b>. With an assumed FX rate of 29, actual TWD movements also affected revenue (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Gross Margin Returns to 46.5%</h3>
<p>Gross margin was 46.5%, within the 45.5–48.5% guidance and 2.6pp below Q2; management noted Q2 included a 1.9pp one-off benefit, while the 2.3pp YoY drop <b>"reflected gross margin changes in certain products"</b>. Gross profit of NT$66.11B grew 2.7% YoY, slower than revenue.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Operating Profit Down 7% YoY, Non-Op Supports EPS</h3>
<p>R&D expenses rose 9.3% YoY to NT$36.35B (25.6% of revenue), pushing operating profit down 7% YoY to NT$22.19B and operating margin from 18.1% to 15.6%. Non-operating income of NT$7.77B—NT$3.2B higher than last year—limited the YoY decline in attributable net profit to 0.5%, keeping EPS at NT$15.84 versus NT$15.94 a year ago.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Solid Cash Flow & Strong Balance Sheet</h3>
<p>Nine-month operating cash flow reached NT$98.27B and free cash flow was ~NT$87.9B against just NT$10.4B in capex. Cash stood at NT$197.77B versus NT$15.4B in short-term debt for ~NT$182.3B in net cash, while inventory fell 2% from year-start. With NT$420.1B in equity, the balance sheet has ample room to keep funding R&D.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Q4 Guidance at NT$142.1–150.6B</h3>
<p>Q4 revenue guidance is NT$142.1–150.6B (assuming USD/TWD of 30.6), with a NT$146.35B midpoint up ~3% QoQ and ~6% above Q4 '24's NT$138.04B; gross margin is guided at 46%±1.5% and opex ratio at 31%±2%. Shares edged down from NT$1,310 to NT$1,300 on the first day after earnings.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q4 Guidance

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: Smartphone SoC Scale and Platform Diversification</h3>
<p>MediaTek is a leading global smartphone SoC vendor whose Dimensity line has broken into high-end AI flagship phones while extending into tablets, smart edge devices, automotive, and AI accelerator ASICs. Spending over 25% of revenue on R&D allows it to fund multiple product lines simultaneously. Its weakness is smartphone market maturity, leaving revenue highly sensitive to handset demand and pricing competition.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue topped guidance**: Q3 revenue of NT$142.10B (+7.8%) beat the top end of guidance, while the 5.5% QoQ dip reflected earlier customer pull-ins.
- **Gross margin normalized**: 46.5%, within guidance, as Q2 had included a one-off benefit.
- **Core profit down**: Operating profit fell 7% YoY, while non-operating gains kept EPS flat at NT$15.84.
- **Solid finances**: Nine-month free cash flow of ~NT$87.9B and net cash of ~NT$182.3B.
- **Q4 guidance**: Revenue of NT$142.1–150.6B, up ~3% QoQ at the midpoint.

> 💡 **One-line takeaway**: MediaTek's Q3 revenue topped guidance on AI flagship chip share gains, but lower gross margin and higher R&D trimmed core operating profit, leaving EPS supported by non-operating income; at ~20x P/E, the market is waiting for new AI products to drive the next leg of growth.
