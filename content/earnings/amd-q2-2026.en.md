## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Record revenue, data center doubles</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue was $11.54B, up 50% YoY and slightly above the top of the $11.2B ± $300M guidance. Data center revenue was $6.72B, up 107% YoY and 58% of revenue. CEO Lisa Su said: <b>"We delivered an excellent quarter, with record revenue and profitability as Data Center revenue more than doubled year-over-year."</b> She added: "We enter the second half with strong momentum as EPYC demand accelerates, Instinct deployments scale and Helios begins to ramp."</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Helios rack and MI400 launch</h3>
<p>AMD launched the Helios rack-scale AI server, with the press release listing Anthropic, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, Oracle and others as deploying it, and introduced the MI400 series and 6th-gen EPYC. Anthropic will deploy up to 2GW of MI450 in Helios racks, and Microsoft will deploy at scale in Azure.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Client strong, Gaming weak</h3>
<p>Client revenue was $3.06B, up 23% YoY on strong Ryzen processor demand; Gaming revenue was $780M, down 31% YoY on lower semi-custom (console) revenue. Embedded was $980M, up 19% YoY as demand strengthened across several end markets. Combined Client and Gaming operating income was $582M, below $767M a year ago.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Margins improve sharply, capex doubles</h3>
<p>Non-GAAP operating margin was 27%, 15pp above 12% a year ago (the base included the MI308 charge). Q2 capex was $808M, 2.9 times the year-ago level, and free cash flow was $1.56B, up 32% YoY. There were no buybacks this quarter, and net cash at quarter-end was about $9.9B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Q3 revenue guided to about $13B, data center accelerates in 2H</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue is guided to about $13B ± $300M, up about 41% YoY and about 13% QoQ, with Non-GAAP gross margin of about 56%. CFO Jean Hu said: <b>"We expect Data Center sales to accelerate in the second half of 2026, driving stronger overall revenue growth and continued earnings expansion."</b></p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q3 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: a full lineup of CPU + GPU + racks</h3>
<p>AMD is one of the few companies with server CPUs (EPYC), AI GPUs (Instinct), networking (Pensando) and a rack-scale solution (Helios), and it courts developers with its open ROCm software. Adoption of Helios by large AI labs and cloud providers makes it the main alternative to NVIDIA.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Very high valuation</b>: P/E on TTM GAAP EPS is above 120x, so the stock depends heavily on the AI GPU ramp.</li>
<li><b>Export controls</b>: Last year AMD booked an $800M charge on MI308 because of export controls; policy risk remains.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Gaming decline</b>: Gaming revenue fell 31% YoY as semi-custom chips hit a cyclical low.</li>
<li><b>Higher capex</b>: Q2 capex nearly tripled YoY, squeezing free cash flow.</li>
<li><b>One-time gain</b>: GAAP net income includes a $483M gain on long-term investments.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Major customer deals</b>: Anthropic up to 2GW, and Microsoft Azure deploying Helios at scale.</li>
<li><b>Net cash</b>: Cash and short-term investments of $13.1B against only $3.2B of debt.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Record revenue**: Q2 revenue of $11.54B (+50%), slightly above the top of guidance.
- **Data center doubles**: Data center revenue of $6.72B (+107%), 58% of revenue, with a 31% segment operating margin.
- **AI push**: Helios racks and MI400 launched; Anthropic up to 2GW and Microsoft Azure deploying at scale.
- **Earnings surge**: Non-GAAP EPS of $1.66 (+246%), though the base included the MI308 charge.
- **Strong guidance**: Q3 revenue guided to about $13B, up about 41% YoY, with data center accelerating in 2H.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: AMD's data center business doubled in Q2 and Helios and MI400 won major customers such as Anthropic and Microsoft, so the growth momentum is clear; but with a P/E above 100x and Gaming in decline, the stock still fell 7% after earnings.
