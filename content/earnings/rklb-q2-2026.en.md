## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: An $8B Iridium deal turns a supplier into a constellation operator</h3>
<p>On 6/28 the company signed a deal to acquire Iridium Communications for $54 per share ($27 in cash plus stock), an enterprise value of about $8B and the largest deal in Rocket Lab's history. CEO Peter Beck said in the press release that it makes the company <b>a front-line space power that launches its own assets</b>. Iridium has 66 satellites, more than 2.5 million subscribers and annual revenue above $870M, close to Rocket Lab's own annual revenue. The exchange ratio has a collar: when RKLB's average price is between $67.50 and $112.50, the stock portion is fixed at $27 of value.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Backlog up 137% YoY, with space systems making up 60%</h3>
<p>Backlog was $2.356B, with space systems at 60%. During and after the quarter the company signed more than $437M of launch contracts and more than $581M of space systems contracts, including <b>$397M</b> for the US Space Force SB-AMTI program (including options; it will launch its own Flatellite satellites on Neutron, and Rocket Lab is one of only two "launch + satellite" suppliers), and over $160M for three geostationary satellites. Beck said in the press release that since Q3 began the company had signed <b>more than $1B</b> of new contracts.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Gross margin tops the high end of guidance, but the company flags a 6pp drop in Q3</h3>
<p>GAAP gross margin was 36.1% and non-GAAP 41.5%, both more than 1 percentage point above the high end of guidance. Launch revenue per mission rose to $9.1M while cost fell to $4.4M, lifting launch gross margin from 30.5% to 42.9%. But Q3 guidance calls for a non-GAAP gross margin of only 35–37%: the first full quarter with Mynaric consolidated will dilute margins. Whether gross margin can hold 40% depends on how fast the acquired businesses are integrated.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Neutron still slated to reach the pad in Q4, but the window for a year-end first flight is narrowing</h3>
<p>The 10-Q wording turned noticeably more cautious: first-stage tank production is currently on track for the goal of delivery to the pad in Q4 2026, but <b>the window for a launch before year-end is narrowing</b>, with exact timing depending on first-stage qualification testing in the second half. The Archimedes engine has passed more than 400 hot fires.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: EBITDA loss shrinks fast, but cash flow worsens</h3>
<p>Adjusted EBITDA was -$8.8M, far better than guidance of -$20M to -$26M, but free cash flow was -$110M, worse than Q1's -$77.4M. The gap comes from working capital: receivables rose nearly twofold in six months and inventory rose 69%. CFO Adam Spice said cash burn will stay high because of Neutron, capacity expansion and infrastructure, and expects adjusted EBITDA to turn positive in the quarter after a successful Neutron test flight, with standalone cash flow turning positive another 18–24 months later.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Moat and Q3 Guidance (with Actual Results)

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: wider vertical integration, adding laser communications and space robotics</h3>
<p>In Q2 the company completed the acquisitions of German laser communications maker Mynaric and space robotics maker Motiv, and set up Rocket Lab Germany to target European sovereign space demand. On the launch side it introduced the globally deployable GHOST launch system, with its first site at Kodiak, Alaska (its 4th launch site), expected to open in 2027 to fly up to 18 HASTE suborbital missions worth $266M for the Space Force. Electron flew 12 times in the first half, with cost per launch down to $4.4M.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Neutron schedule</b>: the company itself admits the year-end first-flight window is narrowing, and contracts such as SB-AMTI specify Neutron launches, so delays would also affect backlog recognition.</li>
<li><b>The large Iridium acquisition</b>: an enterprise value of about $8B for a target with annual revenue comparable to Rocket Lab's, still requiring shareholder and regulatory approval, with high financing and integration risk.</li>
<li><b>Extreme valuation</b>: at the 8/11 close, market cap was about $51.3B, roughly 67 times TTM revenue of $769M.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Gross margin pullback</b>: the Q3 non-GAAP gross margin guidance midpoint is about 5.5 percentage points below Q2, as acquired businesses carry lower margins.</li>
<li><b>Working capital drag</b>: receivables and inventory tied up about $136M of cash in the first half, and free cash outflow widens quarter by quarter.</li>
<li><b>Equity dilution</b>: weighted average shares rose 22% YoY, and the stock consideration for Iridium plus future raises would expand the share count further.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Stronger balance sheet</b>: cash and investments of $2.39B, with convertible notes down from $152M to $13M, leaving almost no financial debt.</li>
<li><b>Defense backlog visibility</b>: SB-AMTI, geostationary satellites and HASTE contracts make the Space Force a multi-product customer.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Beat across the board**: revenue of $234M (+62%), non-GAAP gross margin of 41.5% and adjusted EBITDA of -$8.8M.
- **Iridium changes the company's identity**: the $8B deal moves Rocket Lab from rocket and satellite supplier to an operator with its own constellation and subscribers.
- **Strong order momentum**: backlog of $2.36B (+137%), with more than $1B more signed early in Q3.
- **Cash flow is the weak spot**: free cash flow of -$110M, a growing working capital drag, and profitability timing tied to Neutron's first flight.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Q2 was Rocket Lab's strongest and most ambitious quarter, beating across the board and moving to buy Iridium, but the 10-Q line about a narrowing window already foreshadowed the next quarter's Neutron delay.
