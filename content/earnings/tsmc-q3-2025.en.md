## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Gross Margin Tops Guidance Upper End by 2pp on FX and Volume</h3>
<p>Q3 gross margin reached 59.5% against guidance of 55.5–57.5%, and operating margin hit 50.6% versus guidance of 45.5–47.5%, both exceeding the upper end by 2–3pp. Guidance assumed an FX rate of 29.0, while the actual average was 29.91—a weaker Taiwan dollar that aided gross margin—and wafer shipments rose 9.9% QoQ. CFO Wendell Huang stated that <b>third-quarter business was supported by strong demand for leading-edge process technologies</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: USD Revenue of $33.1B Tops Guidance Upper End</h3>
<p>USD revenue climbed 10.1% QoQ and 40.8% YoY, above the $31.8–33.0B guidance top. In TWD terms, growth was only 30.3% YoY due to Taiwan dollar appreciation (average FX strengthened 7.5% YoY from 32.32 to 29.91). In other words, TSMC grew over 40% in USD, with FX shaving roughly 10 percentage points off TWD-reported growth.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: 3nm Rises to 23%, Advanced Nodes Reach 74%</h3>
<p>3nm accounted for 23% of wafer revenue, 5nm 37%, and 7nm 14%, bringing advanced nodes to 74%, up from 73% in Q1. Per the presentation bar chart, revenue from 7nm and below totaled roughly NT$640B, the highest quarterly level since 2023. Mature nodes (28nm and above) combined for ~19%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: HPC at 57%, Smartphone Up 19% QoQ</h3>
<p>HPC accounted for 57% of revenue with flat QoQ sales; Smartphone represented 30% (+19% QoQ), IoT +20%, Automotive +18%, while Consumer Electronics fell 20% QoQ. Flat HPC off a high base alongside smartphone new-model stocking drove the 6% QoQ revenue increase. The press release did not disclose AI accelerator revenue or CoWoS figures.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: CapEx Up 39% YoY, Free Cash Flow Declines</h3>
<p>Q3 CapEx jumped 39% YoY to NT$287.5B while operating cash flow grew just 9%, pushing free cash flow down 25% YoY to NT$139.4B. Cash and marketable securities still stood at NT$2.75T (+27% YoY), and stockholders' equity surpassed NT$5T. For Q4, the CFO noted that <b>business will continue to be supported by strong demand for leading-edge process technologies</b>, guiding gross margin to 59–61%.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>FX headwind</b>: A 7.5% YoY appreciation in TWD shaved ~10pp off TWD revenue growth relative to USD, and further appreciation would compress gross margin.</li>
<li><b>Geopolitics</b>: Manufacturing capacity is concentrated in Taiwan, leaving customer demand directly exposed to shifts in US tariff and export-control policies.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Rising CapEx</b>: Q3 CapEx climbed 39% YoY, pulling free cash flow down 25% YoY.</li>
<li><b>High HPC base</b>: Quarterly HPC revenue was flat; if AI customer orders slow, 57% of total revenue faces direct exposure.</li>
<li><b>Weak consumer electronics</b>: Consumer electronics fell 20% QoQ and other platforms dropped 8% QoQ, showing uneven non-AI demand.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Margins top guidance</b>: Both gross and operating margins exceeded the upper end of guidance by 2–3pp, with Q4 guidance midpoints moving higher.</li>
<li><b>Operational efficiency</b>: Inventories declined QoQ with DIO down to 74 days, alongside NT$2.75T in cash and marketable securities.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Record revenue and profit**: Revenue NT$989.9B (+30%), EPS NT$17.44 (+39%), with USD revenue above guidance top.
- **Gross margin tops guidance**: 59.5% was 2pp above the top end and operating margin of 50.6% was 3pp above, aided by FX and shipment volume.
- **Continued node migration**: 3nm at 23% and 5nm at 37%, with advanced nodes totaling 74%.
- **Platform rotation**: HPC held at 57% flat QoQ, while Smartphone rose 19% QoQ and IoT and Automotive each grew ~20%.
- **Expansion weighs on cash flow**: CapEx rose 39% YoY, reducing free cash flow 25% YoY to NT$139.4B.

> 💡 **Bottom Line**: TSMC topped the upper end of guidance across USD revenue, gross margin, and operating margin in Q3 2025, with 3nm and HPC continuing to lift the mix; TWD appreciation and expansion CapEx were the two factors tempering TWD-reported growth and cash flow.
