## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Data Center Hits Record $5.4B, Up 24% QoQ</h3>
<p>Data center revenue of $5.38B rose 39% YoY and 24% QoQ on strong EPYC demand and continued Instinct GPU ramp, generating $1.75B in operating income (32.6% margin). Full-year data center revenue reached $16.6B (+32% YoY). CEO Lisa Su said AMD entered 2026 with strong momentum <b>driven by accelerating EPYC and Ryzen adoption and rapid expansion of its data center AI business</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Non-GAAP Gross Margin 57%, Aided by One-Time MI308 Items</h3>
<p>Non-GAAP gross margin was 57% (+3pp YoY), but benefited from ~$360M of MI308 inventory reserve releases and ~$390M in MI308 revenue to China; excluding those, management said it was ~55%. Q1 non-GAAP gross margin guidance of ~55% aligns with that ex-MI308 baseline. Full-year non-GAAP gross margin was 52% (-1pp YoY), reflecting first-half MI308 export-control charges.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Client Hits Record $3.1B on Continued Share Gains</h3>
<p>Client revenue reached a record $3.10B (+34% YoY), driven by strong Ryzen processor demand and continued share gains according to management; full-year client revenue rose 51% to $10.6B. Gaming grew 50% YoY but fell 35% QoQ due to seasonal semi-custom shipments. Embedded grew 3% YoY as demand strengthened across multiple end markets.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Record Full-Year Revenue of $34.6B and FCF of $5.5B</h3>
<p>Lisa Su said <b>2025 was a defining year for AMD with record revenue and earnings</b>. Full-year revenue rose 34% to $34.6B, non-GAAP EPS grew 26% to $4.17, and free cash flow surged 129% to $5.5B. CFO Jean Hu noted the company <b>delivered record non-GAAP operating income and free cash flow while expanding strategic investments</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Q1 Guidance Down 5% QoQ, Stock Drops Sharply</h3>
<p>Q1 revenue guidance is ~$9.8B ± $300M (including ~$100M in MI308 revenue to China), implying ~32% YoY growth and ~5% QoQ decline at the midpoint, with ~55% non-GAAP gross margin. Inventory rose 38% YoY to $7.9B. On the first trading day post-earnings, shares fell 17% from $242 to $200. At CES 2026, AMD showcased its rack-scale Helios platform and MI440X.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Record revenue**: Q4 revenue hit $10.3B (+34%), with record data center ($5.4B, +39%) and client ($3.1B) segments.
- **One-time margin boost**: Non-GAAP gross margin was 57%, or ~55% excluding the MI308 release and China revenue.
- **Full-year records**: 2025 revenue reached $34.6B, non-GAAP EPS $4.17, and free cash flow $5.5B.
- **Q1 sequential dip**: Guidance of ~$9.8B implies a 5% QoQ decline, with ~55% gross margin.
- **Sharp sell-off**: Stock fell 17% on Day 1 as the market reacted sharply to sequential-decline guidance.

> 💡 **One-line takeaway**: AMD capped a record 2025 with all-time highs in data center and client revenue, yet one-time gross margin tailwinds and Q1 guidance down 5% QoQ drove a 17% first-day sell-off.
