## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: AWS accelerates to 24%, custom chips top $10B run rate</h3>
<p>AWS YoY revenue growth accelerated from 17%, 17%, and 20% to 24%, with operating income of $12.5B (+17% YoY), though operating margin slipped from 36.9% to 35.0% as higher depreciation weighed on profitability. Trainium and Graviton combined now exceed a $10B annual revenue run rate, growing triple digits; 1.4M Trainium2 chips are deployed and fully subscribed, and Trainium3 supply is expected to be nearly fully committed before mid-2026. Jassy noted that <b>AWS grew 24%, its fastest pace in 13 quarters</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: 2026 capex guided at ~$200B, over 50% above 2025</h3>
<p>Jassy said that <b>given such strong demand and landmark opportunities in AI, chips, robotics, and low earth orbit satellites, Amazon expects to invest about $200B in capital expenditures across the company in 2026, and anticipates strong long-term return on invested capital</b>. Net capex in 2025 was $128.3B (+65% YoY), reducing free cash flow from $38.2B to $11.2B; $200B implies roughly a 56% increase over 2025 (our calculation; the press release did not specify gross vs. net).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Three special charges total $2.4B; ex-charges margin 12.8%</h3>
<p>Q4 operating income included three special charges: $1.1B for tax disputes in Italy and a lawsuit settlement, $730M in estimated severance costs, and $610M in asset impairments primarily related to physical stores. Excluding these, the press release reported operating income of $27.4B and a 12.8% operating margin. North America margin rose from 8.0% to 9.0%, while International fell from 3.0% to 2.1%. EPS rose only 4.8% as the effective tax rate increased from 10.4% to 18.6%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Ads grow 23%, AI shopping assistant drives $12B in sales</h3>
<p>Advertising revenue reached $21.3B, up 23% YoY (+22% ex-FX), holding above 22% for three straight quarters. The press release said over 300M customers used the Rufus shopping assistant in 2025, <b>delivering nearly $12B in incremental annualized sales</b>. U.S. same-day delivery items jumped nearly 70% YoY, reaching nearly 100M customers; Alexa+ rolled out to all U.S. customers, free for Prime members and $19.99/month for non-members.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Wide Q1 operating income guidance as Leo satellite costs rise</h3>
<p>Q1 revenue guidance of $173.5B–$178.5B (+11–15%) assumes roughly 1.8 percentage points of FX tailwind; operating income is guided at $16.5B–$21.5B, with the low end below last year's $18.4B. The press release said this <b>includes roughly $1B in higher YoY costs as Amazon Leo scales</b>, alongside investments in faster delivery and international price cuts. International operating income already fell 21% YoY this quarter, making investment payback pace worth watching.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q1 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: AWS scale + in-house chips + its own logistics network</h3>
<p>AWS has surpassed a $140B annual revenue run rate, Graviton is used by over 90% of the top 1,000 AWS customers, and Trainium powers Anthropic's Project Rainier cluster. In retail, third-party sellers represent 61% of units, in-house logistics expands same-day delivery, and ads and subscriptions monetize traffic.</p>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **AWS reaccelerates**: Up 24% YoY, fastest in 13 quarters, with custom chips above $10B annual run rate.
- **Profit includes special charges**: Operating income of $25.0B (+18%), or 12.8% margin excluding $2.4B in charges.
- **Slower EPS growth**: Tax rate rose from 10.4% to 18.6%, limiting EPS growth to 4.8%.
- **Cash flow compression**: Full-year net capex of $128.3B (+65%) pulled free cash flow down to $11.2B.
- **Stepping up in 2026**: Jassy expects ~$200B in capex, and the low end of Q1 operating income guidance trails last year.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Amazon closed 2025 with accelerating AWS and strong ad growth, but a ~$200B capex plan for 2026 means AI investment will keep squeezing free cash flow.
