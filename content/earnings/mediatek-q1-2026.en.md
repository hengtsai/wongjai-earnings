## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue in the Upper Half of Guidance</h3>
<p>Q1 revenue was NT$149.15B, in the upper half of the NT$141.2–150.2B guidance given in February, down 0.7% QoQ and 2.7% YoY. The company said the decline was mainly because <b>"lower smartphone revenue offset growth in smart edge platform revenue"</b>. Gross margin of 46.3% and an operating expense ratio of 31.0% were both within guidance.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Gross Margin Down 1.8pp YoY</h3>
<p>Gross margin was 46.3%, up 0.2pp from Q4, which the company attributed to product mix; it was 1.8pp below 48.1% a year ago, which the company said <b>"mainly reflects changes in the gross margins of some products"</b>. Gross profit was NT$69.06B, down 6.4% YoY, a bigger drop than revenue, so profit pressure is coming from pricing and mix.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: R&D Spending Weighs on Operating Margin</h3>
<p>R&D expense was NT$38.35B, up 7.2% YoY and 25.7% of revenue, 2.4pp more than last year; operating income was NT$22.89B, down 23.8% YoY, and operating margin fell from 19.6% to 15.3%. Stepping up R&D while revenue declines shows the company is prioritizing AI accelerators and new products (our inference). EPS was NT$15.17, down 17.7% YoY.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Inventory Up 27%, Cash Flow Turns Negative</h3>
<p>Inventory rose from NT$67.2B at the start of the year to NT$85.5B, up 27%, and operating cash flow was -NT$17.66B versus an inflow of NT$13.42B a year ago. Cash fell NT$50.78B and short-term borrowings rose NT$15.5B. Flat revenue alongside a big inventory build suggests the company is stocking up for upcoming products, or end demand is slowing (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Q2 Guidance Midpoint Down 3% QoQ</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue guidance is NT$140.2–149.2B (assuming NT$31.5 per US$1), with a midpoint of NT$144.7B, about 3% below Q1 and about 4% below NT$150.37B a year ago; gross margin is guided at 46% ± 1.5% and the operating expense ratio at 31% ± 2%. The guidance midpoint is below Q1, yet the stock rose from NT$2,610 to NT$2,870 on the first day after earnings.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q2 Guidance

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: Smartphone SoC Scale and Platform Diversification</h3>
<p>MediaTek is one of the world's largest smartphone chip suppliers by shipments; its Dimensity line has moved into the high end, and it is extending its technology into smart devices, automotive and AI accelerator ASICs. R&D is over 25% of revenue, and its scale lets it invest in several product lines at once. The weak spot: the smartphone market is mature, and revenue is highly sensitive to handset demand and competitive pricing.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Flat revenue**: Q1 revenue of NT$149.15B (down 2.7% YoY), in the upper half of guidance, as weaker smartphones offset smart device growth.
- **Profit down**: gross margin of 46.3% (-1.8pp), operating income down 23.8% YoY, and EPS of NT$15.17.
- **More R&D**: R&D at 25.7% of revenue, 2.4pp more than last year.
- **Higher inventory**: inventory up 27%, and operating cash flow of -NT$17.66B.
- **Cautious guidance**: the Q2 revenue guidance midpoint is down 3% QoQ, yet the stock rose 10% on day one.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: MediaTek's Q1 revenue held in the upper half of guidance, but weak smartphones and heavier R&D cut profit by about 20% YoY, and inventory and cash flow weakened; yet the stock jumped 10%, as the market seems to care more about its new product positioning than the quarter's numbers (our inference).
