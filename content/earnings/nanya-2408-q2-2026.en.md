## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue up 68% QoQ and nearly 7x YoY</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue was NT$82.55B, 68% higher than Q1's NT$49.09B and 7.8 times the NT$10.53B of a year earlier. 1H revenue of NT$131.64B is already nearly twice full-year 2025's NT$66.59B. Surging DRAM prices are the main driver (our inference; the report does not split price and volume).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Gross margin swings from -20.6% to 79.5%</h3>
<p>Gross margin swung from -20.6% a year earlier to 79.5% within one year, another 11.6pp above Q1's 67.9%, while cost of revenue rose only 33% YoY. The operating margin was 73.7% versus -42.8% a year earlier, a textbook memory cycle from trough to peak.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Quarterly EPS of NT$14.66</h3>
<p>Net income attributable to the parent was NT$50.19B and basic EPS NT$14.66, up 74% QoQ; diluted EPS was NT$14.12. 1H EPS of NT$23.38 is 11 times full-year 2025's NT$2.13. The higher share count after the Q2 capital increase dilutes per-share earnings.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: NT$78.7B capital increase, NT$203.2B net cash</h3>
<p>A NT$78.72B cash capital increase was completed in 1H; together with operating cash flow of NT$87.41B, period-end cash reached NT$217.6B with only NT$14.4B of long-term borrowings left, and short-term borrowings were fully repaid. Capex of NT$6.86B was below NT$9.33B a year earlier, so capacity expansion has not yet ramped significantly.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Inventory falls as supply stays tight</h3>
<p>Inventory fell from NT$27.29B at the start of the year to NT$21.53B, down 21%, even as revenue surged, suggesting demand exceeds supply and inventory is being drawn down quickly (our inference). Receivables rose with revenue to NT$40.25B, so collections still bear watching.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: in-house DRAM capacity in Taiwan</h3>
<p>Nanya Technology is a DRAM maker within the Formosa Plastics Group, with its own fabs and process technology. The memory industry is highly cyclical, and profits fall quickly when prices reverse; the capital increase has greatly strengthened the balance sheet, improving its ability to weather the next downturn.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Memory cycle</b>: Gross margin went from -20.6% to 79.5% in a year and could fall just as fast when the cycle turns.</li>
<li><b>Price dependence</b>: Profits come almost entirely from price increases, so any weakening in pricing would hit hard.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Equity dilution</b>: The cash capital increase raised the share count by about 11%, and diluted EPS is below basic EPS.</li>
<li><b>Receivables</b>: Receivables rose to NT$40.25B, about 2.4x the start of the year.</li>
<li><b>Expansion timing</b>: Expanding aggressively at the peak could amplify the next oversupply.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>NT$203.2B net cash</b>: Financial strength greatly improved.</li>
<li><b>Lower inventory</b>: Inventory drawn down, supply tight.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue explosion**: Q2 revenue of NT$82.55B (up 684% YoY, 68% QoQ).
- **Margin turnaround**: Gross margin rose from -20.6% a year earlier to 79.5%, with a 73.7% operating margin.
- **Record profit**: EPS of NT$14.66; 1H EPS of NT$23.38 is 11 times full-year 2025.
- **Ample funding**: NT$78.7B cash capital increase, net cash of about NT$203.2B, and short-term debt cleared.
- **Inventory drawdown**: Inventory down 21% from the start of the year, with tight supply.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Riding the DRAM price surge, Nanya swung in Q2 from losses to a peak with a gross margin near 80%, and used the moment to raise capital and strengthen its balance sheet; but profits rest almost entirely on pricing, and the market's worry about a cycle reversal is already reflected in the share price.
