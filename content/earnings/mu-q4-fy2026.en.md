## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue up 31% QoQ, again well above guidance</h3>
<p>FQ4 revenue was $54.2B, $3.2B above the top of the $49–51B guidance and up 379% YoY; non-GAAP EPS of $33.42 also beat guidance of $31.00±1.00. CEO Sanjay Mehrotra said the company delivered a record FY26 <b>and expects FY27 to be even stronger</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: 87% gross margin, all four business units highly profitable</h3>
<p>GAAP gross margin was 86.8% and operating margin 80.7%; the four units' gross margins ranged from 83% to 90%, and Mobile and Client's operating margin even reached 88%. This shows the AI-driven memory shortage has spread to phones, PCs and cars, not just data center HBM.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Strategic customer agreements bring $12.7B of prepayments</h3>
<p>There was a $12.75B inflow of customer contract deposits for the year, and non-current customer contract liabilities jumped from $570M in FQ3 to $12.9B. Mehrotra said <b>the strategic customer agreements give the company more confidence in the durability of its financial performance</b>; customers' willingness to prepay to lock in supply reflects tight supply.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Cash surges into a large net cash position</h3>
<p>FQ4 operating cash flow was $44B and adjusted free cash flow $33.2B; cash and investments rose $43.3B QoQ to $73.5B, with debt down to just $5.2B. Full-year adjusted free cash flow was $62.3B; Micron declared a quarterly dividend of $0.15 per share and spent only $650M on buybacks for the year.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: FQ1'27 revenue guidance of $61.5B, EPS of $38</h3>
<p>FY27 first-quarter guidance calls for revenue of $61.5B±$1.5B, up about 13% QoQ, a GAAP gross margin of about 86% and non-GAAP EPS of $38.15±1.00. On products, Micron was first to sample 512GB DDR5 RDIMMs, server LPDDR SOCAMM revenue more than doubled QoQ, and 1-gamma LPDDR5X is shipping in flagship phones.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Moat and FQ1'27 Guidance

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Process leadership and oligopoly supply</h3>
<p>Memory is an oligopoly of a few large makers, and Micron's 1-gamma process and HBM technology are in the top tier. AI has turned memory from a commodity into a key component that shapes platform competitiveness, and customers' willingness to sign long contracts and prepay has improved visibility in a historically highly cyclical industry.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Memory cycle</b>: An 87% gross margin is a historical extreme, and prices could reverse quickly if supply rises or demand slows.</li>
<li><b>Capacity race</b>: Peers are expanding at the same time, so supply and demand may flip after 2027.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Receivables</b>: Receivables of $36.2B are about two-thirds of quarterly revenue; customer payment terms bear watching.</li>
<li><b>Customer concentration</b>: Strategic agreements tie Micron to a few large customers, and bargaining power may shift.</li>
<li><b>Rising expenses</b>: Both R&D and SG&A rose sharply from last quarter in FQ4.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Prepayments lock in volume</b>: $12.7B of customer prepayments improve future revenue visibility.</li>
<li><b>Net cash of $68.3B</b>: The balance sheet can fund both expansion and shareholder returns.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Big beat**: FQ4 revenue of $54.2B (+31% QoQ), $3.2B above the top of guidance.
- **Extremely high margins**: GAAP gross margin of 86.8%, with all four units above 83%.
- **Breakout year**: FY26 revenue of $133.2B (+256%), net income of $85B and EPS of $74.33.
- **Customer prepayments**: Strategic customer agreements brought $12.75B of prepayments, improving visibility.
- **Strong guidance continues**: FQ1'27 revenue guidance of $61.5B and non-GAAP EPS of about $38.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Micron's FY26 Q4 revenue, gross margin and cash flow all hit records, and customer prepayments give this cycle more visibility; but an 87% gross margin is a historical extreme, the market fears peak earnings, and the stock fell more than 30% in just over a month.
