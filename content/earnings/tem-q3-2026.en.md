## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Officially "making the turn," with adjusted EBITDA hitting a record $25.6M</h3>
<p>For years the market's biggest doubt was when Tempus could turn gross profit growth into real profit. This quarter adjusted EBITDA jumped from $1.5M a year earlier to $25.6M (+1,633%), an all-time high, and non-GAAP EPS went from -$0.11 to +$0.07. CEO Eric Lefkofsky said the company had long believed that once gross profit dollars grew faster than investment, it would reach an inflection point and generate meaningful EBITDA, adding: "This quarter we made the turn." Full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance was maintained at about $65M.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Oncology volume up 32% YoY, "the strongest in years," as the flywheel speeds up</h3>
<p>Diagnostics revenue was $303M (+20%), driven mainly by oncology: revenue of $172.6M grew 28% and test volume of about 101,500 rose 32% YoY, which Lefkofsky called the strongest volume growth seen in years. Average oncology reimbursement was about $1,700 (excluding MRD, ASP rose from $1,850 last quarter to $1,880). The CEO stressed that the oncology flywheel is turning—diagnostics expand the data set and feed the foundation models, which in turn enable more sophisticated diagnostics—forming a self-reinforcing loop.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Over $550M in data licensing signed this year; the BMS deal raises the bar again</h3>
<p>Data & Apps revenue was $108.1M (+33%, with growth accelerating another 500 basis points), and the main engine, Insights data licensing, grew 29% YoY. In October the company signed an oncology data and model agreement with Bristol Myers Squibb (BMS) worth up to $137.5M over 5 years, and in a BMS + Pathos + Tempus Alzheimer's collaboration Pathos will pay $55M; together the two deals contribute about $192.5M of new revenue over 5 years. Lefkofsky said the company would have been satisfied to sign just one large collaboration this year, and it has signed three this year alone. Data licensing in hand this year already exceeds $550M, with Q4 not yet over.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Acquiring Personalis to enter MRD; the 11/30 shareholder vote decides it</h3>
<p>Tempus is acquiring Personalis (PSNL) for $16.25 per share, mainly in stock (with an option of up to 50% cash, the cash portion to be funded with debt). The deal has cleared regulatory review, Personalis will hold its shareholder meeting on 11/30, and closing is expected in December. MRD (minimal residual disease monitoring) is one of the fastest-growing areas in cancer diagnostics, with an estimated market of more than $20B. Tempus has been the exclusive distributor of Personalis's "NeXT" test since 2023; NeXT volume rose 300% YoY this quarter to about 12,000 tests, and the deal fills out Tempus's MRD portfolio.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: 2027 reimbursement tailwinds plus FDA approvals raise revenue and profit visibility</h3>
<p>Management cited several 2027 reimbursement tailwinds: FDA has expanded xT CDx to "tumor-only" testing, the full switch to the ADLT version will happen by year-end, and new pricing takes effect from 2027/1/1, which alone is estimated to add about $80M of ASP benefit; the xF liquid biopsy has been submitted to FDA and xR is preparing a PMA. This quarter the company also won two FDA approvals, ECG-PH (pulmonary hypertension) and ECG-MR (mitral regurgitation), rounding out its cardiac algorithm suite, and received an ARPA-H contract to build the first FDA-cleared patient-facing automated physician assistant.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: the world's largest multimodal clinical-genomic database × AI flywheel</h3>
<p>Tempus has accumulated more than 600 PB of multimodal data (adding over 50 PB in the quarter), is connected to about 8,000 healthcare institutions and has more than 50 million patient records. More diagnostic volume means more data and stronger models, which in turn strengthen diagnostics—a network effect competitors find hard to replicate. Pharma customers can connect their own GPU clusters to Tempus's Lens platform and train generative AI models within its environment, which is key to the acceleration in data licensing.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Valuation already prices in the inflection</b>: P/S is about 9.6x and the stock is near its 52-week high of $89; if guidance stalls, the pullback could be large.</li>
<li><b>Deal integration and financing</b>: The Personalis deal still needs approval at the 11/30 shareholder meeting, and the company plans to fund it with debt; with convertible debt already above $1.35B, leverage and integration risks coexist.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Still a GAAP loss</b>: The Q3 GAAP net loss of $14.9M includes a $42.8M securities gain; excluding it, the core loss is larger, though the trend keeps improving.</li>
<li><b>Hereditary testing growth slows</b>: Hereditary revenue grew only 3% and volume 2% as last year's high base persists; growth is expected to return to the low-to-mid teens only by year-end.</li>
<li><b>Volatile data licensing</b>: A single large deal (such as BMS) can sharply lift quarterly growth, while growth may fall back when there are no new large deals; revenue visibility depends on contract timing.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Clear reimbursement tailwinds</b>: 2027 ASP benefits from xT ADLT and the xF submission are visible, with xT alone estimated to add about $80M.</li>
<li><b>EBITDA scaling up</b>: Adjusted EBITDA and operating cash flow keep improving as operating leverage starts to pay off, shifting the valuation story from "cash-burning growth" to "profitable growth."</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **The turning point**: Adjusted EBITDA hit a record $25.6M and non-GAAP EPS turned positive at $0.07, establishing the profit inflection.
- **Oncology accelerates**: Core oncology test volume rose 32% YoY and revenue 28%, the "strongest in years."
- **Data boom**: Insights grew 29% YoY, data licensing signed this year exceeds $550M, and three big deals this year raised the bar.
- **Strategic acquisition**: Buying Personalis at $16.25 to enter the $20B MRD market, decided at the 11/30 shareholder meeting.
- **Guidance raised a third time**: Full-year revenue raised to $1.60–1.605B, up about 25% YoY, with EBITDA maintained at about $65M.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: This quarter Tempus turned a "cash-burning AI healthcare dream" into a "data flywheel that has started making money"—oncology volume is accelerating, data licensing keeps landing big deals and adjusted EBITDA hit a record. Next to watch: whether the Personalis integration and 2027 reimbursement tailwinds can turn the inflection into a trend.
