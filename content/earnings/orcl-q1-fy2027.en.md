## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue up 30% YoY, ahead of guidance</h3>
<p>Q1 revenue was $19.35B, up 30% YoY and above guidance of 27–29%; non-GAAP EPS of $1.92 beat guidance of $1.72–1.76. The press release said the growth reflects strong execution in the infrastructure business, which this quarter <b>delivered 850MW of new data center capacity</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: OCI up 121% YoY, demand still exceeds supply</h3>
<p>Cloud infrastructure revenue was $7.39B, up 121% YoY, accelerating for four straight quarters. The press release said customer demand for AI training and inference cloud services <b>continues to grow faster than supply</b>; since the end of Q4, Oracle has delivered more than 300,000 GPUs, with delivered capacity nearly triple FY26 Q4.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: RPO of $664B, more than $30B of new AI contracts</h3>
<p>Remaining performance obligations (RPO) were $664B, up $209B YoY, with more than $30B of new AI cloud contracts signed this quarter. The company stressed that, given the structure of the new contracts, there is <b>no additional impact on its funding plans</b>. Deferred revenue rose from $9.9B to $14.7B, making customer prepayments one source of capex funding.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Capex of $28.5B, funded by equity and prepayments</h3>
<p>Q1 capex was $28.5B, 3.4 times the prior-year quarter, with free cash flow of -$5.4B and -$28.7B over the last four quarters. This quarter Oracle completed a $20B at-the-market equity raise (before commissions); in June the company said it expects to raise about $40B through debt and equity in FY27 and does not expect to issue more bonds in 2026.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: FY27 revenue of at least $90B, EPS of $8.10</h3>
<p>FY27 revenue guidance changed from $90B to "at least $90B", and non-GAAP EPS was raised from $8.05 to $8.10. Q2 guidance calls for revenue growth of 30–34% YoY, cloud revenue growth of 65–71% and non-GAAP EPS of $1.85–1.93, up 21–25% YoY excluding last year's Ampere sale gain.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and FY27 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Database customer base + AI cloud scale</h3>
<p>Oracle has a huge enterprise database customer base, and OCI can put databases and AI compute in the same cloud. This quarter it launched an AI data platform that automatically generates an Enterprise Ontology, letting companies use AI to reason over private data. The massive RPO locks in revenue for years to come.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Persistently negative free cash flow</b>: -$28.7B over the last four quarters, relying on equity raises, borrowing and customer prepayments.</li>
<li><b>High leverage</b>: Total debt of $125.3B, with Q1 interest expense up 55% YoY.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Equity dilution</b>: $20B ATM raise, with diluted shares up 3% YoY.</li>
<li><b>Customer concentration</b>: RPO comes mostly from a few large AI customers, whose ability to perform and pay is key.</li>
<li><b>Legacy software decline</b>: Software licenses fell 15% YoY, and the legacy business keeps shrinking.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>RPO visibility</b>: The $664B RPO is about 9 times TTM revenue.</li>
<li><b>Faster delivery</b>: 850MW and more than 300,000 GPUs delivered this quarter, speeding the conversion of capacity into revenue.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue beat**: FY27 Q1 revenue of $19.35B (+30%), above guidance of 27–29%.
- **OCI accelerates**: Cloud infrastructure revenue up 121% YoY, accelerating for four straight quarters, now 38% of revenue.
- **Order book full**: RPO of $664B with more than $30B of new AI contracts this quarter; demand exceeds supply.
- **Burning cash to expand**: Capex of $28.5B, free cash flow of -$5.4B, and -$28.7B over the last four quarters.
- **Guidance raised**: FY27 revenue of at least $90B and non-GAAP EPS of $8.10.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Oracle's FY27 Q1 revenue and EPS both beat guidance, OCI growth topped 100%, and RPO provides years of visibility; the cost is massive capex, negative free cash flow and equity dilution, and the market still worries about funding pressure.
