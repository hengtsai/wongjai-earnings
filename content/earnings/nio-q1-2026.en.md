## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Deliveries Double Even in the Off-Season</h3>
<p>Q1 deliveries were 83,465 vehicles, up 98% YoY but down 33% from Q4 due to the Chinese New Year off-season. Founder and CEO William Li said that from Q2 the company is <b>entering an intensive cycle of new product launches and deliveries</b>; Q2 delivery guidance is 110K–115K vehicles, up 53–60% YoY. The all-new ES8 has ranked first in China for five straight months in sales among large SUVs and vehicles priced above RMB400K.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Vehicle Margin Improves for Four Straight Quarters</h3>
<p>Vehicle gross margin was 18.8%, above both last year's 10.2% and last quarter's 18.1%, mainly thanks to a more favorable product mix. CFO Stanley Yu Qu said vehicle margin has <b>risen sequentially for four consecutive quarters</b>, and other sales gross margin reached 20.6%, a four-year high, as a growing user base lifted the scale and profitability of service and community businesses. Revenue per vehicle was about RMB273K, roughly RMB37K more than last year (our estimate).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Expenses Fall Sharply, Second Straight Quarter of Non-GAAP Profit</h3>
<p>R&D expenses fell 41% YoY and SG&A 21% YoY, mainly because organizational streamlining cut personnel costs. The operating loss narrowed from RMB6.42B a year ago to RMB310M, and excluding share-based compensation, operating income was RMB66.8M, positive for a second straight quarter. Qu said the company's <b>key operating metrics continued to improve</b> and that it will further raise cost and operating efficiency.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: ES9 and L80 Carry Q2</h3>
<p>The flagship executive SUV ES9 opened pre-orders on 4/9 and officially launched with deliveries on 5/27; ONVO's flagship five-seat large SUV L80 began deliveries after launching on 5/15. William Li said the ES9 integrates several industry-first technologies, and new ONVO models will also adopt NIO's in-house smart-driving chip and NIO WorldModel. Q2 revenue guidance is RMB32.78–34.44B, up 72–81% YoY.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Balance Sheet Still Thin</h3>
<p>The cash position is RMB48.2B and net current assets turned positive; the company believes it has enough funding to support operations for the next 12 months. But shareholders' equity is only RMB4.3B, accounts and notes payable reach RMB54B, and another RMB10.2B of redeemable non-controlling interests sits in mezzanine equity. It is still loss-making on a GAAP basis, so improving the capital structure depends on accumulating sustained profits (our inference).</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q2 Guidance

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Battery-swap network + three-brand lineup</h3>
<p>NIO serves the premium market with its battery-swap network and user community, extends into mid-priced and small cars with two sub-brands, ONVO and firefly, and develops its own smart-driving chip and vehicle operating system. The weaknesses: China's EV price war is fierce, years of accumulated losses have left equity thin, and working capital depends heavily on supplier payment terms and outside funding.</p>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Deliveries doubled**: Q1 deliveries of 83,465 vehicles (+98%), with the NIO, ONVO, and firefly brands at 59K, 13K, and 12K respectively.
- **Margin improvement**: Vehicle gross margin of 18.8%, up four quarters in a row; overall gross margin of 19.0%.
- **Lower expenses**: R&D -41% and SG&A -21%; operating loss narrowed to RMB310M, and Non-GAAP operating income turned positive.
- **New-model cycle**: ES9 and ONVO L80 launched; Q2 delivery guidance of 110K–115K vehicles and revenue growth of 72–81% YoY.
- **Financial health**: Cash position of RMB48.2B and net current assets turned positive, but shareholders' equity is only RMB4.3B.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: In the off-season Q1, NIO doubled deliveries, kept lifting margins, and posted a Non-GAAP profit as cost control paid off; next, the question is whether the ES9 and L80 can push Q2 deliveries to 110K and turn profits into thicker equity.
