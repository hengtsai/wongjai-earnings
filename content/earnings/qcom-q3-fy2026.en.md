## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue at the high end of guidance; handsets down 20% YoY</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue was $9.95B, down 4% YoY, at the high end of the $9.2–10.0B guidance. CEO Cristiano Amon said: <b>"Despite a challenging memory and supply environment, our third quarter results reflect solid execution of our growth strategy, with quarterly revenues at the high end of guidance."</b> Handset chip revenue was $5.09B, down 20% YoY, the main cause of the revenue decline.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Automotive posts 23 straight quarters of double-digit growth</h3>
<p>Automotive revenue was $1.59B, up 61% YoY, the 23rd straight quarter of double-digit YoY growth; IoT was $1.83B, up 9% YoY, and automotive plus IoT grew 28% YoY combined. Non-handset businesses now account for 40% of chip revenue (our estimate), the core of Qualcomm's effort to reduce its reliance on handsets.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Rising input costs, higher product prices</h3>
<p>The press release said: <b>"The semiconductor industry is experiencing a broad-based increase in input costs, across wafer fabrication, assembly, test, advanced packaging, memory and other materials."</b> In response, the company is raising product prices to reflect costs and expects gross margin to improve as the increases take effect. GAAP gross margin fell 2.5pp YoY, and QCT's EBT margin fell from 30% to 26%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: FY27 non-handset revenue to grow over 60%</h3>
<p>Amon said: "we expect year-over-year growth in non-handset revenues, including Data Center, <b>to accelerate from 24% in fiscal 2026 to greater than 60% in fiscal 2027</b>," with an FY29 non-handset revenue target of $40B, almost double the target set in November 2024. This quarter Qualcomm completed its acquisition of Modular, building an open software foundation for generative and agentic AI.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Q4 revenue guided down about 10% YoY</h3>
<p>Q4 revenue is guided to $9.7–10.5B, with the midpoint about 10% below $11.27B a year earlier; Non-GAAP EPS to $2.05–2.25. In Q3 Qualcomm paid $973M in dividends ($0.92 per share) and repurchased 8M shares for $1.4B, for total shareholder returns of $2.3B, above the quarter's free cash flow.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q4 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: wireless patents and low-power computing</h3>
<p>Qualcomm's QTL licensing segment has a 69% EBT margin and is a steady source of cash; Snapdragon holds strong positions in premium Android phones, automotive cockpits and edge AI. The weakness is that handsets are still 60% of chip revenue, exposed to memory shortages and customers designing their own chips.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **High end of guidance**: Q3 revenue of $9.95B (-4%) at the high end of guidance, with Non-GAAP EPS of $2.21.
- **Handset drag**: Handset chips down 20% YoY amid a tough memory and supply environment.
- **Automotive shines**: Automotive up 61% YoY, 23 straight quarters of double digits; automotive plus IoT up 28% YoY.
- **Cost pressure**: Input costs up across the board; the company is raising product prices, and gross margin fell 2.5pp YoY.
- **Transformation targets**: FY27 non-handset revenue growth over 60% and an FY29 target of $40B.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Qualcomm held the high end of Q3 guidance despite the handset decline and rising costs, with automotive and IoT still growing; but Q4 guidance still implies a roughly 10% YoY decline, and the payoff of its transformation depends on whether data center and non-handset businesses deliver in FY27.
