## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: AI recommendations lift both ad volume and price</h3>
<p>Ad revenue was $59.4B, up 27.5% YoY, with impressions up 14% and average price per ad up 12%, putting revenue at the top end of the $58–61B guidance. Guidance had assumed a roughly 2% FX tailwind, yet revenue still grew 27% YoY excluding FX. Zuckerberg said that <b>AI is accelerating the core business today, powering next-generation products, and opening up new business opportunities with enterprises</b> (paraphrased). Other revenue rose 73% YoY but is only about $1B in size.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Operating margin drops 12 points, driven mainly by R&D</h3>
<p>Revenue grew by $13.3B, but costs and expenses grew by $15.0B, so operating income fell 8% YoY. R&D expenses rose 67% YoY to $21.7B, or 36% of revenue; $2.4B in legal expenses and $1.18B in severance cut another 6 points. The Family of Apps operating margin fell from 53.0% to 38.8%, and Reality Labs still lost $4.6B. The CFO said <b>the company still expects this year's operating income to exceed 2025's</b> (paraphrased), which means 2H must earn more than $41.6B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Capex of $31.1B leaves just $780M of free cash flow</h3>
<p>Q2 capex (including finance leases) was $31.1B, up 83% YoY, using up almost all of the $31.9B in operating cash flow. The company issued $24.9B of long-term debt in Q2 and has not bought back shares for two straight quarters. The full-year capex range was narrowed from $125–145B to <b>$130–145B</b>; with $50.9B spent in 1H, 2H must spend another $79.1–94.1B (our estimate). In Q1, the raise was attributed to higher component prices and data center costs for future years.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: About 8,000 laid off in May; headcount turns lower YoY</h3>
<p>Q2 recognized $1.18B in severance, and quarter-end headcount was 75,472, down 1% YoY. The press release said this figure still includes about 8,000 employees affected by the May layoffs, <b>most of whom will no longer be counted by the end of Q3</b> (paraphrased), so actual Q3 headcount will drop noticeably. Meanwhile stock-based compensation rose 58% YoY, showing the company is concentrating resources on a small pool of AI talent rather than expanding across the board.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Legal risk and tax rate rise at the same time</h3>
<p>Q2 recognized $2.4B in legal expenses, so the low end of full-year expense guidance was raised from $162B to $165B (range $165–169B). 2H tax rate guidance was raised from 13–16% to 15–17%. The CFO's written outlook noted that several youth-related trials are scheduled in the US this year, which <b>could ultimately result in material losses</b> (paraphrased), and that EU and US regulation could also significantly affect results.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q3 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: usage data from 3.6B people + AI ad delivery</h3>
<p>Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp are used by 3.6B people every day. Growing impressions and price at the same time shows that AI recommendations make ads more precise and advertisers willing to pay more. Building its own data centers and models frees Meta from renting compute from cloud providers, but at the cost of rapidly rising capex and depreciation.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Free cash flow near zero</b>: Q2 free cash flow was only $780M, 2H capex will rise further, and buybacks have been paused in favor of debt funding.</li>
<li><b>Youth litigation</b>: The company acknowledges that multiple US trials could cause material losses, and it recognized $2.4B in legal expenses in Q2.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Expenses growing faster than revenue</b>: R&D up 67% YoY and depreciation up 46% YoY have pushed the operating margin down from 43% to 31%.</li>
<li><b>Ongoing Reality Labs losses</b>: 1H lost $8.6B on revenue of only $800M, with no break-even in sight in the near term.</li>
<li><b>Rising tax rate</b>: 2H tax rate guidance raised to 15–17% will further weigh on EPS.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>

<li><b>Layoff savings</b>: About 8,000 employees leave in Q3, leaving room for lower personnel costs in 2H.</li>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Strong revenue**: $60.8B (+28%), impressions +14%, price +12%, at the top end of guidance.
- **Profit regresses**: R&D +67% and $3.6B in combined legal and severance costs cut the operating margin from 43% to 31%, with EPS down 13% YoY.
- **Cash flow bottoms out**: Capex of $31.1B left just $780M of free cash flow; $24.9B of debt issued in Q2, with no buybacks for two straight quarters.
- **Tighter guidance**: The low ends of full-year expense and capex guidance were both raised, and the 2H tax rate rises to 15–17%.
- **Legal risk surfaces**: The company acknowledges youth-related lawsuits could cause material losses.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Meta's ad engine is still accelerating, but AI investment is driving expenses, capex and debt up at the same time, leaving Q2 free cash flow near zero; the question now is whether ad growth can keep up with this wave of spending.
