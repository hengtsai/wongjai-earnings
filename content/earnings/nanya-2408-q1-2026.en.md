## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue 6.8x last year</h3>
<p>Q1 revenue was NT$49.09B, 6.8x the NT$7.19B of a year earlier and up another 63% from Q4's NT$30.09B. A single quarter's revenue already reached 74% of full-year 2025's NT$66.59B. Cost of revenue rose only 91%, so growth came mainly from price rather than volume (our inference; the report does not split price and volume), and the memory cycle is clearly heading toward a peak.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Gross margin swung from -15% to 68%</h3>
<p>Gross margin rose from -14.95% a year earlier to 67.87% within a year, up another 18.9pp from about 49.0% in Q4. Operating income was NT$30.11B with a 61.3% operating margin, versus an operating loss of NT$3.16B a year earlier. The opex ratio fell from 29% to 6.5%, as the larger revenue base sharply diluted fixed costs.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: One quarter's EPS is 4x last year's full year</h3>
<p>Net income attributable to the parent was NT$26.06B and EPS NT$8.41, nearly 4x full-year 2025's NT$2.13 and up 135% from Q4's NT$3.58. On 4/14 the company responded to media reports that "Q2 gross margin will break 70%," saying they were purely media or institutional estimates and that it has not published any financial forecast.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: NT$78.7B private placement brings in four strategic partners</h3>
<p>On 3/25 the board set the private placement price at NT$223.9 per share, 85% of the reference price, with Sandisk taking 139M shares, Kioxia 70M, Solidigm 71.39M and Cisco 71.5M — about 352M shares and NT$78.7B in total (our estimate) — to fund facilities and equipment for advanced memory manufacturing. The share count will rise by about 11%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Cash flow strengthens, short-term debt repaid</h3>
<p>Operating cash flow was NT$31.55B versus an outflow of NT$1.82B a year earlier; capex fell to NT$2.81B, leaving free cash flow of about NT$28.7B. Short-term borrowings of NT$5.06B were fully repaid, cash rose to NT$86.28B and net cash was about NT$72.5B. Inventory fell 11%, suggesting shipments were going smoothly (our inference).</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: niche DRAM and Formosa Plastics Group resources</h3>
<p>Nanya focuses on niche and standard DRAM with its own technology and capacity, backed by the Formosa Plastics Group. This private placement brings in storage and networking majors such as Sandisk, Kioxia, Solidigm and Cisco, which should help lock in long-term customers and partnerships (our inference). The weakness is the violent memory cycle: profits swing sharply with prices.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Cyclicality</b>: Gross margin rose from -15% to 68% in one year, and the fall would be just as steep in a downturn.</li>
<li><b>Price dependence</b>: Profits rely almost entirely on DRAM prices, and a price reversal would erode them quickly.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Equity dilution</b>: The private placement of 352M shares increases the share count by about 11%.</li>
<li><b>Expansion risk</b>: Placement proceeds go into capacity expansion, which could leave excess capacity if the cycle turns.</li>
<li><b>Diluted EPS</b>: Diluted EPS of NT$8.08 is below basic EPS.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Strategic partners</b>: Sandisk, Kioxia and others take stakes, strengthening customer relationships.</li>
<li><b>Strong balance sheet</b>: Net cash of about NT$72.5B, with another NT$78.7B once placement proceeds arrive.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue explosion**: Q1 revenue of NT$49.09B was 6.8x a year earlier and up 63% QoQ.
- **Margin rebound**: Gross margin was 67.9% versus -15.0% a year earlier; operating margin was 61.3%.
- **EPS of NT$8.41**: One quarter's EPS is already nearly 4x full-year 2025.
- **Strategic placement**: A private placement of about 352M shares at NT$223.9, or NT$78.7B, brings in four global majors.
- **Cash strengthens**: Operating cash flow of NT$31.55B, short-term debt repaid, net cash about NT$72.5B.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Nanya rode the DRAM price surge in Q1, with revenue and profit exploding together, and used a private placement to bring in global strategic partners for expansion; but this is a classic memory-cycle peak, and what comes next depends on how long prices hold.
