## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue +83%, Organic Growth 33.5%</h3>
<p>Q4 revenue rose 83% YoY to $367M, representing 33.5% organic growth excluding acquired Ambry. Founder and CEO Eric Lefkofsky remarked that <b>in 2025 Tempus continued to set the bar for technology companies in healthcare</b>, adding that diagnostic volume gains alongside an accelerating data business <b>demonstrated the company's unique position in the space</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Diagnostics Surges on Volume and Mix, MRD +56% QoQ</h3>
<p>Diagnostics revenue jumped 122% YoY to $267M, with oncology test volume up 29% YoY and hereditary volume up 23% YoY; MRD testing reached ~4,700 tests, up 56% QoQ. Full-year Diagnostics revenue rose 112% YoY to $955M, including Ambry. Q4 gross margin improved from 60.8% to 64.7%, led by Diagnostics.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Data Business Accelerates, Remaining Contract Value Tops $1.1B</h3>
<p>Data and Services revenue grew 25% YoY to $100M, with Insights data licensing up 69.5% excluding the AstraZeneca warrant impact; full-year Data and Services revenue grew 31% YoY, accelerating from the prior year. Year-end remaining contract value exceeded $1.1B with 126% net revenue retention, showing continued expansion by biopharma customers.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Adjusted EBITDA Positive for Two Straight Quarters</h3>
<p>Q4 adjusted EBITDA reached $12.9M, improving sharply from $1.5M in Q3 and -$7.8M in Q4 2024; full-year adjusted EBITDA was -$7.4M, a $97.3M improvement even after integrating Paige AI and OneOme. Still, GAAP net loss was $54.2M (including $48.7M in stock-based comp) and full-year OCF remained -$218M.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: 2026 Guidance Calls for $1.59B Revenue and ~$65M EBITDA</h3>
<p>Management guided 2026 revenue to ~$1.59B (~25% YoY) and adjusted EBITDA to ~$65M. Lefkofsky noted that compounding network effects from AI investments should <b>drive significant growth</b> over the coming years. Newly launched Paige Predict predicts 123 biomarkers across 16 cancer types from routine pathology slides.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Moat and 2026 Guidance Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: Diagnostic Data and AI Flywheel</h3>
<p>Tempus accumulates vast clinical and genomic datasets through diagnostic testing and licenses them to biopharma for R&D—the higher the test volume, the more valuable the data, forming a flywheel. Ambry hereditary testing, Paige digital pathology, and OneOme pharmacogenomics expand data inputs, while 126% NRR reflects strong biopharma stickiness.</p>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Rapid growth**: Q4 revenue $367M (+83%, +33.5% organic); full year $1.27B (+83%).
- **Diagnostics volume surge**: Diagnostics revenue up 122% YoY; MRD tests up 56% QoQ.
- **Data acceleration**: Insights licensing up 69.5%, remaining contract value >$1.1B, NRR 126%.
- **EBITDA turns positive**: Q4 adjusted EBITDA $12.9M; 2026 guidance ~$65M.
- **Still burning cash**: Full-year net loss $245M, operating cash flow -$218M, convertible notes $728M.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: With 83% revenue growth and positive adjusted EBITDA, Tempus shows its diagnostics-plus-data flywheel is spinning, yet 2026 growth moderating to ~25% alongside negative cash flow leaves valuation heavily reliant on sustained acceleration in data licensing.
