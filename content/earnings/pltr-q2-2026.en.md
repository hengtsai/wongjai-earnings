## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue up 93% YoY, far above its own guidance</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue was $1.94B, up 93% YoY and 19% QoQ, about $135M above the $1.797–1.801B guidance; adjusted operating income of $1.19B was also about $130M above guidance. CEO Alex Karp described the quarter as <b>beyond anything imagined</b>, and the Rule of 40 score rose to 155%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: U.S. commercial up 149% YoY</h3>
<p>U.S. commercial revenue was $764M, up 149% YoY and 28% QoQ; U.S. commercial TCV was $2.13B, up 153% YoY, and remaining deal value was $6.24B, up 124% YoY. Karp said <b>demand for AI sovereignty has been unleashed</b>, with customers wanting maximum control over their operations, data and decisions.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: U.S. government accelerates to 90% too</h3>
<p>U.S. government revenue was $809M, up 90% YoY and 18% QoQ, still slightly above U.S. commercial. Total U.S. revenue was $1.57B, 81% of the total. The company closed 73 deals of $10M or more and 98 deals of $5M or more this quarter, with total contract value of $3.37B, up 49% YoY.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Margins rise again, 63% cash flow margin</h3>
<p>GAAP operating margin was 47%, versus 27% a year ago; adjusted operating margin was 62%. Gross margin was 84.7%. Q2 operating cash flow was $1.22B and adjusted free cash flow $1.22B, a 63% margin. Stock-based compensation was $265M, 14% of revenue, down from 16% a year ago (our estimate).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Full-year guidance raised again</h3>
<p>Full-year revenue guidance was raised from $7.65–7.66B to $8.15–8.16B, up about 82% YoY; U.S. commercial revenue is guided to at least $3.42B, up at least 134%; adjusted operating income to $4.89B and adjusted free cash flow to $4.5–4.7B. Q3 revenue is guided to $2.16B, and the company expects GAAP profitability in every quarter of the year.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q3 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: AIP and the Ontology platform</h3>
<p>Palantir uses its Ontology to model enterprise data and processes, letting AI models make decisions inside customers' private environments. Karp stressed that customers' competitive advantages <b>should not become training data for future models</b>. Long-standing government and defense relationships plus fast commercial expansion form two growth engines.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Big beat**: Q2 revenue of $1.94B (+93%), about $135M above guidance.
- **Two U.S. engines**: U.S. commercial +149% and U.S. government +90%; the U.S. is 81% of revenue.
- **Profits soar**: GAAP operating margin of 47%, net margin of 55%, and adjusted free cash flow margin of 63%.
- **Guidance raised again**: Full-year revenue of $8.15–8.16B (about +82%), U.S. commercial at least +134%.
- **Very high valuation**: TTM P/E of about 140x; the stock jumped 29.5% the next day.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Palantir's Q2 revenue, profit and cash flow all exploded, U.S. commercial and government both accelerated, and full-year guidance was raised sharply; the growth is truly remarkable, but a P/E above 100x means the stock has already priced in years of growth.
