## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Adjusted EBITDA Turns Positive</h3>
<p>Adjusted EBITDA reached $1.5M vs. -$21.8M a year ago, turning positive for the first time. Founder and CEO Eric Lefkofsky called it <b>an important milestone reflecting the strength of the core business</b>, adding that slowing reinvestment while sustaining growth <b>demonstrates the durability of the business model</b>. Still, GAAP operating loss remained $61M.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Test Volume Accelerates</h3>
<p>Clinical testing reached 217K tests in Q3 (+33% YoY), with oncology volume accelerating to ~27% growth ($140M revenue, +32% YoY) and hereditary volume up ~37% ($103M revenue). Gross margin expanded from 58.5% to 62.8%, likely reflecting fixed-cost leverage and lower cost per test at scale (our assessment).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Data Licensing Keeps Growing</h3>
<p>Data & Services revenue rose 26% YoY to $81.3M, led by Insights data licensing (+37.6% YoY) with $150M in new contracts signed in Q3. Licensing multimodal clinical data to pharma provides high-margin revenue distinct from pure testing peers. Tempus was also selected by ARPA-H as a testing and CRO provider for the ADAPT program.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Paige Deal Expands Digital Pathology</h3>
<p>Tempus acquired digital pathology AI firm Paige, expanding its datasets and engineering team, while earning FDA clearances for its xR RNA NGS IVD assay, Tempus Pixel cardiac imaging, and ECG low-ejection-fraction software. The trade-off is an estimated ~$5M quarterly loss added by Paige, pushing goodwill and intangibles to $840M.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Revenue Guidance Raised, EBITDA Trimmed</h3>
<p>Full-year revenue guidance was raised from ~$1.26B to ~$1.265B (~80% YoY). However, due to Paige, Q4 adjusted EBITDA is guided at ~$20M, leaving full-year adjusted EBITDA only slightly positive—below August's ~$5M target (our assessment). Guidance implies ~$361M in Q4 revenue, up ~8% QoQ (our calculation).</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Moat & Guidance

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: Diagnostics + Data Flywheel</h3>
<p>Tempus builds clinical and molecular datasets through oncology and hereditary testing, then licenses that data to pharma and develops AI diagnostics—creating a testing-to-data flywheel. Weaknesses include ongoing GAAP losses, $840M in goodwill and intangibles from M&A, and >$1.2B in total debt exceeding cash on hand.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Strong revenue growth**: Q3 revenue of $334M (+85%), genomics +117%, data & services +26%.
- **Profitability milestone**: First positive adjusted EBITDA at $1.5M; gross margin rose to 62.8%.
- **Volume acceleration**: 217K tests (+33%), with oncology volume accelerating to ~27% growth.
- **M&A expansion**: Paige acquisition enters digital pathology, adding ~$5M in quarterly loss.
- **Guidance update**: Full-year revenue raised to ~$1.265B; full-year EBITDA lowered to slightly positive.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Tempus posted rapid Q3 revenue growth and its first positive adjusted EBITDA as the diagnostics-and-data flywheel gained traction; however, GAAP losses remain and M&A has elevated debt and goodwill, making Q4's ~$20M EBITDA target the key test of sustained profitability.
