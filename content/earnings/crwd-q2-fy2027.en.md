## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Best quarter ever, net new ARR up 51% YoY</h3>
<p>Founder and CEO George Kurtz said <b>Q2 was the best quarter in CrowdStrike's history</b>, with net new ARR of $333M, up 51% YoY, and record net new ARR from new customers. Ending ARR was $5.84B, up 25% YoY and above the top of guidance.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: AI drives security demand</h3>
<p>Kurtz said the "Mythos moment" has led the market to broadly accept that <b>AI adoption requires security</b>, and that securing AI is the biggest market opportunity in the company's history. This quarter CrowdStrike launched continuous identity verification for AI agents, extended AI detection and response to AI gateways such as Databricks, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure, and partnered with Cerebras.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Falcon Flex up 101% YoY, module adoption rising</h3>
<p>Ending ARR from Falcon Flex customers exceeded $2.29B, up 101% YoY, and growth is accelerating. Among subscription customers, 51% use 6 or more modules and 26% use 8 or more. Flex lets customers try before they buy and is the main tool lifting net new ARR.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Profit and cash flow hit Q2 records</h3>
<p>Non-GAAP operating income was $372M, a 25.3% operating margin versus 21.8% a year ago; non-GAAP EPS was $0.31, up 35% YoY. Operating cash flow of $530M and free cash flow of $377M were both Q2 records. GAAP net income was $5.3M, swinging to a profit.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Full-year guidance raised, net new ARR growth of 34%</h3>
<p>Full-year net new ARR growth guidance was raised 630 basis points to 34% (midpoint); FY27 guidance calls for ARR of $6.60–6.61B, revenue of $5.99–6.01B and non-GAAP EPS of $1.25–1.26. Podbere said guidance was raised on the strong Q2 and <b>a record Q3 pipeline</b>.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q3 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Single agent and platform approach</h3>
<p>CrowdStrike uses a single lightweight agent plus a cloud platform to deliver modules for endpoint, cloud, identity, SIEM and more, so customers can add modules with almost no redeployment. More than half of subscription customers already use 6 or more modules, making switching costs high; AI security is a new upsell opportunity.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue beat**: Q2 revenue of $1.47B (+26%) beat guidance of $1.436–1.442B.
- **ARR accelerates**: Record net new ARR of $333M, up 51% YoY, with ending ARR of $5.84B.
- **Flex takes off**: Falcon Flex customer ARR exceeded $2.29B, up 101% YoY.
- **Profitability improves**: Non-GAAP operating margin of 25.3% and EPS of $0.31; GAAP turned profitable.
- **Guidance raised**: Full-year net new ARR growth raised to 34%, with revenue of $5.99–6.01B.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: CrowdStrike's FY27 Q2 net new ARR hit a record and accelerated, AI security demand and Falcon Flex are driving platform expansion, and guidance was raised across the board; but at nearly 180x P/E, the stock already prices in high expectations.
