## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: RPO Jumps $68B QoQ to $523B</h3>
<p>Principal financial officer Doug Kehring noted that <b>RPO rose by $68B, or 15% sequentially, in Q2 to $523B, driven by new commitments from Meta, NVIDIA, and others</b>. RPO surged 438% YoY to a new record above last quarter's $455B, fulfilling management's prior-quarter view that RPO could exceed $500B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: OCI Grows 68% YoY</h3>
<p>Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) revenue rose 68% YoY and 22% QoQ to $4.08B, while total Cloud revenue climbed 34% YoY to $7.98B, now half of total revenue. CEO Clay Magouyrk said multicloud database is Oracle's fastest-growing business, jumping 817% in Q2, with more than half of 72 multicloud data centers completed and over 211 cloud regions live or planned worldwide.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Ampere Stake Sold in Shift to Chip Neutrality</h3>
<p>The sharp EPS jump came largely from a $2.7B pre-tax gain on the sale of Oracle's stake in Ampere. Chairman Larry Ellison said Oracle no longer views designing and manufacturing its own chips as strategic, shifting to a <b>chip-neutrality</b> policy where it will keep buying NVIDIA's latest GPUs while remaining ready to deploy whatever chips customers want. Excluding that gain, Non-GAAP operating income grew just 10%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: $12B Capex and $108.1B Total Debt</h3>
<p>Q2 capex jumped 42% QoQ to $12.03B against operating cash flow of just $2.07B, leaving quarterly free cash flow at about -$9.97B (our estimate). Total debt rose $15.5B in six months to $108.1B, and interest expense climbed 22% YoY. TTM free cash flow widened to -$13.18B, more than double last quarter's -$5.88B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Stock Drops 11% After Earnings</h3>
<p>CEO Mike Sicilia noted that AI can be embedded across all three layers of Oracle's stack—data centers, databases, and applications—and that the top five AI models run on Oracle Cloud. Still, the stock fell 10.8% on the first trading day after earnings, from $223.01 to $198.85, likely reflecting market concerns over heavy capex and rising debt (our inference).</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **RPO expands further**: Reached $523B (+$68B QoQ), with new commitments from Meta, NVIDIA, and others.
- **OCI accelerates**: Cloud infrastructure reached $4.08B, up 68% YoY.
- **One-time gain**: Selling Ampere brought a $2.7B pre-tax gain, lifting EPS sharply.
- **Capex surge**: Q2 capex hit $12B, pushing TTM free cash flow to -$13.18B.
- **Rising debt**: Total debt increased $15.5B in six months to $108.1B.

> 💡 **One-line takeaway**: Oracle's OCI and RPO kept surging in FY26 Q2, but EPS growth relied on the Ampere sale gain while $12B in quarterly capex deepened free cash flow and debt pressures, prompting a 10.8% first-day selloff.
