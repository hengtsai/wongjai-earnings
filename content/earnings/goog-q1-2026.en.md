## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Cloud tops $20B for the first time, backlog nearly doubles</h3>
<p>Google Cloud revenue was $20B, up 63% YoY, operating margin rose from 17.8% to 32.9%, AI solutions became the biggest growth driver for the first time, and revenue tied to its own generative AI models grew nearly 800% YoY. Backlog nearly doubled QoQ to $462B, including TPU hardware contracts for the first time, with over 50% expected to be recognized within 24 months. CEO Sundar Pichai said <b>Google is compute-constrained in the near term, and Cloud revenue would have been higher if it could meet demand</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: TPUs sold directly into customer data centers</h3>
<p>Google announced it will deliver TPUs directly to select customers for installation in their own data centers, expanding its addressable market. The new TPU 8t targets training and the 8i targets inference, with the latter offering 80% better performance per dollar than the previous generation. CFO Anat Ashkenazi said these contracts will generate only a small amount of revenue this year, with the vast majority in 2027, and that <b>TPU hardware sales revenue will fluctuate quarter to quarter depending on delivery timing</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Capex up 107% YoY, with another big rise in 2027</h3>
<p>Q1 capex was $35.7B, and full-year guidance was raised from $175–185B to $180–190B, with the increase coming from Intersect, the renewable energy developer acquired in March; the company also signaled a significant capex increase in 2027. Ashkenazi said <b>the sharp increase in technical infrastructure investment will keep pressuring the P&L through higher depreciation and operating costs such as energy</b>. Free cash flow fell 47% YoY, and $31.1B of debt was issued during the quarter.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: AI drives record search queries</h3>
<p>Search and other revenue grew 19% YoY; Pichai said users love AI Mode and AI Overviews, query volume hit an all-time high, and retail and financial advertisers contributed most. Paid subscriptions reached 350M, and this was the strongest quarter yet for consumer AI plans, driven mainly by the Gemini App. Its own models now process over 16B tokens per minute via API, up from 10B last quarter.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Wiz consolidated, EPS includes $2.35 valuation gain</h3>
<p>The Wiz acquisition closed in March and was folded into the Cloud segment, adding $24.4B of goodwill; the company expects it to be a low-single-digit percentage-point headwind to Cloud operating margin for the rest of 2026. Other income was $37.7B, mainly unrealized valuation gains on private equity investments, adding $2.35 to EPS; excluding it, EPS was about $2.76. Verily is no longer consolidated after closing outside funding, and the quarterly dividend was raised 5% to $0.22.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Moat and Q2 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: The only cloud that owns the full enterprise AI stack</h3>
<p>Pichai stressed that Google Cloud is the only cloud provider with its own offering at every layer of the enterprise AI stack: TPUs and Axion CPUs, Gemini models, the BigQuery data platform, the Gemini Enterprise agent platform, plus Wiz security. Over the past 12 months, 330 Cloud customers each processed over 1T tokens, and existing customers' usage exceeded their original commitments by 45%.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Cloud accelerating**: Revenue $20B (+63%), operating margin 32.9%, with AI solutions the biggest growth driver.
- **Orders surging**: Backlog $462B, nearly doubled QoQ, including TPU hardware contracts for the first time.
- **Solid core business**: Revenue $109.9B (+22%), operating income +30%, Search +19%.
- **Valuation in EPS**: $2.35 of the $5.11 comes from equity valuation gains.
- **Stepping up investment**: Capex up 107% YoY, $180–190B for the full year, with another big rise in 2027.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Driven by AI demand, Google Cloud is delivering both high growth and high margins, and backlog nearly doubled; the price is doubled capex and halved free cash flow, so the question now is whether compute can come online fast enough to keep up with orders.
