## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue up 33% YoY, currency adds 4 percentage points</h3>
<p>Ad impressions rose 19% and price per ad 12%, taking ad revenue to $55B, up 33% YoY, or 29% excluding currency. Zuckerberg called it <b>a milestone quarter, with strong momentum across the apps and Meta Superintelligence Labs shipping its first model</b>, and said <b>Meta is making progress toward bringing personal superintelligence to billions of people</b>. Q2 guidance is $58–61B, assuming a currency tailwind of about 2%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: EPS of $10.44, of which $3.13 is a tax benefit</h3>
<p>The quarter booked an $8.03B income tax benefit, making the effective tax rate -23%; excluding it, EPS was $7.31, up about 14% YoY, far below the reported 62%. Operating income rose 30% YoY, but other income swung from +$830M a year ago to -$1.12B, mainly $1.08B of unrealized losses on equity investments. For this quarter's profitability, operating income is a better guide than EPS.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Capex guidance raised by $10B on component price increases</h3>
<p>Full-year capex guidance was raised from $115–135B to $125–145B, 73–101% above 2025's $72.2B. The CFO's written outlook said this <b>reflects expected higher component prices this year</b>, and secondarily more data centers for future-year capacity. Q1 spending was only $19.8B, implying an average of $35.1–41.7B per quarter for the remaining three quarters (our estimate). Full-year total expense guidance of $162–169B was unchanged.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Free cash flow of $12.4B, but buybacks paused</h3>
<p>Operating cash flow was $32.2B, up 34% YoY, and free cash flow after capex was $12.4B, up 20%. But the company bought back no stock at all this quarter, versus $12.75B a year ago; the $1.35B dividend was paid as usual and surplus cash went into marketable securities. Long-term debt did not increase, and net cash is about $22.4B. With capex accelerating, the company chose to hold on to cash first.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Daily active people dip QoQ, Reality Labs loss narrows slightly</h3>
<p>Average daily active people in March were 3.56B, up 4% YoY but slightly below last quarter; the press release attributed <b>the sequential decline to internet disruptions in Iran and Russia's restrictions on WhatsApp</b>. Reality Labs revenue was $400M, down 2% YoY, with an operating loss of $4.03B, slightly smaller than last year's $4.21B. The CFO also warned that several youth-related trials in the U.S. this year <b>could ultimately result in material losses</b>.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q2 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Usage data from 3.5B people + AI ad targeting</h3>
<p>Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp are used by 3.56B people every day, and growth in both impressions and price per ad shows AI recommendations are making ads more precise and advertisers are willing to pay more. Owning its data centers and models gives Meta control of compute, but at the cost of rapidly rising capex, depreciation and stock-based compensation.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Accelerating capex</b>: Full-year guidance raised to $125–145B, averaging $35.1–41.7B per quarter over the remaining three quarters, which will shrink free cash flow noticeably.</li>
<li><b>Youth litigation</b>: The company itself acknowledges several U.S. trials could cause material losses, and U.S. and European regulation could also affect results.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>One-time item in EPS</b>: The $8.03B tax benefit lifted EPS 62% YoY; excluding it, growth was only about 14%.</li>
<li><b>Fast expense growth</b>: R&D up 46% YoY and depreciation up 54%, and operating margin is already 0.9pp below last year.</li>
<li><b>Users down QoQ</b>: Iran and Russia caused daily active people to dip QoQ; geopolitical risk directly affects users.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Strong ad momentum</b>: Impressions +19%, price per ad +12%, and Q2 guidance still implies 22–28% growth.</li>
<li><b>Financial flexibility</b>: Net cash of about $22.4B with no increase in long-term debt; pausing buybacks preserves room to invest.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue accelerating**: $56.3B (+33%), impressions +19%, price per ad +12%, still +29% excluding currency.
- **One-time item in EPS**: $10.44 includes a $3.13 tax benefit; excluding it, $7.31, up about 14% YoY.
- **Capex raised**: Full-year guidance up to $125–145B, mainly on component price increases, averaging over $35B per quarter for the remaining three quarters.
- **Holding cash first**: Free cash flow of $12.4B (+20%), but no buybacks this quarter.
- **Users down QoQ**: 3.56B daily active people; Iran's internet shutdown and Russia's WhatsApp restrictions caused the sequential dip.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Meta's ad engine delivered 33% growth in Q1, but about 30% of the eye-catching EPS came from a tax benefit, and full-year capex was raised again; the market cares that spending keeps speeding up, not how much was earned this quarter.
