## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Strongest Bookings Quarter Ever, Backlog Near $100B</h3>
<p>Backlog rose from $66.8B to $99.4B, up $32.6B in one quarter. Co-founder and CEO Michael Intrator said: <b>"This was the strongest bookings quarter in CoreWeave's history, with revenue backlog reaching nearly $100 billion."</b> It came mainly from Meta's new $21B commitment signed in March and a multi-year contract with Anthropic, while customers such as Cohere and Mistral also expanded.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: The Shift From Training to Inference Is an Opportunity</h3>
<p>Intrator said: "AI natives and enterprise customers are choosing CoreWeave because <b>we sit between the models and the silicon</b>, delivering the infrastructure, software, and expertise required to build and run AI at scale. <b>As the market moves from training to inference, that distinction matters more than ever.</b>" This quarter it launched dedicated inference services and flexible capacity offerings, and was among the first named by NVIDIA as a GB200 NVL72 inference reference cloud.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Revenue Doubles, Margins Squeezed by Depreciation and Interest</h3>
<p>Revenue rose 112% YoY, but depreciation and amortization rose from $440M to $1.15B, the operating loss widened to $144M, and adjusted operating margin fell from 17% to 1%. Interest expense was $536M, double last year, and the GAAP net loss was $740M, a 36% net loss margin. Depreciation on new capacity is recognized from the start, while revenue catches up only once customers fully ramp usage (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: $7.7B of Capex, Funded by Debt and NVIDIA</h3>
<p>Quarterly capex was $7.7B, 5.5 times last year, and free cash flow was -$4.7B. Funding sources include the $8.5B DDTL 4.0 non-recourse loan (floating at SOFR + 2.25%, or about 5.9% fixed) and NVIDIA's $2B equity investment. Intrator said the company is well on its way to more than 8 GW by 2030, <b>"having positioned our capital structure to scale with the opportunity ahead."</b></p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Power Heading Toward 8 GW</h3>
<p>Active power topped 1 GW, and contracted power rose by more than 400 MW QoQ to over 3.5 GW, with a more diversified set of power suppliers. Intrator said the company is heading toward more than 8 GW by 2030, and an expanded partnership with NVIDIA will accelerate the buildout of over 5 GW of AI factories by 2030. Going from 1 GW to 8 GW requires enormous capex, so financing capacity is key (our inference).</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: AI-native cloud and close ties with NVIDIA</h3>
<p>CoreWeave built its cloud specifically for AI, is often the first to bring new NVIDIA platforms online, and locks in AI labs and big tech companies such as Meta and Anthropic with long-term contracts; a backlog near $100B provides high visibility. The weakness is its capital structure: expansion relies almost entirely on debt and leases, and interest and depreciation will weigh on GAAP profit for a long time.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue doubles**: Q1 revenue of $2.08B (+112%, up 32% QoQ), adjusted EBITDA margin of 56%.
- **Orders soar**: Backlog rose from $66.8B to $99.4B, with a new $21B from Meta and a multi-year Anthropic contract.
- **Profit under pressure**: Depreciation of $1.15B and interest of $540M; GAAP net loss of $740M, adjusted operating margin down to 1%.
- **Capex**: $7.7B in the quarter and free cash flow of -$4.7B, funded by new debt and NVIDIA's $2B stake.
- **Power expansion**: Over 1 GW active and over 3.5 GW contracted, targeting over 8 GW by 2030.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: CoreWeave's Q1 brought record bookings and a backlog approaching $100B, leaving nothing to fault on demand; but $7.7B of quarterly capex and doubled interest expense make profitability and financing capacity the key variables for the stock.
