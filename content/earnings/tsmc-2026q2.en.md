## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Gross margin of 67.7%, up for four straight quarters</h3>
<p>Gross margin climbed from 58.6% in 2025 Q2 to 67.7%, rising for four straight quarters and beating the top of guidance by another 0.2pp; the operating margin of 60.3% was 1.8pp above the top end. The quarter's average exchange rate of 31.60 was slightly stronger than assumed, so the margin beat came from operations. The operating expense ratio fell to 7.8%. But Q3 gross margin guidance is 65–67%, signaling the company expects a pullback.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: 2nm contributes 3% for the first time, 3nm rises to 30%</h3>
<p>2nm appeared in the revenue mix for the first time at 3% of wafer revenue; 3nm rose from 25% to 30%, and 7nm and below totaled 77%. CFO Wendell Huang said third-quarter business will be supported by strong demand for leading-edge technologies, <b>including the steep ramp of 2nm</b> (paraphrased). New nodes usually dilute gross margin early on, which may be one reason Q3 gross margin guidance is below Q2 (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: HPC up 20% QoQ for two straight quarters, now 66%</h3>
<p>HPC revenue rose 20% QoQ, lifting its share from 61% to 66%; smartphone fell QoQ for two straight quarters, dropping to 22%. HPC is now two-thirds of revenue, with AI accelerators and data center chips the main growth drivers (our inference). Automotive grew 15% QoQ, the fastest among non-HPC platforms.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: NT$95.8B in non-operating income; EPS includes a one-off component</h3>
<p>Non-operating income jumped from NT$28.8B in Q1 to NT$95.8B, up about NT$67.0B QoQ; pre-tax income rose 25% QoQ, faster than operating income's 16%, and EPS growth of 77% YoY also exceeded operating income's 65%. The press release gave no reason, but the timing matches the 5/15 announcement of the sale of an 8.1% stake in Vanguard International Semiconductor (VIS) (our inference). For core business growth, operating income is a better gauge than EPS.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Full-year outlook raised as receivables and inventory climb</h3>
<p>The full-year USD revenue outlook was raised from "more than 30%" to "slightly above 40%", and Q3 guidance calls for USD revenue of $44.6–45.8B, up 11–14% QoQ, assuming an exchange rate of 32. On the other hand, receivables rose 21% QoQ with DSO up to 29 days, and inventory rose 24% QoQ with DIO up to 87 days, outpacing revenue for two straight quarters; the question is whether this turns into shipments in Q3.</p>
</div>

Annualized ROE of 45.9% and a 60% operating margin show high profitability even amid major capacity expansion.</p>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Another beat**: USD revenue of $40.20B hit the top of guidance, and the gross margin of 67.7% and operating margin of 60.3% both exceeded the upper bound.
- **2nm takes off**: 2nm contributed 3% for the first time and 3nm rose to 30%, with advanced nodes totaling 77%.
- **HPC dominates**: HPC up 20% QoQ to 66%, while smartphone fell QoQ for two straight quarters.
- **EPS includes one-offs**: Non-operating income of NT$95.8B, up about NT$67.0B QoQ, pushed EPS growth to 77% YoY, above operating income's 65%.
- **Outlook up, margin to dip**: Full-year growth slightly above 40%, but Q3 gross margin guidance falls back to 65–67%.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: TSMC's core business hit new highs in Q2, 2nm officially took off and the full-year outlook was raised a second time; but lower Q3 gross margin guidance, non-operating income inflating EPS, and receivables and inventory outpacing revenue for two straight quarters form the backdrop to the stock's 7% drop after earnings.
