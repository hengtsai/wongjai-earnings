## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Record $12.3B revenue, EPS hits top of guidance</h3>
<p>CEO Cristiano Amon said Qualcomm <b>delivered strong quarterly results with record total revenue</b>. Revenue rose 5% YoY to $12.25B, Non-GAAP EPS of $3.50 hit the top end of guidance, and GAAP EPS of $2.78 came in above guidance. QCT revenue also hit a record $10.61B, while QTL licensing pre-tax margin rose to 77%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Memory shortage weighs on Q2 handset demand</h3>
<p>Amon said the <b>near-term handsets outlook is being impacted by industry-wide memory supply constraints</b>, though consumer demand for premium and high-tier handsets remains encouraging. Q2 revenue guidance of $10.2B–$11.0B implies about a 3% YoY drop at the midpoint (QCT $8.8B–$9.4B, QTL $1.2B–$1.4B), explicitly factoring in memory constraints and related price increases at several handset customers.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Automotive tops $1B for second straight quarter</h3>
<p>QCT automotive revenue hit a record $1.10B (+15% YoY), marking a second straight quarter above $1B, while IoT grew 9% YoY to $1.69B. Non-handset businesses totaled $2.79B, or 26% of QCT. Amon noted growing momentum in personal, industrial, and physical AI, with CES product launches gaining customer traction and FY2029 revenue targets remaining on track.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Alphawave acquisition closes, expanding into data centers</h3>
<p>Qualcomm closed its acquisition of Alphawave Semi during the quarter, which the release said will accelerate expansion into data centers; goodwill rose $2.8B QoQ alongside $1.09B in net acquisition and investing cash outflows. Alphawave sits in non-reportable data center operations and is included in Q2 guidance. SG&A rose 20% YoY and R&D rose 10%, pulling operating margin down from 30.5% to 27.5%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: $3.6B returned to shareholders, share count down 4% YoY</h3>
<p>Q1 capital returns totaled $3.6B: $949M in dividends ($0.89 per share) and $2.6B in buybacks of 15M shares. Operating cash flow rose 8% YoY to $4.97B, with free cash flow around $4.4B. Diluted shares dropped from 1.122B to 1.079B (-3.8% YoY), a key driver of modest Non-GAAP EPS growth.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Memory shortage</b>: Tight industry memory supply and higher prices are curbing handset customer shipments, pulling Q2 revenue guidance down ~3% YoY.</li>
<li><b>Handset reliance</b>: Handsets still make up 74% of QCT revenue and grew only 3%.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Margin compression</b>: Gross margin slipped 1.2pp YoY and operating margin dropped from 30.5% to 27.5%.</li>
<li><b>M&A integration</b>: Alphawave added $2.8B in goodwill while data center expansion remains early-stage.</li>
<li><b>Expense growth</b>: SG&A grew 20% YoY and R&D 10%, outpacing revenue.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Record revenue**: Revenue reached $12.25B (+5%), with Non-GAAP EPS of $3.50 at the top of guidance.
- **Strong automotive**: Automotive reached $1.10B (+15%), above $1B for two straight quarters, while IoT rose 9% YoY.
- **Memory headwind**: Industry memory shortages weigh on handset customers, putting Q2 guidance down ~3% YoY.
- **Data center push**: Closed Alphawave acquisition, adding $2.8B to goodwill.
- **Steady returns**: $3.6B returned in Q1, share count down 3.8% YoY, ~2.6% dividend yield.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Qualcomm delivered record revenue and automotive results, but memory shortages prompted handset customers to pull back, turning Q2 guidance negative YoY and sending shares down 8.5% on day one.
