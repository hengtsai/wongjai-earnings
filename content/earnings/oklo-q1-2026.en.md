## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: $1.2B Raised via ATM, $2.5B in Cash</h3>
<p>This quarter Oklo completed its $1.5B ATM program while the stock was high, selling 12.38M shares at an average of about $97 for net proceeds of $1.18B. Cash and marketable securities rose to $2.54B, with no financial debt. The presentation said the funds will strengthen the balance sheet and support deployment and long-term growth; but the share count rose about 8% in one quarter, diluting existing shareholders.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Groves Test Reactor Built in 229 Days</h3>
<p>The Groves isotope test reactor in Texas was built in 229 days and received a certificate of substantial completion; Oklo has submitted the documented safety analysis to DOE, and next comes reactor equipment installation, integrated testing, and fuel delivery, targeting criticality on July 4, 2026. It is Oklo's first operating nuclear facility and the starting point of its isotope business.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Meta's Ohio Campus Files for Grid Interconnection</h3>
<p>Oklo and Meta plan to develop a 1.2 GW advanced nuclear campus in Pike County, Ohio; in January they signed a prepayment agreement under which Meta prepays for power so Oklo can secure nuclear fuel first. The company filed its PJM interconnection application on 4/27. PJM projects a possible 50–60 GW capacity shortfall over the next decade, which the presentation sees as favoring campus-style deployment.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Regulatory Progress on Two Tracks, DOE and NRC</h3>
<p>Aurora-INL is on the DOE authorization track, with the NSDA approved and the PDSA under review; separately, the NRC approved Aurora's principal design criteria report. The presentation said the NRC is advancing Part 53 and a proposed Part 57 framework aimed at licensing microreactors in 6–12 months, which would make DOE-first assets more strategically valuable.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Expenses Expand Rapidly</h3>
<p>R&D rose 245% YoY and G&A 141% YoY, widening the operating loss to $51.2M; R&D and G&A staff grew by about 136 people combined in a year, and stock-based compensation rose from $2.3M to $15.6M. Interest income of $21.3M offset part of the loss. The 10-Q said the first plant is targeted for deployment in 2028 but acknowledged complex challenges in supply chain, construction, and design.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Full-Year Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Vertical integration of power, fuel, and isotopes</h3>
<p>Oklo builds on fast-reactor technology proven by EBR-II while also pursuing fuel recycling, fuel fabrication, and isotopes; the presentation calls power the core, fuel the support, and isotopes an expansion into high-value products. Customers include Meta and Switch. The weaknesses: no revenue or commercially operating plant yet, and fuel cost and availability remain uncertain.</p>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Big equity raise**: Completed the $1.5B ATM program, with net proceeds of $1.18B this quarter and $2.54B in cash and marketable securities.
- **Groves complete**: Isotope test reactor built in 229 days, targeting criticality on 7/4, its first operating nuclear facility.
- **Meta partnership**: Prepayment agreement for the 1.2 GW Ohio campus; PJM interconnection filed on 4/27.
- **Wider losses**: R&D +245% and G&A +141%; operating loss of $51.2M, partly offset by interest income.
- **Regulatory progress**: NRC approved design criteria; DOE is reviewing the Aurora-INL safety analysis.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: In Q1 Oklo raised $1.2B while its stock was high and quickly completed the Groves test reactor, accelerating both funding and execution; but revenue is still zero, the first plant is not due until 2028, and the market cap already prices in success years ahead.
