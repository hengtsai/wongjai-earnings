## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: AWS accelerates to 28%, fastest in 15 quarters</h3>
<p>AWS YoY growth rose from 17% in Q2'25 to 20%, 24% and 28%, with operating income of $14.2B, 59% of the company total. But operating margin of 37.7% was below last year's 39.5%, as depreciation up 33% YoY is already eating into margins. Jassy noted <b>AWS grew 28% on a very large base, its fastest in 15 quarters</b>. Over the past 12 months it deployed more than 2.1M AI chips, over half of them in-house Trainium.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: In-house chips top $20B run rate, locking in OpenAI and Anthropic</h3>
<p>The chip business, including Graviton, Trainium and Nitro, has an annual run rate above $20B, growing at triple digits YoY. OpenAI committed to use about 2 GW of Trainium capacity through AWS, ramping from 2027; Anthropic will get up to 5 GW of Trainium. AWS also announced it will deploy more than 1M NVIDIA GPUs starting in 2026, running both paths in parallel.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Half of the $2.78 EPS comes from Anthropic</h3>
<p>Non-operating income was $16B, including a $16.8B pre-tax gain on the Anthropic investment. At a 21% tax rate, EPS excluding it is about $1.56, close to last year's $1.59. Real core growth is better seen in operating income, up 30%. The old version's core EPS of $2.19, derived as operating income ÷ shares, left out income taxes and has been removed.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Capex up 77% YoY, free cash flow near zero</h3>
<p>Q1 purchases of property and equipment were $44.2B, the trailing 12-month net figure was $147.3B, up $59.3B YoY, and free cash flow fell from $25.9B to $1.2B. Amazon issued $53.4B of long-term debt in Q1, so cash actually rose to $143.1B. This is the first quarter Amazon began funding its AI build-out with debt, and net cash fell from $57.4B at the start of the year to about $24B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Retail units up 15% YoY, advertising TTM tops $70B</h3>
<p>North America operating margin rose from 6.3% to 7.9%, and advertising grew 24% YoY. Jassy said <b>trailing 12-month advertising revenue exceeded $70B and store unit growth reached 15%, the highest since the tail end of the pandemic lockdowns</b>, and that more than 1B items have been delivered same-day or next-day so far this year. He concluded that <b>Amazon is in the middle of some of the biggest inflection points of a lifetime, and in a leading position</b>.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q2 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: AWS scale + in-house chips + logistics network</h3>
<p>AWS is the largest cloud platform, and its in-house Trainium and Graviton chips lower compute costs and lock in big customers such as OpenAI and Anthropic. On the retail side, its own logistics network speeds up delivery, while advertising and Prime subscriptions monetize traffic; the three businesses reinforce one another.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Free cash flow at zero</b>: Just $1.2B over the trailing 12 months and -$17.2B in Q1 alone, with capex still accelerating.</li>
<li><b>EPS relies on investment gains</b>: Q1 non-operating income of $16B was 40% of pre-tax income, so changes in Anthropic's valuation flow straight into EPS.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>AWS margin slipping</b>: Operating margin of 37.7% is below last year's 39.5%, and depreciation up 33% YoY will keep pressing on it.</li>
<li><b>Debt-funded expansion</b>: $53.4B of debt issued in one quarter, with net cash down from $57.4B to about $24B.</li>
<li><b>Customer concentration</b>: Trainium growth depends on long-term commitments from OpenAI and Anthropic, both of which are burning heavy cash.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>In-house chips</b>: A $20B run rate growing at triple digits YoY, which can reduce reliance on NVIDIA and lift AWS margins.</li>
<li><b>Advertising and retail efficiency</b>: Advertising up 24% YoY and North America operating margin up to 7.9%, with room to improve further.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **AWS re-accelerates**: Up 28% YoY, fastest in 15 quarters, with 59% of company operating income, but operating margin 1.8pp below last year.
- **Record operating margin**: Operating income $23.9B (+30%), North America operating margin up from 6.3% to 7.9%.
- **Discount the EPS**: $2.78 includes a $16.8B pre-tax Anthropic gain; excluding it, about $1.56 (our estimate).
- **In-house chips take shape**: Run rate tops $20B, with Trainium commitments of about 2 GW from OpenAI and up to 5 GW from Anthropic.
- **Cash flow bottoming**: Trailing 12-month free cash flow is just $1.2B, and $53.4B of debt was issued in Q1 to fund capex.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Amazon's core business and AWS both accelerated this quarter, and its in-house chips won big deals from OpenAI and Anthropic; but half of EPS came from investment gains, free cash flow is near zero, and it has started funding its AI build-out with debt.
