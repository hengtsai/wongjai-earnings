## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Cloud up 82% YoY, clearly accelerating even excluding TPUs</h3>
<p>Google Cloud revenue was $24.8B, up 82% YoY, and the operating margin rose from 20.7% to 35.6%. This quarter the company delivered TPU systems into customer data centers and recognized revenue for the first time; CFO Anat Ashkenazi stressed that <b>Cloud revenue growth accelerated meaningfully even excluding TPU system sales</b> (paraphrased). Backlog rose by more than $50B QoQ to $514B, with over 50% expected to be recognized within 24 months. Gemini Enterprise is already used by nearly 90% of the Fortune 100.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Full-year capex raised to $195–205B</h3>
<p>Q2 capex was $44.9B, up 100% YoY, turning quarterly free cash flow to -$5.9B. Full-year guidance was raised from $180–190B to $195–205B, mainly to accelerate capacity delivery, and 2027 will rise significantly again. Ashkenazi said <b>the company remains in a supply-constrained environment</b> (paraphrased) and will keep investing as long as returns are attractive; she also flagged continued pressure on free cash flow, with Q3 expanding the use of third-party capacity as a bridge, which will modestly weigh on gross margin in the near term.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: $49.6B equity raise; long-term debt doubles to $98.2B in six months</h3>
<p>In June Alphabet raised $49.6B net through common stock plus mandatory convertible preferred stock, and long-term debt doubled in six months to $98.2B. Ashkenazi said the funding order is operating cash flow first, then debt, and that the equity raise was to maintain <b>a resilient, strong balance sheet</b> (paraphrased); she added that <b>the company does not currently plan to return to the equity market</b> (paraphrased) and will use the ATM program only to cover taxes on stock-based compensation. There were no buybacks in 1H, and Q2 interest expense was $1.28B versus only $260M a year earlier.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: $6.26 of the $9.11 EPS comes from equity valuation</h3>
<p>Other income was $98.0B, mainly $99.0B of unrealized gains on equity securities; the company disclosed that these gains added $77.1B to net income and $6.26 to EPS. Excluding them, EPS is about $2.85, and the 30% YoY rise in operating income reflects the real growth of the core business. Of $242.5B in cash and marketable securities, $87.1B is marketable equity, so future share price swings will make earnings highly volatile.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: AI Mode tops 1B monthly users, Gemini App at 950M</h3>
<p>Search and other revenue rose 17% YoY. CEO Sundar Pichai said AI Mode has surpassed 1B monthly active users globally and, like AI Overviews, is driving incremental queries; the Gemini App has 950M monthly active users, with daily actives up threefold. The model API processes about 22B tokens per minute, up from 16B last quarter. He said <b>the company remains supply-constrained, a sign of momentum and rapid adoption</b> (paraphrased). From Q3, Search faces a tougher comparison after last year's acceleration.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Moat and Second-Half Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: full-stack AI from TPU to Gemini</h3>
<p>Alphabet designs its own TPUs and owns the Gemini models, then reaches billions of users and businesses through Search, YouTube, Android and Cloud, making it one of the few companies that builds everything from chips to applications. TPUs have started selling directly into customer data centers, turning chips into a product too. Full-stack integration has brought the cost of serving AI Mode responses to its lowest since launch, sustaining a 34% operating margin despite heavy capex.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Cloud boom**: Revenue of $24.8B (+82%), operating margin 35.6%, backlog $514B.
- **Solid core business**: Revenue of $119.8B (+24%), operating income +30%, Search +17%.
- **Distorted EPS**: $6.26 of the $9.11 is equity valuation gains; excluding them, EPS is about $2.85.
- **Capex up again**: $195–205B for the year with a big increase again in 2027; Q2 free cash flow was -$5.9B.
- **New funding mix**: A $49.6B equity raise, $20.3B of debt and zero buybacks; the company says it will not return to the equity market.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Google Cloud's growth has caught up with the pace of AI investment, but capex is running even faster, and Alphabet raised equity for the first time to build compute; the question now is whether the backlog can turn into enough cash flow before 2027.
