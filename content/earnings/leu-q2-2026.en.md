## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue up 14% on uranium sales</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue was $176M, up 14% YoY: uranium revenue was $53.4M (zero a year earlier), but SWU revenue fell $25.7M as volume dropped 23% while the average price rose 3%. LEU customers mostly have annual purchase commitments, so quarterly gross profit swings with contract delivery timing.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: $900M HALEU contract formally signed</h3>
<p>This quarter Centrus formally signed a $900M HALEU enrichment contract with DOE and signed its first large commercial HALEU supply agreement, which may include prepayments. CEO Amir Vexler said that alongside the HALEU contracts the company <b>also secured potential prepayments from buyers</b>, strengthening its finances.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Backlog grows from $3.8B to $4.5B</h3>
<p>Total backlog is $4.5B and extends to 2040, and contingent LEU and HALEU backlog rose from $2.3B at the start of the year to $3B. Vexler said demand momentum is healthy and supply remains tight, <b>putting upward pressure on SWU prices</b>, and called the company the only listed, proven enricher in the market.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Expansion in full swing, first centrifuge due by year-end</h3>
<p>The company selected Geiger Brothers as the expansion's construction contractor and locked in most key suppliers, using larger commitments to reduce price volatility risk. The first new centrifuge is expected to be completed at Oak Ridge by year-end, and the full-year Piketon hiring target was raised from 100 to 175 people.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Profit falls as expansion lifts expenses</h3>
<p>GAAP net income was $16.8M, down 42% YoY, mainly because SG&A rose $12.8M, advanced technology costs rose $7.5M and gross profit fell $4M, partly offset by an $8.3M increase in investment income. Adjusted net income was $38.7M, up 12% YoY. Full-year revenue guidance was kept at $450–500M.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Full-Year Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: US domestic enrichment and its HALEU position</h3>
<p>Centrus is one of the few US companies with a record of producing HALEU, has formally won a $900M DOE contract and keeps adding contingent orders. Its weaknesses are that expansion still needs a lot of money and time, and the existing LEU business is still exposed to policy on Russian imports.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue growth**: Q2 revenue of $176M (+14%), driven by $53.4M of uranium sales, while SWU revenue fell 20%.
- **HALEU lands**: a $900M HALEU contract formally signed with DOE, plus the first large commercial HALEU agreement.
- **Backlog up**: total backlog of $4.5B and contingent backlog of $3B, both higher than at the start of the year.
- **Expansion moving**: construction contractor selected, the first new centrifuge expected by year-end, and the Piketon hiring target raised.
- **Profit down**: GAAP net income -42%, as SG&A and advanced technology costs rise with the expansion.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: In Q2 Centrus locked in the HALEU contract and a commercial agreement, lifted backlog to $4.5B and moved its expansion into full execution; but core profit fell on delivery timing and higher expenses, and the stock later dropped sharply.
