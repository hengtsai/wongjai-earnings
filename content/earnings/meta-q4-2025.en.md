## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Ad volume and price both rise, revenue tops guidance ceiling</h3>
<p>Q4 revenue of $59.9B exceeded the $56–59B guidance ceiling given in Q3; ad impressions grew 18% YoY and average price per ad rose 6%, lifting ad revenue 24% YoY. Zuckerberg said <b>Meta had strong business performance in 2025 and looks forward to advancing personal superintelligence for people around the world in 2026</b>. Family daily active people reached 3.58B, showing continued user growth.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Costs up 40% YoY, operating margin drops 7 percentage points</h3>
<p>Q4 total costs were $35.1B, up 40% YoY and far above 24% revenue growth: R&D rose 41% YoY and G&A jumped from $760M to $3.7B. Operating income grew only 6%, and operating margin fell from 48.3% to 41.3%. Family of Apps operating margin declined from 59.9% to 52.2%, showing margin pressure comes not just from Reality Labs, as AI investment in the core business is also weighing on profitability.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: 2026 capex up to $135B, while operating income growth is still pledged</h3>
<p>The CFO guided 2026 capex to $115–135B (vs. $72.2B in 2025), with growth driven mainly by efforts <b>to support Meta Superintelligence Labs and the core business</b>; total expenses are guided to $162–169B. The company also stated that <b>despite the sharp increase in infrastructure investment, 2026 operating income is expected to remain higher than in 2025</b>. Q1 revenue guidance is $53.5–56.5B, up roughly 26–34% YoY.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Free cash flow declines, turning to debt issuance</h3>
<p>Full-year operating cash flow rose 27%, but capex surged 84%, reducing free cash flow from $52.1B to $43.6B. Meta issued $29.9B in long-term debt in Q4, doubling long-term debt to $58.7B; non-marketable equity investments rose from $6.1B to $27.5B, with $18.3B invested over the year. Share buybacks were paused in Q4, shrinking net cash from $49B to $22.8B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Reality Labs loses $19.2B in FY25, expected flat in 2026</h3>
<p>Reality Labs Q4 revenue was $960M, down 12% YoY, with an operating loss of $6B; full-year loss widened 8% to $19.2B. The CFO noted 2026 expense growth will be concentrated in Family of Apps, and that <b>Reality Labs operating losses will remain roughly in line with 2025 levels</b>. Management also flagged legal and regulatory headwinds in the U.S. and Europe, noting multiple youth-related trials in the U.S. this year that <b>could ultimately result in material losses</b>.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue above guidance**: Q4 revenue of $59.9B (+24%) topped the company's guidance ceiling, with full-year revenue of $201B passing $200B for the first time.
- **Margin compression**: Costs rose 40%, pushing Q4 operating margin from 48.3% to 41.3%, while Family of Apps margin also fell 7.7pp.
- **Capex doubling**: $72.2B in 2025 (+84%), with 2026 guidance at $115–135B.
- **Weaker cash flow**: Full-year FCF of $43.6B (-16%), with $29.9B debt issued and buybacks paused in Q4.
- **One-time tax charge**: Full-year EPS of $23.49 includes a $15.9B Q3 tax charge, or ~$29.7 excluding it.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Meta's 2025 Q4 ad revenue came in above guidance, but AI spending shaved 7 percentage points off operating margin; with 2026 capex set to nearly double again at the top end, management is betting operating profit can still grow.
