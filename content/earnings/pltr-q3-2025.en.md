## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue 9% Above Guidance Top End</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue was $1.18B (+63% YoY, +18% QoQ), roughly $94M above the $1.083–1.087B guidance top end given in August. Adjusted operating income of $600M also topped the $493–497M guidance. CEO Alex Karp said the results <b>demonstrate the undeniable transformative impact of AIP compounding AI leverage</b>, with Rule of 40 reaching 114%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: U.S. Commercial Surges 121% YoY</h3>
<p>U.S. commercial revenue reached $397M (+121% YoY, +29% QoQ), making it the fastest-growing segment; U.S. commercial TCV was $1.31B (+342% YoY) and remaining deal value reached $3.63B (+199% YoY). The AIP platform accelerated enterprise adoption with notably more large deals. Total U.S. revenue accounted for 75% of total revenue.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Government Business Also Accelerates</h3>
<p>U.S. government revenue was $486M (+52% YoY, +14% QoQ), remaining the largest portion of U.S. revenue at 55% (our calculation). Karp noted that total U.S. revenue growth <b>accelerated to 77% YoY</b>. Having both government and commercial engines accelerating together gives Palantir a rare dual foundation of defense and enterprise clients in AI software.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: High Profitability and Strong Cash Flow</h3>
<p>GAAP operating income was $393M (33% operating margin) and GAAP net income was $476M (40% net margin); adjusted free cash flow was $540M (46% margin). Gross margin reached 82% while operating expenses grew just 25% YoY, showing clear operating leverage. With $6.4B in cash and Treasuries and no debt, management reiterated GAAP profitability in every quarter this year.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Q4 and Full-Year Guidance Raised Again</h3>
<p>Q4 revenue guidance is $1.327–1.331B (+61% YoY), which Karp described as <b>the highest sequential revenue growth guidance in company history</b>. Full-year revenue guidance was raised to $4.396–4.400B (+53%), U.S. commercial to over $1.433B (+104%), adjusted operating income to ~$2.15B, and free cash flow to $1.9–2.1B.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q4 Guidance

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: AIP Platform + Government Trust</h3>
<p>Palantir's Foundry, Gotham, and AIP platforms are deeply embedded in government and enterprise data and decision workflows, creating high switching costs, while long-term defense and intelligence work has built trust and security clearance barriers. Weaknesses include an extreme valuation, a still-small revenue base, and stock-based compensation near 15% of revenue that continues to dilute shareholders.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue topped guidance**: Q3 revenue of $1.18B (+63%) came in ~9% above the guidance top end.
- **U.S. commercial surge**: U.S. commercial revenue reached $397M (+121%) with TCV of $1.31B (+342%).
- **High profitability**: Adjusted operating margin of 51%, GAAP net margin of 40%, and Rule of 40 at 114%.
- **Guidance raised**: Full-year revenue of $4.396–4.400B (+53%), Q4 guidance +61% YoY.
- **Extreme valuation**: At over 120x P/S, shares still fell 8% despite an all-around beat on guidance.

> 💡 **One-line takeaway**: Palantir raised guidance across revenue and profit on explosive U.S. commercial AIP momentum, yet shares still dropped at over 120x P/S—showing that years of rapid growth are already priced in.
