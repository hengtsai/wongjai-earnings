## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Deliveries up 49%, all three brands up in volume and price</h3>
<p>Q2 deliveries were 107,658, up 49.4% YoY. Founder William Li said <b>sales volume and average transaction prices grew for all three brands, NIO, ONVO and Firefly</b>. The ES8 delivered its 140,000th unit 335 days after launch, and the ES9 was the best-selling model priced above RMB500,000 in June and July.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Vehicle margin of 18.5%, up 8.2pp YoY</h3>
<p>Vehicle margin rose from 10.3% a year ago to 18.5%, and overall gross margin was 18.4%. CFO Stanley Qu said strong sales of high-margin models and continued cost structure optimization <b>kept gross margin healthy despite rising cost pressure</b>, and services and community businesses also kept contributing profit.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Non-GAAP operating income positive for a second straight quarter</h3>
<p>The operating loss narrowed from RMB4.91B a year ago to RMB350M; excluding share-based compensation, operating income was RMB210M, positive for a second straight quarter. Net loss was RMB530M and non-GAAP net income RMB26.1M. The improvement came mainly from strong revenue growth and higher gross margin.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Q3 deliveries of 108,000–111,000</h3>
<p>Q3 guidance calls for 108,000–111,000 deliveries, up 24–27.5% YoY, and revenue of RMB33.29–34.05B, up 52.7–56.2% YoY. July and August deliveries already total 71,770, so September needs about 36,000–39,000 (our estimate). The new five-seat ES8 launched in July, with deliveries starting the next day.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: The balance sheet is still thin</h3>
<p>The period-end cash position was about RMB56.7B, but parent shareholders' equity was only RMB4.06B, short-term borrowings rose RMB3.5B in six months, and accounts and notes payable were RMB60.4B. Although NIO is near breakeven, its capital structure still relies on supplier payment terms and external financing at subsidiaries.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q3 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Three-brand coverage and battery swap network</h3>
<p>NIO targets the premium segment with NIO, family large SUVs with ONVO and premium small cars with Firefly, leading in each brand's price band; its battery swap network and user community differentiate it. Weaknesses are China's fierce EV price war and a thin balance sheet.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Delivery growth**: Q2 deliveries of 107,658 (+49%), with all three brands up in volume and price.
- **Margins up**: Vehicle margin of 18.5% and overall gross margin of 18.4%, up more than 8pp YoY.
- **Near breakeven**: Operating loss of RMB350M; non-GAAP operating income of RMB210M, positive for a second straight quarter.
- **Guidance slows**: Q3 deliveries of 108,000–111,000, only about 0–3% QoQ growth.
- **Capital structure**: Cash of RMB56.7B, but parent equity of only RMB4.06B and rising short-term borrowings.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: NIO's Q2 volume and gross margin both rose and non-GAAP operating income was positive for a second straight quarter, a clear operational improvement; but Q3 growth is slowing and the balance sheet is thin, so the market still assigns a very low revenue multiple.
