## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue doubles, opex ratio falls to 1.66%, operating margin back above 4%</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue was NT$1.15T, up 28% QoQ, while operating expenses rose only 11%, so the opex ratio fell from 1.92% to 1.66% and operating margin rose from 3.74% to 4.24%. Gross margin of 5.90% was 1.49pp below last year because AI racks pass large GPU and memory costs through into revenue; gross profit still grew 62% YoY. At the August earnings call, President Jeff Lin said the AI server market is an oligopoly with demand exceeding supply, new platforms carry higher prices, and the company remains confident of sustaining a certain margin (as reported by the media).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Operating cash flow swings positive to NT$92.9B and goes to repaying short-term debt</h3>
<p>In the first half, stocking up on materials in advance drove an operating cash outflow of NT$251.4B; in Q3 it turned into an inflow of NT$92.9B, free cash flow was NT$77.1B, and short-term borrowings fell NT$30B. The swing came mainly from a NT$224.5B increase in accounts payable, while receivables and inventory rose only NT$81.4B and NT$108.9B. In August, Chairman Simon Lin said high leverage is normal in the AI industry and that memory shortages and price hikes have led customers to reserve capacity early, increasing the company's short-term cash pressure, but the company is financially healthy and will keep working down its leverage (as reported by the media). Q3 is the first time that statement showed up in the financial statements.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: GDR adds about NT$46.3B of equity, debt ratio falls to 78.6%</h3>
<p>In August the board approved a GDR of up to 250M common shares to cope with rising AI component prices, early stocking and plant expansion. In Q3 share capital rose NT$2.5B, cash capital increases brought in NT$46.28B, parent equity rose NT$72.7B in the quarter, and the debt ratio fell from 82.4% to 78.6%. The cost is about 8% more shares: share capital of NT$34.3B corresponds to about 3.43B shares, and Q3 diluted EPS of NT$5.32 is 7% below basic EPS, reflecting potential shares from convertibles and other instruments.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Consolidated net income of NT$32.5B, 43% goes to minority shareholders of subsidiaries</h3>
<p>Q3 consolidated net income was NT$32.49B, of which NT$18.4B was attributable to the parent and NT$14.08B, or 43%, to non-controlling interests. A sizable share of Wistron's AI server profit sits in subsidiaries it does not fully own (such as Wiwynn), so Wistron should be judged on "net income attributable to parent" and EPS, not consolidated revenue alone. The good news is that the parent's share is rising: from only 45% a year earlier to 57% in Q3, with parent net income up 148% YoY, faster than the 97% growth in consolidated net income.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Strong start to Q4 as Vera Rubin takes over from GB300</h3>
<p>October revenue was NT$402.6B, up 118% YoY and 6% MoM, bringing the first 10 months to NT$3.29T, up 99.5% YoY. In August, Jeff Lin said both GB300 and Vera Rubin have won orders from new customers, on both the enterprise and CSP sides; Vera Rubin has entered volume production, shipments will scale further next year, and orders in the first half of 2027 are expected to keep rising (as reported by the media). On 11/11 the board approved further capex of NT$12.84B in Tainan and Hsinchu and $17.76M in Mexico to expand AI server capacity.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q4 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: end-to-end from components to full racks, plus capacity in Taiwan, the US and Mexico</h3>
<p>Wistron makes both components such as GPU baseboards and L10–L11 full-rack systems. At the March earnings call it said system-level products began shipping in Q3 last year and that it is targeting small and mid-sized NeoClouds beyond its existing large customers; Jeff Lin said these end customers <b>"won't fight with our customers"</b>. Capacity in Taiwan, the US and Mexico lets it supply customers close to where they are.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>High leverage and interest</b>: Borrowings and corporate bonds total NT$460.3B; nine-month finance costs were NT$24.1B, up 135% YoY and equal to 22% of operating income.</li>
<li><b>Nine-month free cash flow is still -NT$197.8B</b>: The Q3 turnaround relied on suppliers fronting money; if payments bunch up in Q4, cash flow could turn negative again.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Pass-through costs weigh on gross margin</b>: Gross margin is 5.90%, down 1.49pp YoY; if memory and GPU prices keep rising, gross margin could be diluted further.</li>
<li><b>Equity dilution</b>: The GDR adds about 250M shares and diluted EPS is 7% below basic EPS, so per-share earnings growth will lag net income growth.</li>
<li><b>Product transition</b>: During the shift from GB300 to Vera Rubin, any problems with yields or customer order timing could make quarterly revenue volatile.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Strong start to Q4</b>: October revenue was NT$402.6B, up 118% YoY, so Q4 revenue will likely set another record.</li>
<li><b>New networking business</b>: Shipments are up more than 10x this year, with margins better than PCs and AI systems, which can lift the overall margin slightly.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue doubles, profit grows faster**: Revenue of NT$1.15T (+102%), net income attributable to parent of NT$18.4B (+148%) and record EPS of NT$5.74.
- **Operating margin recovers**: The opex ratio fell to 1.66% and operating margin rose from 3.74% to 4.24%, offsetting the lower gross margin.
- **Cash flow turns positive**: Q3 operating cash flow of NT$92.9B and short-term debt down NT$30B, but nine-month free cash flow is still -NT$197.8B.
- **GDR buys breathing room**: Parent equity rose NT$72.7B in the quarter and the debt ratio fell to 78.6%, at the cost of about 8% more shares.
- **Good start to Q4**: October revenue up 118% YoY as Vera Rubin takes over.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: In Q3 Wistron turned doubled revenue into cash and a higher operating margin, and the GDR gave its balance sheet some breathing room. Next to watch: whether cash flow can stop relying on suppliers fronting money as Vera Rubin takes over.
