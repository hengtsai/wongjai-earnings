## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Record revenue, gross margin above guidance</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue of €11.66B landed at the top of the €11–12B guidance range, and the 57.5% gross margin beat the 55–57% guidance. CEO Christophe Fouquet said the record revenue <b>reflects strong execution on the company's capacity ramp</b>; installed base management revenue of €3.3B beat guidance on continued strong customer demand for capacity upgrades, which also lifted the gross margin.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Capacity may fall short in 2027–2028, customers want to book into 2030</h3>
<p>Fouquet said customers keep raising their short- and medium-term equipment needs because of the scale and duration of AI investment, and <b>some customers are looking to place orders for 2029 and even 2030</b>. The company now expects planned supply for 2027 and 2028 to fall short of demand and is studying a 2028 capacity increase above the 30% it previously cited.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: ArFi units jump, China's share rebounds</h3>
<p>ASML sold 121 lithography systems in Q3, 30 more than in Q2, mainly because ArFi immersion rose from 23 to 40 units, lifting ArFi's share of system sales from 29% to 39%. EUV was 17 units and 49%. By region, China's share rose from 14% to 23% and South Korea's fell from 43% to 31%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Net income doubles, but includes a one-off investment gain</h3>
<p>Operating income was €5.17B, up 110% YoY, and the 44.4% operating margin was the highest in five quarters. Net income was €4.96B, up 134% YoY, including a €510M equity investment gain; the carrying value of equity investments also rose from €1.32B to €2.24B in Q3. Even excluding this, operating profit growth remains very strong.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Q4 revenue guidance midpoint €14.7B, full year €44–45B</h3>
<p>Q4 guidance calls for revenue of €14.2–15.2B, a gross margin of about 57% and installed base management of about €3.7B; full-year revenue of €44–45B narrows July's €43–45B range, with a gross margin of about 56%. ASML bought back about 2.8 million shares for €4.1B in Q3 and will pay an interim dividend of €1.88 per share on 11/4.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q4 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Sole supplier of EUV</h3>
<p>ASML is the world's only supplier of EUV lithography systems, which advanced logic and memory processes cannot do without. This quarter it announced a collaboration with TSMC to move High NA EUV to 12-inch masks, which can raise dual-mask exposure throughput by more than 40%. The larger the installed base, the more upgrade and service revenue.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Export controls</b>: China's share of system sales rebounded to 23%, so policy changes directly affect revenue.</li>
<li><b>AI investment cycle</b>: Upward demand revisions stem mainly from customers' views on the scale of AI investment; if that turns, orders will slow.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Capacity bottleneck</b>: Supply in 2027–2028 may not meet demand; the pace of expansion sets the revenue ceiling.</li>
<li><b>One-off gain</b>: Q3 net income includes a €510M investment gain that cannot be extrapolated.</li>
<li><b>Cash flow volatility</b>: Prepayments make quarterly cash flow swing widely between -€2.2B and €11.8B.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Service revenue</b>: Installed base management grew 68% YoY on strong demand for capacity upgrades, with steadier margins.</li>
<li><b>Shareholder returns</b>: €4.1B of Q3 buybacks and an interim dividend of €1.88 per share.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Record revenue**: Q3 revenue of €11.66B (+55%) landed at the top of guidance, and the 57.5% gross margin beat guidance.
- **Demand exceeds capacity**: Planned capacity for 2027–2028 may not meet demand, and customers want to book 2029–2030.
- **Mix shift**: ArFi units rose from 23 to 40, and China's share rose to 23%.
- **Net income includes a one-off**: Net income of €4.96B includes a €510M equity investment gain.
- **Q4 re-accelerates**: Q4 revenue guidance of €14.2–15.2B, with the midpoint up about 51% YoY.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: ASML's Q3 revenue and gross margin both beat, and management said for the first time that 2027–2028 capacity may not keep up with AI-driven demand. Near term, watch China's rising share and the one-off investment gain; long term, watch the pace of capacity expansion.
