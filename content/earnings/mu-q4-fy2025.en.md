## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue and gross margin top guidance</h3>
<p>Q4 revenue was $11.32B (+46% YoY, +22% QoQ), above the $10.4–11.0B guidance top end; GAAP gross margin of 44.7% exceeded the 40–42% guidance ceiling, and GAAP EPS of $2.83 was $0.39 above the top of guidance (our estimate). The CEO said <b>Micron capped a record fiscal year with strong Q4 performance</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Cloud memory revenue triples</h3>
<p>Beginning this quarter, results are reported under four business units. Cloud Memory revenue was $4.54B, more than tripling YoY, with a 59% gross margin and 48% operating margin, making it the largest and most profitable segment. Core Data Center revenue was $1.58B (-23% YoY), Mobile and Client grew 25% YoY, and Automotive and Embedded rose 17% YoY (YoY rates are our estimates).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Record full-year revenue</h3>
<p>FY25 full-year revenue rose 49% YoY to $37.38B, with GAAP net income of $8.54B and EPS of $7.59 versus just $0.70 a year earlier; full-year operating cash flow doubled to $17.53B. The CEO said the data center business reached a record high in FY25 and enters FY26 with the company's most competitive product portfolio to date.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Heavy capex limits free cash flow</h3>
<p>Full-year net capex was $13.80B with adjusted free cash flow of $3.72B; Q4 net capex was $4.93B, leaving free cash flow at just $800M. Total debt rose to $14.58B against $11.94B in cash and marketable investments (our estimate). Micron is investing heavily to expand capacity for AI-driven memory demand (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Q1 guidance points to continued growth</h3>
<p>FY26 Q1 revenue guidance is $12.5B±$300M, up about 10% QoQ (our estimate), with GAAP gross margin of 50.5%±1% and EPS of $3.56±$0.15—gross margin guidance up roughly 6pp from Q4. Shares fell from $166.41 to $161.71 on day one after earnings, likely reflecting the sharp pre-earnings rally (our inference).</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Moat and Q1 Guidance

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: DRAM, HBM, and NAND Technology</h3>
<p>Micron is one of the world's major DRAM and NAND suppliers, seeing sharp growth in high-bandwidth memory and data center memory needed for AI servers, with Cloud Memory revenue tripling in a year. The company continues to invest in advanced nodes and capacity. Weaknesses include severe memory industry cyclicality, declining Core Data Center revenue, heavy expansion capex, and limited free cash flow.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Above guidance**: Q4 revenue of $11.32B, GAAP gross margin of 44.7%, and EPS of $2.83 all topped the high end of guidance.
- **Cloud Memory surge**: Revenue more than tripled YoY with a 48% operating margin.
- **Record full year**: FY25 revenue of $37.38B (+49%) and EPS of $7.59.
- **Capital intensive**: Full-year net capex of $13.8B left free cash flow at $3.7B.
- **Q1 guidance**: Revenue of $12.5B and gross margin of about 50.5%.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Micron capped a record year by topping Q4 guidance, with Cloud Memory revenue tripling in a year and Q1 gross margin guided above 50%; shares dipped slightly post-earnings, and whether capex and gross margins can rise together will be a key focus in FY26.
