## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue and Non-GAAP EPS Top Guidance</h3>
<p>Q4 revenue of $11.27B (+10% YoY) beat the $10.3–11.1B guidance ceiling; QCT of $9.82B also topped guidance, and Non-GAAP EPS of $3.00 (+12% YoY) exceeded the $2.75–2.95 range. Handset revenue of $6.96B (+14% YoY) drove growth, while automotive reached $1.05B, surpassing $1B in a single quarter for the first time (our estimate).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: New Tax Law Triggers $5.7B Non-Cash Charge</h3>
<p>Management stated that the new U.S. tax law (the One Big Beautiful Bill Act) will subject the company to the corporate alternative minimum tax starting in FY26, prompting a $5.7B valuation allowance against U.S. federal deferred tax assets—a $5.29 per-share GAAP impact that resulted in a Q4 GAAP loss of $2.89 per share. The company expects its future effective tax rate to stay at 13–14% with lower cash taxes.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Record Full-Year QCT Revenue</h3>
<p>FY25 revenue rose 14% YoY to $44.28B, with QCT reaching a record $38.37B (+16% YoY). CEO Cristiano Amon noted that <b>non-Apple QCT revenue grew 18% YoY and combined full-year automotive and IoT revenue increased 27%</b>. Full-year automotive reached $3.96B (+36% YoY) and IoT hit $6.62B (+22% YoY), together accounting for 28% of QCT (our estimate).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Licensing Revenue Declines as Expenses Rise</h3>
<p>QTL licensing revenue fell 7% YoY to $1.41B with EBT down 9% YoY; full-year QTL revenue was flat at $5.58B. SG&A expenses rose 19% YoY to $910M, while other items excluded from Non-GAAP included $100M in acquisition costs and $59M in restructuring charges. Gross margin was 55.3%, down 1.1pp YoY (our estimate).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: $12.6B Returned to Shareholders, Q1 Guidance Shows Growth</h3>
<p>FY25 shareholder returns totaled $12.6B: $8.79B in buybacks (56M shares) and $3.81B in dividends, reducing ending shares outstanding by 3.5% YoY to 1.074B (our estimate). Q1 guidance calls for revenue of $11.8–12.6B (~5% YoY growth at the midpoint) and Non-GAAP EPS of $3.30–3.50. Shares dropped from $179.72 to $173.20 on the first day after earnings.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q1 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: Snapdragon plus patent licensing</h3>
<p>Qualcomm's QTL licensing business generates a 72% EBT margin and steady cash flow; Snapdragon maintains a deep customer base in premium Android handsets, while automotive and IoT now make up nearly 30% of QCT. The CEO noted expansion into data centers and advanced robotics, alongside new automated driving software. Weaknesses are declining licensing revenue and QCT's continued 70% reliance on handsets.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Above guidance**: Revenue of $11.27B and Non-GAAP EPS of $3.00 both topped guidance.
- **Tax charge**: New tax law triggered a $5.7B non-cash charge, swinging GAAP to a loss.
- **Full-year records**: FY25 revenue of $44.28B and QCT of $38.37B set new highs.
- **Auto tops $1B**: $1.05B in Q4; full-year revenue up 36% YoY.
- **Q1 guidance**: Revenue of $11.8–12.6B and Non-GAAP EPS of $3.30–3.50.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Qualcomm's core business topped FY25 Q4 guidance with record full-year QCT revenue, but a $5.7B non-cash tax charge under the new tax law drove a GAAP loss alongside softer licensing revenue, nudging shares lower post-earnings.
