## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue Up 36% YoY</h3>
<p>Q1 revenue was $348M, up 36% YoY; diagnostics was $261M, up 35%, and data and applications $87M, up 41%. Founder Eric Lefkofsky said the growth reflects <b>accelerating demand for its AI diagnostics platform</b> and the value of its multimodal data and AI models, with oncology testing and data modeling especially strong.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: MRD Tests Up About 5x YoY</h3>
<p>MRD (minimal residual disease) tests reached about 6,500, up about 500% YoY and higher again than about 4,700 in Q4. Oncology test volume rose 28% YoY; hereditary test volume rose 54%, but only 7% excluding the Ambry acquisition, so growth momentum is concentrated in oncology and MRD.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Pharma Partnerships Expand</h3>
<p>This quarter Tempus formed a multi-year strategic collaboration with Merck that uses Tempus data and the Lens platform to speed up biomarker research, and expanded its work with Gilead. Insights data licensing revenue rose 44% YoY, gross margin rose from 60.7% to 63.8%, and the data business helped gross profit grow 43%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Wider GAAP Loss, EBITDA Near Breakeven</h3>
<p>Adjusted EBITDA was -$2.8M, better than -$16.2M a year ago but below Q4's +$12.9M. The GAAP net loss of $126M was wider than last year, mainly because of a $32.3M unrealized loss on marketable securities and $56.3M of stock-based compensation; the operating loss was $84.7M.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Full-Year Guidance Nudged Higher</h3>
<p>Full-year revenue guidance was raised from about $1.59B to $1.59–1.60B, up about 25% YoY, while adjusted EBITDA stays at about $65M. Because Q1 EBITDA was negative, the company is implying about $68M over the remaining three quarters (our estimate), putting more weight on the second half.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Full-Year Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: the testing data + AI model flywheel</h3>
<p>Tempus builds clinical and genomic data from a large volume of oncology tests, then licenses it to drug makers such as Merck and Gilead and builds AI models on it. The rapid ramp in MRD widens its test menu, and the bigger the data set, the more valuable the licenses and models become.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue growth**: Q1 revenue of $348M (+36%), with diagnostics +35% and data and applications +41%.
- **MRD ramp**: about 6,500 MRD tests, up about 500% YoY.
- **Pharma partnerships**: a multi-year collaboration with Merck and an expanded partnership with Gilead.
- **Profits not yet steady**: adjusted EBITDA of -$2.8M; the GAAP net loss of $126M includes a securities valuation loss.
- **Guidance raised**: full-year revenue of $1.59–1.60B and adjusted EBITDA of about $65M.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Tempus kept revenue and data licensing growing 30–40% in Q1, and MRD is ramping fast; but EBITDA swung negative, debt exceeds cash, and the full-year profit target depends on the last three quarters.
