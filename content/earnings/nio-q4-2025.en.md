## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: First-ever quarterly operating profit</h3>
<p>Q4 operating income was RMB810M and net income RMB280M, versus an operating loss of RMB6.03B a year earlier. CFO Stanley Yu Qu said non-GAAP operating income of RMB1.25B <b>turned positive for a quarter for the first time, a major milestone in operating performance</b>. But the full-year net loss was still RMB14.94B, and Q4 net income attributable to ordinary shareholders was only RMB120M, or RMB0.05 per share; whether profits can continue depends on the Q1 off-season (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Product mix lifts margins</h3>
<p>Vehicle margin was 18.1%, up 3.4pp QoQ, which the company attributed mainly to a better product mix. NIO-brand deliveries rose from 37,000 to 67,000, lifting its share to 54%, and revenue per vehicle was about RMB253K, up 15% QoQ. William Li said the all-new ES8 <b>set a monthly delivery record for models priced above RMB400,000</b>, and the ONVO L90 was the best-selling large battery-electric SUV of 2025.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Expenses cut by about 30% in a year</h3>
<p>R&D expense was RMB2.03B, down 44% YoY, and SG&A RMB3.54B, down 27%, which the company attributed mainly to lower personnel costs after organizational optimization. Revenue grew 76% YoY while R&D plus SG&A fell 35% — the other half of the turnaround. William Li said that in 2026 the company will still <b>invest decisively in its 12 full-stack core technologies</b>, so whether R&D spending can stay low remains to be seen (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Q1 guidance nearly doubles deliveries</h3>
<p>Q1 guidance calls for 80,000–83,000 deliveries, up 90–97% YoY, and revenue of RMB24.48–25.18B, up 103–109%. 47,979 vehicles were delivered in January and February. But compared with Q4's 125,000, Q1 is down about 34–36% QoQ, and with smaller off-season volume, operating profit may not hold (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Cash growth relies on suppliers</h3>
<p>The cash position rose RMB9.2B QoQ to RMB45.9B, but accounts and notes payable rose RMB13.8B over the same period to RMB53.3B, far more than shareholders' equity of RMB4.2B. The press release also disclosed that in February Shenji (its smart-driving chip subsidiary) brought in RMB2.26B of outside investment, with NIO still holding 62.7%; on 3/6 the board granted William Li 248M performance-based restricted shares tied to market-cap and net income targets.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Moat and Q1 Guidance

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: three brands and the battery-swap network</h3>
<p>NIO covers different price points with three brands — premium NIO, family-oriented ONVO and compact firefly — and all three set delivery records in Q4; in-house chips and electric drive systems lower costs, and the battery-swap network is a service rivals find hard to replicate. The weakness is the balance sheet: shareholders' equity is only RMB4.2B, and working capital relies heavily on suppliers.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Record deliveries**: Q4 deliveries of 124,807 vehicles (+72%), with NIO, ONVO and firefly all setting quarterly records.
- **First profit**: Operating income of RMB810M, net income of RMB280M and non-GAAP operating income of RMB1.25B.
- **Margin improvement**: Vehicle margin of 18.1% and overall gross margin of 17.5%, driven mainly by a higher share of pricier models.
- **Lower expenses**: R&D down 44% YoY and SG&A down 27%, as organizational optimization pays off.
- **Still a thin balance sheet**: Cash of RMB45.9B, but payables of RMB53.3B and shareholders' equity of only RMB4.2B.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: NIO posted its first quarterly operating profit in Q4 on higher volumes of pricier models and cost cuts, proving it can make money at scale; but it still lost RMB14.9B for the year and has only RMB4.2B of equity, so holding on to profits in the Q1 off-season is the next test.
