## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Groves test reactor reaches criticality in under a year</h3>
<p>The Groves isotope test reactor in Texas went from an empty lot in September 2025 to first criticality in July 2026, about 11 months. The presentation said this <b>validates Oklo's ability to build and operate nuclear facilities at industry-leading speed</b>, with private funding and under strict DOE oversight.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: First revenue booked, still small</h3>
<p>Q2 recognized $1.21M of revenue and $0.72M of cost of sales, Oklo's first revenue ever, though the presentation did not say where it came from. R&D expense rose 244% YoY to $39.5M, widening the operating loss to $73.2M; $23.2M of interest income offset part of the loss.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Aurora-INL wins DOE approval of its preliminary safety analysis</h3>
<p>DOE approved the preliminary safety analysis for the first 75MW Aurora plant, and site development and construction continue. For the 1.2GW Ohio campus, Oklo signed an MOU with Kiewit covering engineering, procurement and construction, and is advancing PJM grid interconnection and transmission planning.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Fuel strategy, a Centrus LOI and plutonium talks</h3>
<p>Centrus signed a letter of intent to support HALEU for up to 5 Aurora plants; Oklo is also in advanced talks with DOE on surplus defense plutonium and signed an MOU with Standard Nuclear to offtake recycled material. Fuel is a key bottleneck for deploying advanced reactors.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: $1.9B ATM raise, $3B in cash</h3>
<p>First-half ATM sales raised about $1.9B, lifting cash and marketable securities to $3B with no financial debt. First-half operating cash flow was -$65.5M and capex $127M, so at that free cash flow rate the funds would last about 7.8 years (our estimate); but Class A weighted shares rose from 140M a year earlier to 176M, about 26% dilution (our estimate).</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: integrated power, fuel and isotopes</h3>
<p>Oklo runs three businesses, power, fuel fabrication and recycling, and isotopes, which share licensing, fuel, siting and construction capabilities. Groves reaching criticality quickly shows execution; but commercial plants are still years from generating power, and until then revenue will come mainly from small businesses such as isotopes.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **First revenue**: Q2 revenue of $1.21M, with the operating loss widening to $73.2M.
- **Groves criticality**: the isotope test reactor went from empty lot to first criticality in about 11 months.
- **Regulatory progress**: Aurora-INL and the A3F fuel facility both received DOE approval of their preliminary safety analyses.
- **Fuel strategy**: a Centrus LOI supports HALEU for up to 5 plants, and plutonium talks are under way.
- **Well funded**: about $1.9B raised via ATM, $3B of cash and marketable securities and no financial debt.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: In Q2 Oklo proved its execution with Groves' fast path to criticality and began booking small amounts of revenue; after its raises it holds $3B of cash, but its power plants are still years from generating, so the valuation depends on further regulatory and fuel progress.
