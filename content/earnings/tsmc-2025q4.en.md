## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Gross Margin Hits 62.3%, 1.3pp Above Guidance Top</h3>
<p>Q4 gross margin rose from 59.5% in Q3 to 62.3%, while operating margin of 54.0% beat the 51% upper end of guidance by 3pp. Wafer shipments fell 3% QoQ yet revenue grew 5.7% QoQ, indicating a mix shift toward higher-priced nodes (our inference). FX also contributed: Q4 average FX of 31.01 was about 1.3% weaker than the 30.6 guidance assumption. CFO Wendell Huang noted that <b>fourth-quarter business was supported by strong demand for leading-edge process technologies</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: 3nm Jumps 5pp in One Quarter, Advanced Nodes Reach 77%</h3>
<p>3nm rose from 23% to 28% of wafer revenue, with 5nm at 35% and 7nm at 14%, bringing 7nm and below to 77% combined. By platform, Smartphone grew 11% QoQ to 32% of revenue and HPC grew 4% QoQ to 55%, together accounting for nearly 90%. New handset ramps boosted smartphone demand alongside the 3nm share gain (our inference). Consumer electronics fell 22% QoQ but represents only 1%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: 2026 Capital Budget Set at $52–56B</h3>
<p>TSMC guides for a 2026 capital budget of $52–56B. 2025 CapEx was NT$1.27T—or ~$40.9B at the full-year average FX rate of ~31.1—implying another 27–37% increase in 2026 (our calculation). Management also outlined long-term targets: a 2024–2029 USD revenue CAGR near 25%, long-term gross margin of 56% or higher, and ROE in the high 20s.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: 2026 Revenue Projected to Grow Near 30%</h3>
<p>Management projects 2026 USD revenue growth near 30%. Q1 guidance calls for USD revenue of $34.6–35.8B (+3–6% QoQ, +36–40% YoY), gross margin of 63–65%, and operating margin of 54–56%, all higher than Q4 actuals. Wendell Huang stated that <b>entering the first quarter of 2026, business is expected to continue being supported by strong demand for leading-edge process technologies</b>. Q1 assumes FX at 31.6.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: NT$1T Free Cash Flow, Inventory Days Drop to 74</h3>
<p>Full-year free cash flow reached NT$1.00T (+15% YoY), growing despite a 33% rise in CapEx. Q4 operating cash flow was NT$725.5B, up 70% from Q3. Inventory of NT$288.1B was flat YoY even as revenue rose 20%, cutting DIO from 80 to 74 days; DSO stood at 26 days. Cash and marketable securities surpassed NT$3T for the first time.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q1 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: Leading-Edge Nodes and Long-Term Financial Discipline</h3>
<p>Advanced nodes (7nm and below) account for 77% of wafer revenue, with 3nm up 5pp sequentially, concentrating customers' flagship smartphone and AI chips at TSMC. Management also committed to long-term gross margins above 56% and ROE in the high 20s, backed by over NT$1T in annual free cash flow to fund heavy expansion and dividends.</p>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Surging CapEx</b>: The 2026 capital budget reaches up to $56B; if AI demand softens, depreciation will weigh on gross margin.</li>
<li><b>Concentration in AI & Smartphones</b>: HPC and smartphones together represent 87% of revenue, so slower orders from major customers directly hit sales.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>FX Volatility</b>: Q4 gross margin benefited from a weaker TWD; Q1 guidance assumes 31.6, so TWD appreciation would pose a headwind.</li>
<li><b>Sequential Shipment Decline</b>: Q4 wafer shipments fell 3% QoQ, leaving revenue growth reliant on pricing and product mix.</li>
<li><b>Overseas Fab Dilution</b>: As new overseas fabs ramp into production, long-term gross margin guidance only commits to 56% or above.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Consistent Guidance Outperformance</b>: Q4 revenue, gross margin, and operating margin all topped the upper end of guidance.</li>
<li><b>Ample Liquidity</b>: Cash and marketable securities of NT$3.07T and full-year free cash flow of NT$1T.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Across-the-Board Guidance Outperformance**: USD revenue of $33.73B, 62.3% gross margin, and 54.0% operating margin all topped the upper end of guidance.
- **3nm Acceleration**: 3nm reached 28% (+5pp QoQ), lifting advanced nodes to 77% combined.
- **Full-Year Results**: Revenue NT$3.81T (+31.6%), EPS NT$66.25 (+46%), and free cash flow NT$1T.
- **2026 Outlook**: USD revenue growth near 30%; Q1 gross margin guided at 63–65%.
- **Stepping Up Expansion**: 2026 capital budget of $52–56B, up roughly 30% from 2025.

> 💡 **Bottom Line**: TSMC once again topped guidance across the board in Q4, while its $52–56B capital budget and near-30% growth outlook signal expanding AI demand; investors should watch FX tailwinds and depreciation pressure from capacity expansion.
