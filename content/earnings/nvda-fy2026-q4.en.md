## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Networking up 263% YoY as NVLink lifts rack value</h3>
<p>Data center compute revenue was $51.3B, up 58% YoY, and networking $11.0B, up 263%. The CFO Commentary attributes networking growth to <b>the ramp of NVLink compute fabric in GB200 and GB300 systems, along with growth in Ethernet and InfiniBand platforms</b>. Hyperscalers remain the largest customer group, slightly over half of data center revenue, but this quarter's growth came mainly from other customers.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Gross margin back to 75%; the H20 hit stays in Q1</h3>
<p>Full-year gross margin of 71.1% was dragged down by Q1's $4.5B H20 charge, but Q4 was back at 75.0%, 1.6pp above the prior quarter. The CFO attributed this to <b>the continued Blackwell ramp, with improvements in product mix and cost structure</b>. Q1 FY27 gross margin guidance of 74.9% already includes 0.1pp of stock-based compensation, indicating that Blackwell-generation gross margins can hold around 75%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Inventory doubles in a year, which the company says is deliberate</h3>
<p>Inventory was $21.4B, up 112% YoY, faster than revenue's 65%; based on Q4 cost of revenue, inventory days were about 114 (our estimate). Supply-related commitments were $95.2B. The company explained that <b>it has strategically secured inventory and capacity to meet demand beyond the next several quarters</b>. Receivable days fell from 53 last quarter to 51, so collections have not deteriorated.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Pouring cash into the ecosystem — $25.8B out in Q4</h3>
<p>In Q4 investing activities, the company paid $13.0B to Groq (a non-exclusive licensing agreement) and bought $12.8B of non-marketable equity, while buybacks that quarter were only $3.8B. Non-marketable equity investments rose from $3.4B to $22.3B in a year, and goodwill rose $15.6B; this quarter it also announced an investment in Anthropic and a technology partnership. Q4 non-operating gains of $5.49B included the Intel stake, which is why GAAP EPS of $1.76 was above non-GAAP EPS of $1.62.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: The agentic AI inflection point; Vera Rubin takes the baton</h3>
<p>Jensen Huang said <b>computing demand is growing exponentially and the agentic AI inflection point has arrived; Grace Blackwell with NVLink is today's king of inference, delivering an order-of-magnitude lower cost per token, and Vera Rubin will extend that lead</b>. The Rubin platform consists of six new chips and cuts inference token costs by up to another 10x, with AWS, Google Cloud, Azure and OCI among the first to deploy; Meta also signed a multiyear partnership covering millions of Blackwell and Rubin GPUs.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Moat and Q1 FY27 Guidance Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: full-rack solutions of GPU + networking + software</h3>
<p>NVIDIA sells entire racks: GPUs, Grace CPUs, NVLink and InfiniBand/Ethernet, plus CUDA-X software. Full-year networking revenue was 2.4x last year's, showing that customers are buying complete AI factories, not just chips; and $95.2B of supply commitments lock up upstream capacity in advance.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>China export controls</b>: Q1 guidance excludes China data center compute revenue entirely, and if policy expands to other regions, the impact is hard to estimate.</li>
<li><b>Inventory and commitments</b>: Inventory doubled in a year to $21.4B and supply commitments are $95.2B, which would face write-downs if AI capex slows.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Investing in customers</b>: Non-marketable equity rose from $3.4B to $22.3B, mostly in AI customers, and valuation swings will affect GAAP profits.</li>
<li><b>Customer concentration</b>: Data center is 91% of revenue and hyperscalers slightly over half of that, so a few customers' capex determines growth.</li>
<li><b>Gaming supply constraints</b>: Gaming revenue fell 13% QoQ, and the company expects supply constraints to be a headwind for Gaming from Q1.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Vera Rubin transition</b>: Inference token costs fall by up to another 10x, with the four big clouds deploying first, extending upgrade demand.</li>
<li><b>Cash generation</b>: FY26 free cash flow of $96.6B with a 44.7% margin, enough to invest in the ecosystem and buy back stock at the same time.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Another high-growth quarter**: Revenue of $68.1B (+73%), up 20% QoQ; FY26 full year $215.9B (+65%).
- **Networking stands out**: Networking revenue of $11.0B, up 263% YoY, as NVLink racks increase spend per customer.
- **Gross margin stabilizes**: Q4 gross margin of 75.0%; the full year's 71.1% was only dragged down by Q1's H20 charge.
- **Bigger inventory and investments**: Inventory doubled and supply commitments reached $95.2B; in Q4 the company paid Groq $13.0B and bought $12.8B of non-marketable equity.
- **Q1 guidance of $78B**: Up about 77% YoY and excluding China; non-GAAP now includes stock-based compensation.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: FY26 proved that Blackwell can deliver 65% growth and a 75% gross margin at the same time; but NVIDIA has started heavy stocking, reserving capacity and investing in customers, so from here investors need to watch both revenue and the balance sheet.
