## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: ARR tops $5.25B, growth accelerates to 24%</h3>
<p>CEO George Kurtz said <b>FY26 will go down as the best year in CrowdStrike's history</b>; ending ARR was $5.25B, making it the fastest and only pure-play cybersecurity software company to reach that scale, and full-year net new ARR was $1.01B, topping $1B for the first time. Q4 net new ARR was $331M, up 47% YoY, and ARR growth accelerated to 24%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Falcon Flex up more than 120% YoY</h3>
<p>Ending ARR from Falcon Flex accounts reached $1.69B, up more than 120% YoY, now about one-third of total ARR (our estimate). Customers using 6 or more modules make up 50% and those using 8 or more 24%, as platformization drives cross-selling. Kurtz said that as enterprises rapidly adopt AI, CrowdStrike is <b>the critical infrastructure securing every layer of AI, from GPUs to agents to prompts</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: GAAP profit for the quarter, loss for the year</h3>
<p>Q4 GAAP operating loss narrowed to $6.9M, GAAP net income was $38.7M — positive for the first time — and non-GAAP operating income was $326M, up 45% YoY. But the full-year GAAP net loss was $163M, wider than last year, mainly because of $1.1B in stock-based compensation, up 27% YoY. The gap between non-GAAP and GAAP remains key to assessing true profitability.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Record cash flow, deferred revenue up 27%</h3>
<p>Q4 operating cash flow was $498M and free cash flow $376M; for the full year they were $1.61B and $1.24B, both records. Deferred revenue rose 27% YoY to $4.75B, faster than revenue growth. With $5.23B in cash and only $750M of long-term debt, the balance sheet is healthy. Podbere said the combination of accelerating growth, expanding profitability and record cash flow puts the company <b>in rare territory</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: FY27 ARR guidance raised again, targeting $20B by FY36</h3>
<p>Podbere said the company entered FY27 with a record Q1 pipeline and therefore has <b>strong confidence in raising its FY27 ARR outlook again</b>. FY27 guidance calls for ending ARR of $6.47–6.52B, revenue of $5.87–5.93B and non-GAAP EPS of $4.78–4.90; the long-term target is ending ARR of $20B in FY36. The company also acquired SGNL and Seraphic Security this quarter.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and FY27 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: the single-agent Falcon platform</h3>
<p>CrowdStrike integrates endpoint, identity, cloud and AI security modules with a single agent and cloud architecture, so the marginal cost for customers to add modules is low; half of customers already use 6 or more modules. On the Microsoft marketplace, Falcon can be bought with Azure commitments, widening sales channels. ARR growth has accelerated even after the July 2024 outage, showing high customer stickiness.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **ARR accelerating**: Ending ARR of $5.25B, up 24% YoY; Q4 net new ARR hit a record $331M, and the full year topped $1B for the first time.
- **Platformization**: Falcon Flex ARR of $1.69B, up more than 120% YoY; half of customers use 6 or more modules.
- **GAAP profit**: Q4 GAAP net income of $38.7M was positive for the first time, but $1.1B of full-year stock-based compensation kept the year in a GAAP loss.
- **Strong cash flow**: Full-year free cash flow of $1.24B, deferred revenue up 27% YoY.
- **Guidance raised**: FY27 ARR of $6.47–6.52B and revenue of $5.87–5.93B; long-term target of $20B ARR in FY36.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: With ARR growth accelerating to 24% and GAAP net income turning positive for the first time, CrowdStrike has put the outage behind it; but a non-GAAP P/E above 100x and $1.1B in stock-based compensation make the valuation highly dependent on continued growth.
