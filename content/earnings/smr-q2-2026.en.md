## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue nearly zero</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue was only $75K, versus $8.05M a year earlier. The press release says last year's revenue came mainly from Fluor's Phase 2 front-end engineering and design work on Romania's RoPower project, completed at the end of 2025, with no similar work this year. NuScale's revenue will recover only once commercial plant contracts are signed.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: TVA power purchase agreement still under negotiation</h3>
<p>Exclusive global partner ENTRA1 continues to negotiate a definitive power purchase agreement with TVA, which the press release says could be <b>the largest nuclear deployment program in US history</b>. Romania's RoPower project plans 6 modules at a former coal plant and is working to meet the conditions attached to a Nuclearelectrica shareholder resolution.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: CEO stresses near-term deployability</h3>
<p>President and CEO John Hopkins said the question for power buyers is no longer whether to use nuclear <b>but which technology can actually deliver, and when</b>. He stressed that NuScale holds the industry's only NRC design certification, uses fuel that is available today, and has a supply chain of more than 60 partners with more than 30 agreements signed.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Higher R&D and G&A</h3>
<p>R&D expense rose $6.6M YoY to $18.4M, mainly to raise the technology readiness of module components; G&A rose $4.4M YoY on higher headcount and organizational costs. The operating loss widened to $64M, partly offset by an $8.5M increase in investment income.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Ample funds to last many years</h3>
<p>Cash and investments at quarter-end were $1.89B with no debt. At the estimated Q2 operating cash flow of about -$58M, that would fund many years of operations (our estimate). But Q1 saw a large $315M cash outflow, and if future commercial projects require NuScale to put in capital, cash burn could speed up again.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: the only NRC design certification</h3>
<p>NuScale is the only SMR with US NRC design certification, and the CEO says its technology uses proven fuel that is available today. That gives it an edge in deploying fastest; its weakness is that it still has no definitive commercial plant contract, and revenue is nearly zero.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue near zero**: Q2 revenue of $75K, with no new engineering revenue after the Romania front-end work ended.
- **Contracts pending**: ENTRA1 and TVA are still negotiating a PPA, and RoPower awaits fulfillment of shareholder-meeting conditions.
- **Regulatory edge**: the only NRC design certification, more than 60 supply chain partners and progress on HIPS design.
- **Wider losses**: operating loss of $64M, with higher R&D and G&A.
- **Ample funds**: $1.89B of cash and investments with no debt, and Q2 operating cash flow of about -$58M.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: NuScale holds the industry's only NRC design certification and $1.89B of cash, but revenue is nearly zero and neither TVA nor Romania is finalized, so the stock depends entirely on when it lands its first definitive contract.
