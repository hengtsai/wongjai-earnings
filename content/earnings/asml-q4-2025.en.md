## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Record €13.2B Q4 Bookings as AI Demand Strengthens</h3>
<p>Q4 net bookings surged 86% YoY to €13.16B, including €7.4B in EUV, lifting ending backlog to €38.8B. CEO Christophe Fouquet noted that over recent months many customers have grown <b>significantly more positive on the medium-term market outlook, largely on stronger conviction in the sustainability of AI demand</b>, driving marked upward revisions to medium-term capacity plans that showed up in record orders.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Record Q4 Revenue Includes Two High NA Systems</h3>
<p>Q4 revenue jumped 29% QoQ to a record €9.72B, which Fouquet highlighted included <b>revenue recognition for two High NA systems</b>, while gross margin of 52.2% matched guidance. Full-year revenue rose 16% to €32.67B, with gross margin (52.8%) and net income (€9.61B) both setting records. New system shipments totaled 300 units, 80 fewer than in 2024 but at higher ASPs.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Further Growth in 2026 Driven by EUV</h3>
<p>ASML guided 2026 revenue to €34–39B (+12% YoY at the midpoint) and gross margin to 51–53%, with Fouquet attributing the growth <b>primarily to a significant increase in EUV sales</b> alongside installed base business growth. Q1 guidance calls for €8.2–8.9B in revenue, 51–53% gross margin, ~€1.2B in R&D, and ~€300M in SG&A as ASML keeps investing in headcount and capacity.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: New €12B Buyback Program and 17% Dividend Hike</h3>
<p>ASML completed its 2022–2025 buyback program in December with €7.6B repurchased (€1.7B in Q4), and launched a new up-to-€12B buyback running through year-end 2028, with up to 2M shares allocated to employee plans and the rest cancelled. The 2025 total dividend rose 17% YoY to €7.50 per share, with a final dividend proposal of €2.70.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Streamlining Technology and IT Organizations</h3>
<p>ASML announced plans to streamline its Technology and IT organizations to <b>sharpen its focus on engineering and innovation</b> ahead of major semiconductor ecosystem growth in the coming years, with details to follow. Year-end FTE headcount stood at 43,520, up roughly 730 YoY. Q4 R&D expenses rose 13% YoY, trimming operating margin from 36.2% to 35.3%.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Moat and 2026 Guidance Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: Sole Supplier of EUV and High NA Lithography</h3>
<p>ASML is the world's sole volume producer of EUV lithography tools, with High NA now recognizing revenue and essential for leading-edge logic and memory fabs alike. Its large installed base generates over €8B annually in service and upgrade revenue, while the €38.8B backlog provides high visibility and customer prepayments keep working capital light.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Export Controls</b>: Dutch and US restrictions on EUV and advanced DUV shipments mean shifts in Chinese demand directly affect revenue.</li>
<li><b>Customer CapEx Cycles</b>: Bookings rely on expansion plans of a concentrated group of logic and memory fabs; softer AI demand would hit directly.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>

<li><b>Q1 Sequential Dip</b>: Q1 revenue guidance of €8.2–8.9B steps down from Q4's €9.7B.</li>
<li><b>Organizational Restructuring</b>: Streamlining Technology and IT teams may introduce near-term execution risk and charges.</li>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Record bookings**: Q4 net bookings hit €13.16B (€7.4B EUV), ending backlog at €38.8B.
- **Record revenue**: Q4 revenue reached €9.72B including two High NA tools; full-year hit €32.67B (+16%).
- **Earnings growth**: Full-year net income rose 27% to €9.61B and EPS grew 28% to €24.71.
- **Further 2026 growth**: Revenue guided at €34–39B with 51–53% gross margin, powered by EUV.
- **Substantial capital returns**: New €12B buyback and +17% YoY dividend.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Record Q4 bookings confirm AI is prompting customers to step up leading-edge capacity investments, setting up another EUV-driven growth year in 2026 backed by a new €12B buyback.
