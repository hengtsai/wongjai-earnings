## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Gross margin beats by 1.2pp, and this time it isn't currency</h3>
<p>Gross margin of 66.2% was 1.2pp above the 65% top of guidance, and operating margin of 58.1% was 2.1pp above the 56% top. The Q1 average exchange rate of 31.59 was almost identical to the 31.6 guidance assumption, so the beat came mainly from operations. Revenue rose 8.4% QoQ, wafer shipments rose 5.4% QoQ, and the operating expense ratio fell to 8.3%. CFO Wendell Huang said <b>first-quarter business was supported by strong demand for its leading-edge process technologies</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: HPC up 20% QoQ, share jumps from 55% to 61%</h3>
<p>HPC revenue rose 20% QoQ, lifting its share 6pp in one quarter to 61%; smartphones fell 11% QoQ, with their share down to 26%. In the traditional smartphone off-season, HPC growth still drove total revenue up 8% QoQ, making AI and data centers the main revenue engine (our inference). Consumer electronics rose 28% QoQ and IoT 12%, but together they are only 7%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: 3nm share slips to 25%</h3>
<p>3nm's share of wafer revenue fell from 28% to 25%, 5nm rose to 36%, and 7nm and below fell from 77% to 74% in total. Flagship phone chips mostly use 3nm, so the smartphone off-season lowered 3nm's share (our inference), while 5nm's rise fits AI accelerator demand. Even as the advanced-node share slipped, gross margin hit a record.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Full-year outlook raised to over 30%</h3>
<p>The company raised its 2026 U.S. dollar revenue growth outlook from January's "close to 30%" to "over 30%." Q2 guidance calls for U.S. dollar revenue of $39.0–40.2B, up 9–12% QoQ and 30–34% YoY, gross margin of 65.5–67.5% and operating margin of 56.5–58.5%, assuming an exchange rate of 31.7. Huang said that <b>moving into the second quarter, business is expected to continue to be supported by strong demand for its leading-edge process technologies</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Receivables up 29% QoQ, cash conversion slows</h3>
<p>Accounts receivable rose from NT$281.8B to NT$363B, up 29% QoQ and faster than revenue's 8%; inventory rose 8%, with days of inventory up to 80. Operating cash flow of NT$699B was 4% below Q4, and free cash flow of NT$348.2B was also below Q4's NT$368.6B. Days sales outstanding are still only 26, so there is no sign of a collection problem for now, but it is worth tracking next quarter.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>HPC concentration</b>: HPC is 61%, so a slowdown in AI customers' capex would directly hit revenue and gross margin.</li>
<li><b>Capex burden</b>: The 2026 capital budget of $52–56B announced in January means heavy depreciation pressure if demand weakens.</li>
</ul></div>

<li><b>Weaker smartphones</b>: Smartphones down 11% QoQ, 3nm share down to 25%.</li>
<li><b>Currency</b>: Q2 guidance assumes 31.7; a stronger NT dollar would squeeze gross margin.</li>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Beat across the board**: U.S. dollar revenue of $35.90B, gross margin of 66.2% and operating margin of 58.1%, all above the top of guidance.
- **Currency-neutral**: The 31.59 average rate was almost identical to the assumption, so the margin beat came from operations.
- **HPC takes over**: HPC up 20% QoQ to 61%, smartphones down 11% QoQ.
- **Outlook raised**: Full-year U.S. dollar revenue growth raised to over 30%, with Q2 up 9–12% QoQ.
- **Working capital slowing**: Receivables up 29% QoQ, 80 days of inventory, and free cash flow down 6% QoQ.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: TSMC relied on HPC to deliver 8% QoQ growth in the smartphone off-season and pushed gross margin to 66.2% with neutral currency; the full-year outlook was raised to over 30%, and what to watch is receivables and inventory rising together.
