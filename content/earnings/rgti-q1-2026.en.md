## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue Triples on On-Premises Sales</h3>
<p>Q1 revenue was $4.4M, up 199% YoY and 136% QoQ. CEO Subodh Kulkarni attributed the growth to <b>new on-premises Novera quantum processor sales</b>; this quarter Rigetti completed delivery to Canada's University of Saskatchewan as the core of the university's first quantum computer. The base is tiny, so a single hardware delivery can swing revenue sharply.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: 108-Qubit System Fully Launched</h3>
<p>The Cepheus-1-108Q is built from twelve 9-qubit chiplets and is live on Rigetti QCS, Amazon Braket, Microsoft Azure Quantum, and qBraid. Kulkarni called it <b>one of the world's most powerful universal gate-based quantum computers</b>, and it validates the chiplet architecture for production use.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Fidelity Keeps Improving</h3>
<p>Using its own adiabatic CZ gate scheme, the company achieved a median two-qubit fidelity of 99.8% with a 40 ns gate time on a 9-qubit system, and up to 99.9% on a prototype system. The company said this increases confidence that high fidelity can be maintained as systems scale. However, the press release did not disclose the two-qubit fidelity of the 108-qubit system itself.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Up to $100M Investment in the UK</h3>
<p>Rigetti announced up to $100M of investment in the UK, aiming to deploy a system of more than 1,000 qubits there within three to four years in support of the UK government's quantum infrastructure program. It is the first time the company has tied a thousand-qubit timeline to a specific investment amount, and it will raise capex over the next few years (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: GAAP Profit Is Only on Paper</h3>
<p>GAAP net income of $33.1M this quarter came entirely from a $53.7M warrant fair-value gain; the operating loss actually widened 20% to $26M, and the Non-GAAP net loss was $14.7M. R&D rose 29% YoY. Kulkarni stressed the company's <b>strong balance sheet and disciplined capital allocation</b>, with $569M in cash and investments and no debt.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Full-Year Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Chiplet architecture + in-house fab</h3>
<p>Rigetti designs and manufactures quantum chips in its own Fab-1 and pioneered multi-chip quantum processors, scaling by tiling 9-qubit chiplets; its superconducting qubit gate speeds are about 50–70 ns, which the company says is roughly a thousand times faster than trapped-ion and neutral-atom approaches. The weaknesses: fidelity still trails some rivals, revenue is tiny, and there is no stable commercial demand yet.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue jump**: Q1 revenue of $4.4M (+199%), from Novera on-premises processor sales.
- **Product launch**: The 108-qubit Cepheus-1-108Q is live on Rigetti's own cloud and three major cloud platforms.
- **Technical progress**: 99.8% fidelity and 40 ns gate time on the 9-qubit system, up to 99.9% on a prototype.
- **Paper profit**: A $53.7M warrant fair-value gain produced GAAP net income of $33.1M, while the operating loss widened to $26M.
- **Ample funding**: $569M in cash and investments and no debt; announced up to $100M of UK investment.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Rigetti's Q1 revenue tripled and its 108-qubit system went live, but revenue is still only a few million dollars and GAAP net income came from a fair-value gain; its $570M of cash can last many years, while a P/S above 600x already prices in long-term success.
