## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue within guidance, gross margin at the top</h3>
<p>Q1 revenue of €8.77B was within the €8.2–8.9B guidance; gross margin of 53.0% hit the top of the 51–53% guidance, 1pp below a year ago and 0.8pp above Q4. Operating margin of 36.0% was the highest in five quarters, with R&D up only 2% YoY. CEO Christophe Fouquet said <b>the semiconductor industry's growth outlook remains solid, driven by AI-related infrastructure investment, with chip demand exceeding supply</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Customers accelerate expansion, low end of full-year guidance up €2B</h3>
<p>Full-year revenue guidance was raised from €34–39B to €36–40B, lifting the midpoint by €1.5B and implying about 10–22% YoY growth; gross margin guidance was held at 51–53%. Fouquet said customers, backed by long-term agreements, are accelerating capacity expansion for 2026 and beyond, <b>raising their short- and medium-term demand expectations for ASML products over the past few months</b>, with bookings <b>remaining very strong</b>. He also said the guidance range <b>covers the possible outcomes of export control discussions</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Installed base management up 24% YoY on upgrade demand</h3>
<p>Installed base management (services plus field upgrades) revenue was €2.49B, up 24% YoY and 17% QoQ, or 28% of revenue; Q2 guidance of about €2.5B stays high. Fouquet said the company serves customers in two ways: <b>shipping new systems and upgrading the performance of installed tools</b>. The presentation lists the PEP1-E productivity package for the NXE:3800E, raising throughput from 220 to 230 wafers per hour, so customers can add capacity without waiting for new tools.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Operating cash flow turns to -€2.19B, cash down €4.9B in a quarter</h3>
<p>Customer prepayments drove record operating cash flow of €11.4B in Q4, which reversed to -€2.19B in Q1: receivables rose €1.38B and current liabilities fell by about €4B. Add €620M of dividends, €1B of buybacks and €690M of debt repayment, and cash and short-term investments fell from €13.3B to €8.4B. This is a common seasonal pattern for ASML (Q1 last year was also negative), but the amount is larger than last year, so watch whether prepayments are replenished by new orders.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Dividend up 17%, buybacks continue</h3>
<p>The 2025 dividend is €7.50, up 17% YoY; after three interim dividends of €1.60 each, the proposed final dividend is €2.70. In Q1, under the 2026–2028 program, ASML bought back about 900K shares for about €1.1B. Diluted shares fell 1.7% YoY, lifting EPS 19% YoY, faster than net income's 17%.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q2 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Sole EUV supplier plus a huge installed base</h3>
<p>ASML is the world's only volume maker of EUV lithography tools, which advanced logic and memory customers need to expand capacity. About €2.5B a quarter of installed base management revenue comes from servicing and upgrading existing tools, so revenue does not depend solely on new tool shipments. The press release said <b>all businesses are expected to grow again</b> in 2026.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Export controls</b>: The company explicitly said the guidance range must cover the outcome of export control discussions, leaving China-related revenue uncertain.</li>
<li><b>Customer capex cycle</b>: Growth depends on capacity expansion at a handful of foundries and memory makers, so an AI investment slowdown would hit orders directly.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Volatile cash flow</b>: Q1 operating cash flow of -€2.19B and cash down €4.9B in a quarter, relying on prepayments being replenished.</li>
<li><b>Lower Q2 gross margin</b>: Q2 guidance of 51–52% is below Q1's 53.0%.</li>
<li><b>Bookings not disclosed</b>: The press release did not disclose bookings, making demand changes harder to track.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Guidance raised</b>: Full-year revenue guidance raised to €36–40B as customers lift short- and medium-term demand expectations.</li>
<li><b>Service revenue</b>: Installed base management up 24% YoY, with upgrade packages keeping existing tools contributing revenue.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue in line, gross margin at the top**: Revenue €8.77B (+13%), gross margin 53.0%, operating margin 36.0%.
- **Full-year guidance raised**: Revenue €36–40B with gross margin unchanged at 51–53%, as customers lift short- and medium-term demand.
- **Fast service growth**: Installed base management €2.49B, up 24% YoY, with upgrade packages boosting existing tool capacity.
- **Cash flow turns negative**: Operating cash flow -€2.19B, with cash and short-term investments down from €13.3B to €8.4B.
- **Shareholder returns**: Dividend up 17% YoY, about €1.1B bought back in the quarter, EPS up 19% YoY.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: ASML's profit was solid this quarter and full-year guidance was raised, so demand is not the problem; what to watch is lower Q2 gross margin guidance, cash flow as prepayments are worked down, and the export control variable the company itself flagged.
