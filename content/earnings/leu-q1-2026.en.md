## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue Edges Up as SWU Volume Falls and Price Rises</h3>
<p>Q1 revenue was $76.7M, up 5% YoY. SWU revenue fell $9.7M as volume dropped 47% while the average selling price rose 52%; technical solutions revenue rose 47% YoY, mainly from the DOE HALEU operations contract. Most LEU customers buy under annual purchase commitments, so quarterly revenue swings with delivery timing.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Expansion R&D Weighs on Profit</h3>
<p>Advanced technology costs rose from $3M to $18.9M, leaving operating income of just $0.8M; GAAP net income of $10M was propped up by $17M of investment income. CEO Amir Vexler said the company has <b>shifted into full execution mode</b>, speeding up its expansion and building a top-tier partner network.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Palantir Helps Find $300M in Cost Savings</h3>
<p>Centrus is working with Palantir to roll out an AI platform, which has initially identified about $300M in potential cost savings and may shorten manufacturing lead times and speed up the expansion schedule; it also signed an engineering and procurement agreement with Fluor and selected Geiger Brothers. The company also began a multi-year investment in centrifuge manufacturing at Oak Ridge.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Exploring a HALEU Joint Venture with Oklo</h3>
<p>Centrus is exploring a joint venture with Oklo to provide HALEU deconversion services. Vexler said the quarter <b>brought historic federal funding</b> and the start of a major expansion of its centrifuge manufacturing plant; he also noted that global conflicts highlight the need to diversify energy sources and develop domestic power.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Full-Year Revenue Guidance Raised</h3>
<p>Full-year revenue guidance was raised from $425–475M to $450–500M, while capital investment stays at $350–500M; the Piketon hiring target was raised from 50 to 100 people. Contingent orders rose to $2.4B, all under definitive contracts, and total backlog is $3.9B.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue edges up**: Q1 revenue of $76.7M (+5%), with lower SWU volume at higher prices and technical solutions +47%.
- **Profit falls**: advanced technology costs rose to $18.9M, and GAAP net income fell 63% YoY.
- **Partner network**: Palantir helped identify about $300M in cost savings, and Fluor and Geiger Brothers joined the expansion.
- **Bigger backlog**: backlog of $3.9B, with $2.4B of contingent orders all under definitive contracts.
- **Guidance raised**: full-year revenue of $450–500M, and the Piketon hiring target doubled.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Centrus's core Q1 profit fell sharply because of expansion R&D, but it is speeding up its build-out with partners such as Palantir and Fluor and raised full-year revenue guidance; the market answered the expansion progress with a 12% one-day gain.
