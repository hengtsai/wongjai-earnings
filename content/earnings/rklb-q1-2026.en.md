## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Record Revenue and Gross Margin</h3>
<p>Revenue was $200M, up 63.5% YoY, and GAAP gross margin hit a record 38.2%, both above the top of guidance. CEO Peter Beck said the quarter <b>delivered record financial results with revenue above $200M</b>, with steady execution across launch and space systems programs. Launch services revenue grew 79% YoY, faster than the 57% growth in Space Systems.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: More Launches Sold in Q1 Than in All of 2025</h3>
<p>The company signed 31 Electron and HASTE contracts in the quarter, plus 5 dedicated Neutron launches. It said launches sold in Q1 already exceeded all of 2025, with more than 70 launches under contract. Backlog was $2.22B, up 20% QoQ, with about 36% expected to be recognized within 12 months (10-Q). HASTE also won a record contract under the MACH-TB program.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Entering Golden Dome Space-Based Interception</h3>
<p>Rocket Lab was selected to work with Raytheon and others in direct support of the Department of Defense's Space-Based Interceptor (SBI) program, part of the Golden Dome for America missile defense effort, drawing on both its launch and satellite capabilities. The company said this could become one of the US government's most important national security programs and give it a chance at meaningful revenue, but the press release did not disclose contract value or timing.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Acquisitions Fill Supply-Chain Gaps</h3>
<p>On 4/14 it closed the acquisition of German laser communications terminal maker Mynaric for about $155M (10-Q), forming Rocket Lab Europe; on 5/6 it agreed to buy space robotics and precision mechanisms maker Motiv, bringing expensive, supply-constrained parts such as solar array drive assemblies in-house. Beck said the company has more than $2B in liquidity, enough to <b>keep growing and pursue acquisitions</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Neutron's First Flight Still Set for This Year</h3>
<p>Integration of Neutron's first-flight hardware continued, Archimedes engine qualification testing advanced, and the second stage and reusable fairing also made progress; the company said the first flight remains on track for later this year. R&D expense was $80.5M, up 46% YoY, mainly from Neutron. Q2 revenue guidance is $225–240M, but GAAP gross margin is guided down to 33–35%.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q2 Guidance

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: Vertical Integration of Launch + Satellites</h3>
<p>Electron is the world's most frequently launched small orbital rocket, HASTE provides hypersonic testing, and the Space Systems business's satellites and components have supported more than 1,700 missions. Making launch, satellites and key components in-house lets Rocket Lab take on end-to-end defense and constellation missions. The weak spot: Neutron has not flown yet, and in medium-lift launch it still has to face SpaceX.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Twin records**: Q1 revenue of $200M (+63.5%) and GAAP gross margin of 38.2%, both above the top of guidance.
- **Strong bookings**: 31 Electron/HASTE contracts plus 5 Neutron launches; backlog of $2.22B, up 20% QoQ.
- **Defense push**: selected to support the Golden Dome space-based interceptor program and won a record MACH-TB contract.
- **Expansion by acquisition**: closed the Mynaric deal, agreed to buy Motiv, and raised about $450M through the ATM.
- **Guidance keeps rising**: Q2 revenue of $225–240M, but GAAP gross margin falls to 33–35% and the EBITDA loss widens.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Rocket Lab set records in Q1 revenue, gross margin and bookings, and is speeding up its defense and acquisition push; after the 34% jump the next day the stock trades at over 90x sales, so the next test is whether Neutron flies on schedule.
