## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue up 62% YoY</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue was NT$895.44B, up 62.4% YoY and 5.8% QoQ; first-half revenue was NT$1.74T, up 94% YoY. Revenue is scaling fast, but the financial report does not break out products, so the sources of growth must be read alongside the company's investor conference materials (not available to us).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Operating income roughly triples YoY</h3>
<p>Gross margin rose from 4.44% to 5.66% and the operating expense ratio fell from 2.48% to 1.92%, so operating income reached NT$33.45B, up 210% YoY, for a 3.74% operating margin. Improvement on both gross margin and expenses let operating income grow far faster than revenue.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Non-operating items turn negative, minority interests take nearly 40%</h3>
<p>Non-operating items swung from a NT$7.36B gain a year earlier to a NT$2.36B loss, possibly due to higher interest on much larger borrowings (our inference; the summary financials do not break it out). Of net income of NT$23.74B, non-controlling interests took NT$8.92B, leaving net income attributable to the parent of NT$14.83B and EPS of NT$4.72, up 115% YoY.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Working capital balloons, cash flow deeply negative</h3>
<p>Receivables rose 36% in six months to NT$574.1B and inventory rose 50% to NT$532.9B, so first-half operating cash flow was -NT$251.4B, worse than -NT$138.1B a year earlier. Short-term borrowings rose from NT$235.2B to NT$425.4B, and long-term borrowings also rose to NT$58.2B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Cash capital increase via global depositary receipts approved</h3>
<p>On 8/4, alongside approving Q2 results, the board approved a cash capital increase issuing common shares for global depositary receipts (GDR) and a capital injection into its Vietnam subsidiary. With huge working capital needs and fast-rising borrowings, the raise helps strengthen the capital structure but dilutes shareholders.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: large-scale assembly and supply chain management</h3>
<p>Wistron is one of the world's major electronics contract manufacturers, with large-scale assembly, testing and global supply chain management capabilities. Contract manufacturing has low gross margins, and competitiveness comes from scale and delivery; when revenue scales quickly, working capital and financing capacity become the key constraints.</p>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue growth**: Q2 revenue of NT$895.4B (+62%), and NT$1.74T in the first half, up 94% YoY.
- **Profit surge**: operating income of NT$33.45B (+210%) and EPS of NT$4.72 (+115%).
- **Cash flow deteriorates**: receivables and inventory surged, and first-half operating cash flow was -NT$251.4B.
- **Borrowings climb**: short-term borrowings rose to NT$425.4B, and borrowings plus bonds are about 2.7 times equity.
- **Capital raise**: the board approved a cash capital increase via GDR, strengthening capital but diluting shareholders.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Wistron's Q2 revenue and operating income both surged, with clear operating leverage; but expanding shipments consumed huge amounts of working capital, cash flow is deeply negative and borrowings have doubled, making the GDR equity raise a necessary capital top-up.
