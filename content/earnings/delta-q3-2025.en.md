## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue Up 21% QoQ</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue reached NT$150.32B (+34% YoY), up 21% from Q2's NT$124.04B, bringing nine-month revenue to NT$393.27B (+28% YoY). Growth clearly accelerated, though the financial statements do not break out products, so growth drivers require company investor conference materials (not obtained here). Accounts receivable rose 30% from the start of the year, in line with revenue growth.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Operating Leverage Lifts Operating Margin</h3>
<p>Operating income rose 51% YoY to NT$24.81B, lifting operating margin from 14.6% to 16.5%. Gross margin of 34.87% was flat YoY and 0.6pp lower than Q2, so margin expansion came entirely from the operating expense ratio falling from 20.3% to 18.4% as operating expenses rose just 21% against 34% revenue growth. R&D was NT$12.78B, up 18% YoY.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: EPS NT$7.16, Up 51% YoY</h3>
<p>Net income attributable to parent rose 51% YoY to NT$18.61B, with EPS at NT$7.16—up 33% from Q2's NT$5.37 and above every quarter of 2024 (our estimate). Nine-month EPS of NT$16.47 already exceeds full-year 2024's NT$13.56. Non-operating income of NT$2.17B exceeded last year's NT$1.25B, though core operations remained the main growth driver.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Solid Cash Flow, Capacity Expansion Accelerates</h3>
<p>Nine-month operating cash flow rose 18% YoY to NT$60.32B and capex jumped 40% YoY to NT$29.52B, leaving free cash flow around NT$30.79B, roughly flat YoY. Borrowings plus corporate bonds totaled NT$68.28B, leaving net cash of roughly NT$70.1B after cash. Capex outpacing operating cash flow indicates accelerating capacity expansion (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Muted Stock Reaction</h3>
<p>Shares fell 4.7% from NT$1,060 to NT$1,010 on the first day post-earnings. Despite strong revenue and EPS growth, the pullback likely reflects sequential gross margin compression and prior share price gains (our inference). At roughly 52.5x TTM EPS of NT$19.23, valuation already prices in high growth, leaving the stock sensitive to gross margin swings.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: power efficiency and scale</h3>
<p>Delta Electronics is the global leader in power supplies, backed by decades of expertise in high-efficiency power and thermal management. Scale lowers its expense ratio as revenue grows, while R&D above 8% of revenue forms a competitive barrier. The weakness is limited disclosure—without a product breakdown, outside investors cannot verify AI-related revenue mix, profitability, or gross margin structure from official filings.</p>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue accelerating**: Q3 revenue reached NT$150.32B (+34% YoY, +21% QoQ).
- **Operating leverage**: Operating margin of 16.5% (+1.9pp) came entirely from a lower expense ratio.
- **EPS surge**: EPS of NT$7.16 (+51% YoY); 9M EPS of NT$16.47 already tops full-year 2024.
- **Flat gross margin**: Gross margin of 34.9% was 0.6pp below Q2.
- **Solid cash position**: Nine-month free cash flow of ~NT$30.8B and net cash of ~NT$70.1B.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Delta delivered accelerating Q3 revenue growth and 51% YoY EPS growth as operating leverage lifted operating margin; however, flat gross margin and a post-earnings pullback show that at 52x P/E the market wants higher earnings quality.
