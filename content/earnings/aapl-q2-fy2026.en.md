## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: iPhone 17 Drives a March-Quarter Record, Greater China Up 28%</h3>
<p>iPhone revenue was $57.0B, up 22% YoY, or 51% of total revenue. Cook said <b>iPhone set a March-quarter revenue record on very strong demand for the iPhone 17 lineup</b>, and stressed double-digit growth in all five regions. Greater China was $20.5B, up 28%, the fastest-growing region; Rest of Asia Pacific rose 25%, Europe and Japan 15% each, and the Americas 12%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Gross Margin Rises to 49.3%, Products Margin Up 2.9pp</h3>
<p>Overall gross margin rose from 47.1% to 49.3%. Products gross margin rose from 35.9% to 38.7%, and Services from 75.7% to 76.7%. Services were 27.9% of revenue but contributed 43% of gross profit ($23.75B ÷ $54.78B). Products and Services revenue grew at almost the same pace (17% and 16%), so the margin gain came mainly from products.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: R&D Up 34%, Twice the Pace of Revenue</h3>
<p>R&D expense was $11.4B, up 34% YoY and 10.3% of revenue (9.0% last year); SG&A rose only 11%. Total operating expenses were $18.9B, up 24%, faster than revenue's 17%, but gross margin expansion still lifted operating margin from 31.0% to 32.3%. The press release did not break down R&D spending; over the same period net intangible assets rose from $11.1B to $21.3B, which the press release did not explain.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Record March-Quarter Cash Flow, Another $100B Buyback Authorization</h3>
<p>Parekh said <b>the March quarter generated more than $28B in operating cash flow, with both operating cash flow and EPS setting March-quarter records</b>. The board authorized another $100B in buybacks and raised the quarterly dividend 4% to $0.27. But actual Q2 buybacks were $12.3B, less than half the $25.9B a year ago; net cash rose from $33.8B in September to $61.9B, and debt fell $13.9B in six months.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Services Hit Another Record, New Products Widen the Installed Base</h3>
<p>Services revenue was $31.0B, up 16% YoY, an all-time high. Parekh said <b>strong demand pushed the active installed base to new highs across every major product category and region</b> (the press release gave no figures). This quarter Apple launched the iPhone 17e, M4 iPad Air and MacBook Neo; Mac and iPad grew 6% and 8%, and Wearables, Home and Accessories returned to growth at 5%.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Heavy reliance on iPhone</b>: iPhone is 51% of revenue, so a weaker upgrade cycle would directly drag on overall growth.</li>
<li><b>Greater China volatility</b>: this quarter's +28% was a main engine, but local competition and policy are big variables, and the comparison base will rise.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>R&D surge</b>: up 34% YoY, twice revenue growth; without matching new revenue it will squeeze operating margin.</li>
<li><b>Slower buybacks</b>: Q2 buybacks of $12.3B, down 53% YoY, so the EPS boost from a shrinking share count may weaken.</li>
<li><b>Jump in intangibles</b>: up $10.2B in six months with no explanation in the press release; check the 10-Q notes.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Steady Services growth</b>: up 16% YoY at a 76.7% gross margin, contributing over 40% of gross profit.</li>
<li><b>Net cash rebuilding</b>: $61.9B of net cash plus a new $100B authorization give plenty of room for shareholder returns.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **March-quarter records across the board**: revenue of $111.2B (+17%) and EPS of $2.01 (+22%), with double-digit growth in all five regions.
- **iPhone and China as twin engines**: iPhone $57.0B (+22%) and Greater China $20.5B (+28%).
- **Gross margin expansion**: 49.3% overall (+2.2pp), driven mainly by a 2.9pp rise in products margin.
- **R&D accelerating**: R&D of $11.4B (+34%), 10.3% of revenue, growing twice as fast as revenue.
- **Cash rebuilding**: Q2 free cash flow of $26.7B (+28%), net cash of $61.9B, and a new $100B buyback authorization.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Apple's FY26 Q2 delivered a March-quarter record on the back of iPhone 17 and Greater China, with gross margin expanding at the same time; the surge in R&D and slower buybacks are two new changes worth tracking.
