## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue up 48% YoY and 15% QoQ</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue was NT$183.26B, up 47.7% YoY and 15% above Q1's NT$159.35B. First-half revenue was NT$342.61B, up 41% YoY. Revenue grew sharply, but the report does not break out products, so the sources of growth need the company's investor conference materials to interpret (not obtained for this article).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Operating margin rises to 16.7%</h3>
<p>Operating income was NT$30.57B, up 63.8% YoY, with operating margin rising from 15.05% to 16.68%. Gross margin of 35.64% was roughly flat YoY and 1.4pp below Q1; the margin improvement came mainly from the opex ratio falling from 20.5% to 19.0%, as greater scale brought operating leverage.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: EPS of NT$9.68, up 80% YoY</h3>
<p>Net income attributable to the parent was NT$25.14B, up 80% YoY, and EPS NT$9.68, up 22% from NT$7.91 in Q1. Non-operating income of NT$4.43B, versus only NT$900M a year earlier, is why net income grew faster than operating income. First-half EPS of NT$17.59 is already 76% of full-year 2025's NT$23.14.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Inventory and short-term borrowings rise</h3>
<p>Inventory rose from NT$101.5B at the start of the year to NT$139.4B, up 37%, and receivables rose 17%. Short-term borrowings rose from NT$2.4B to NT$16.3B, but corporate bonds and long-term loans fell; total borrowings plus bonds are NT$64.3B, and net of cash the company still has net cash of about NT$118.0B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Solid cash flow, continued investment</h3>
<p>First-half operating cash flow was NT$63.1B, up 45% YoY, capex was NT$27.3B, up 43% YoY, and free cash flow was about NT$35.8B. R&D was 8.6% of revenue and grew 33% YoY in amount, as the company keeps R&D spending high even amid rapid revenue growth.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: power efficiency and scale</h3>
<p>Delta is the global leader in power supplies, with years of accumulated expertise in high-efficiency power and thermal management. Scale lowers its expense ratio as revenue grows, and R&D stays above 8% of revenue, forming a competitive barrier.</p>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue growth**: Q2 revenue of NT$183.26B (+48%, +15% QoQ).
- **Higher margins**: Operating margin of 16.7% (+1.6pp), mainly from a lower expense ratio.
- **EPS surge**: EPS of NT$9.68 (+80%), helped by NT$4.43B of non-operating income.
- **Inventory build**: Inventory up 37% from the start of the year; short-term borrowings up NT$13.9B.
- **Solid cash flow**: First-half free cash flow of about NT$35.8B and net cash of about NT$118.0B.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Delta's Q2 revenue and profit both jumped, and operating leverage lifted its margin; but with inventory climbing, gross margin down QoQ and a rich P/E, the key is whether revenue growth can keep working down inventory.
