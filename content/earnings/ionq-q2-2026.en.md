## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue up 287% YoY, a record for the fifth straight quarter</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue was $80.05M, up 287% YoY and 20% above the midpoint of $65–68M guidance. Chairman and CEO Niccolo de Masi called it <b>the fifth consecutive record quarter and the strongest in the company's history</b>. CFO Inder Singh said growth came from global deployments of Tempo quantum computers and cloud usage.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Warrant revaluation drives a huge GAAP loss</h3>
<p>The GAAP net loss was $1.87B, or $5.08 per share, including a $1.65B warrant valuation loss, a non-cash loss caused by the rising share price; the same line was a $1.06B gain in Q1. Excluding it, the adjusted loss per share was $0.33, wider than $0.08 a year earlier.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Expenses surge, gross margin is low</h3>
<p>The operating loss was $337M, with R&D of $161M, G&A of $118M and D&A of $46.1M all sharply higher; cost of revenue was 75% of revenue, for a gross margin of about 25%, far below roughly 60% a year earlier (our estimate). The adjusted EBITDA loss was $120M, of which $24.7M came from SkyWater partnership costs.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: SkyWater deal closes, a move to vertical integration</h3>
<p>On 7/31 IonQ completed its acquisition of foundry SkyWater and also acquired integrated photonics company Nexus Photonics. De Masi said this makes IonQ <b>the first vertically integrated full-stack quantum platform</b> as it moves toward 256-qubit systems and quantum error correction. This quarter it also signed MOUs with Anduril and Sandia National Laboratories.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Full-year guidance raised to $280–290M</h3>
<p>Full-year revenue guidance was raised from $260–270M to $280–290M, excluding SkyWater, and the company kept its target of 100% organic growth. De Masi also cited the White House executive order on quantum, saying quantum sensing, networking, security and computing have all become <b>national priorities for the United States</b>.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Full-Year Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: trapped-ion technology + a full-stack platform</h3>
<p>IonQ is built around trapped-ion quantum computers and has used acquisitions to expand into quantum networking, quantum security, space optical communications and chip foundry work, forming a rare full-stack platform. About $2B of cash (after the acquisition) lets it keep investing, but most businesses are still in early commercialization.</p>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue explosion**: Q2 revenue of $80.05M (+287%), 20% above the guidance midpoint, a record for the fifth straight quarter.
- **Huge GAAP loss**: a $1.65B warrant valuation loss and a GAAP loss of $5.08 per share.
- **Core cash burn**: operating loss of $337M, adjusted EBITDA of -$120M and a gross margin of about 25%.
- **Vertical integration**: the SkyWater acquisition closed after the quarter, leaving about $2B of cash.
- **Guidance raised**: full-year revenue of $280–290M (excluding SkyWater), 100% organic growth.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: IonQ posted its fifth straight record revenue quarter and raised full-year guidance again, and the SkyWater deal pushes the platform toward vertical integration; but core losses and cash burn are growing, and GAAP results are heavily distorted by warrant revaluation.
