## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Excluding SpaceX and tax benefits, GAAP net income is only about $80M</h3>
<p>Q2 other income was $590M, including a $1.005B unrealized valuation gain on SpaceX equity ($763M after tax); the stake was acquired in Q1 for $2B, disclosed in the Q4 report as an investment in xAI, and became SpaceX equity after xAI merged into SpaceX. Also deducting the $274M one-off tax benefit, the $1.114B of net income attributable to common stockholders shrinks to about $77M, or about $0.02 per share (our estimate). Operating income of $398M, down 57% YoY, is the truest measure of profitability this quarter.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: R&D and compensation expenses up nearly 50% YoY, eating up gross profit growth</h3>
<p>Gross profit rose by $870M YoY (+23%), but operating expenses rose by $1.4B (+47%): R&D $2.37B (+49%) and SG&A $1.98B (+45%). The company attributed the drop in operating income to <b>higher spending on AI and other R&D projects, stock-based compensation (including the 2025 CEO performance award) and SG&A</b> (paraphrased). Quarterly stock-based compensation was $1.15B, 1.8 times last year's; the energy segment also bore warranty costs from a supplier cell issue.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Capex doubles in a quarter as the company enters its "largest investment period"</h3>
<p>Q2 capex was $5.79B, $3.3B more than in Q1; PP&E rose by $6.6B in six months, and free cash flow was -$1.09B. The Update said <b>Tesla is in its largest and most exciting investment period, and growth will not be linear</b> (paraphrased). The money is going into battery materials, Megafactory Texas, Optimus production lines, Cortex 2 training compute (Texas compute capacity in MW more than doubled in 1H) and an Austin semiconductor fab. Cash still stands at $43.5B, down $1.2B QoQ.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Record Q2 deliveries, but battery capacity is the bottleneck</h3>
<p>Deliveries were 480K, up 25% YoY, with records in South Korea, Australia, Japan, Taiwan and other markets, and the Model YL launched in the US in July. But automotive sales grew 27% YoY, below delivery growth, implying lower average prices, and regulatory credit revenue fell to just $146M. The company said plainly that <b>battery pack capacity is the main near-term constraint on higher vehicle output</b> (paraphrased), and it is expanding battery pack capacity in Berlin, cathode and lithium refining in Texas, and LFP cell and 4680 capacity in Nevada.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: FSD and services become new pillars; Robotaxi expands to 7 metro areas</h3>
<p>Active FSD subscriptions reached 1.48M (+56%); in North America, <b>more than 55% of new vehicle deliveries included an FSD subscription</b> (paraphrased), and approvals were obtained in Lithuania, Estonia, Denmark and Belgium in addition to the Netherlands. Services and other gross profit rose $300M QoQ to a record $648M, with a 14% gross margin. Cybercab has entered production at Gigafactory Texas and begun employee rides; unsupervised Robotaxi expanded to Miami, Orlando and Tampa in July.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Record revenue, bottoming profit**: Revenue of $28.2B (+26%) and 480K deliveries, but an operating margin of just 1.4%.
- **Expenses outrun gross profit**: R&D +49%, SG&A +45%, and stock-based compensation of $1.15B is 1.8 times last year's.
- **EPS relies on non-operating items**: A $1B SpaceX valuation gain and $270M in tax benefits propped up EPS of $0.32.
- **Investment phase begins**: Quarterly capex of $5.8B, up $3.3B QoQ, with free cash flow turning negative at -$1.09B.
- **Software and services take the baton**: 1.48M FSD subscriptions (+56%) and record service gross profit of $648M.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Tesla's Q2 car sales recovered and FSD and services grew, but R&D, compensation and capex all surged at once, pushing the core operating margin down to 1.4%; reported EPS was held up mainly by the valuation of the SpaceX stake.
