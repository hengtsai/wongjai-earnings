## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue at 2.4x Last Year</h3>
<p>Q1 revenue was NT$846.30B, up 144% YoY and another 17% above Q4's NT$720.94B; a single quarter now equals nearly 40% of full-year 2025 revenue of NT$2.19T (our estimate). The report does not break out products, but surging revenue with a falling gross margin is consistent with a ramp in AI server rack shipments (our inference). Operating expenses rose only 26%, showing clear economies of scale.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Gross Margin Falls to 5.2%</h3>
<p>Gross margin was 5.21%, 2.6pp below the year-ago 7.81%. Operating income was NT$29.14B, up 92% YoY, but operating margin fell from 4.37% to 3.44%. Gross profit on contract-built systems rises with revenue, but the ratio is diluted by high-priced components (our inference). R&D expenses were NT$7.63B, up 20% YoY, falling to 0.9% of revenue.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Minority Shareholders Take Nearly Half of Net Income</h3>
<p>Net income after tax was NT$18.02B, but non-controlling interests took NT$8.39B, or 47%, leaving only NT$9.63B for the parent. A year earlier, non-controlling interests took 52%. A considerable share of Wistron's profit comes from subsidiaries it does not wholly own, so the portion that reaches parent shareholders is limited (our inference). EPS was NT$3.06, up 65% YoY.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Inventory Surges by NT$256.1B</h3>
<p>Inventory rose from NT$355.5B at the start of the year to NT$611.6B, up 72%, and receivables rose by NT$144.9B. Together they absorbed a large amount of working capital: operating cash flow was -NT$140.6B, versus -NT$27.2B a year earlier. Inventory grew far faster than revenue's 17% QoQ increase, suggesting heavy stocking of materials for next quarter's shipments (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Funded by Short-Term Borrowing</h3>
<p>Short-term borrowings rose from NT$235.2B to NT$354.9B, an increase of nearly NT$120B; borrowings plus corporate bonds totaled NT$445.3B, cash fell to NT$100B, and net debt was about NT$345.3B. Non-operating items widened from -NT$340M to -NT$5.63B as interest costs rose with borrowings (our inference; the summary financials do not break out non-operating items).</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Server manufacturing scale and customer relationships</h3>
<p>Wistron is a large electronics contract manufacturer that, through its subsidiaries, is deeply embedded in the cloud server and AI server supply chain; its revenue scale keeps the expense ratio below 2%, and its ability to win orders shows in doubled revenue. The weaknesses: contract-manufacturing margins are thin, revenue growth requires heavy working capital, which raises borrowing and inventory risk, and a sizable share of profit belongs to minority shareholders.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue surge**: Q1 revenue of NT$846.30B (+144%, up 17% QoQ).
- **Profit growth**: Operating income of NT$29.14B (+92%), EPS of NT$3.06 (+65%).
- **Margins slip**: Gross margin fell to 5.21% and operating margin to 3.44%.
- **Working capital**: Inventory up 72%, operating cash flow of -NT$140.6B, short-term borrowings up nearly NT$120B.
- **Minority interests**: Non-controlling interests took 47% of net income.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Wistron's Q1 revenue and profit grew sharply on the AI server shipment wave; but inventory and borrowings surged in tandem, cash flowed out heavily, and nearly half of net income belongs to minority shareholders, so working capital and earnings quality are the keys going forward.
