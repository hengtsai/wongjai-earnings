## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue 30% Above Guidance Midpoint</h3>
<p>Q1 revenue was $64.67M, up 755% YoY and 30% above the midpoint of the $48–51M guidance. Chairman and CEO Niccolo de Masi called it <b>the company's fourth straight record quarter and the biggest in its history</b>. CFO Inder Singh said growth came from global system sales, demand for Tempo and higher cloud usage.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: First Sale of a 256-Qubit System</h3>
<p>IonQ sold its first sixth-generation chip-based 256-qubit system to the University of Cambridge in the UK and received its first batch of ion-trap chip samples. De Masi said this <b>marks a key shift toward commercial scale</b>, as work moves from component testing to full-system testing. The company also published a full architecture roadmap for fault-tolerant quantum computing.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Warrant Revaluation Flips GAAP to a Profit</h3>
<p>GAAP net income was $805M, or $2.19 per share, but it came from a $1.06B warrant revaluation gain; the operating loss was $272M, 3.6x last year, and adjusted loss per share was $0.34. R&D expense was $126M, up 215% YoY, and G&A was $88.6M.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: RPO of $470M Improves Order Visibility</h3>
<p>Remaining performance obligations were $470M, up 554% YoY. Singh said about 60% of revenue came from commercial customers and 35% from international customers, more than a third came from multi-product customers, and the company now sells in more than 30 countries. This quarter it was also selected for DARPA's HARQ program and won a $39M space communications contract from the SDA.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Full-Year Revenue Guidance Raised to $260–270M</h3>
<p>Full-year revenue guidance was raised from $225–245M to $260–270M. Adjusted EBITDA guidance was kept at -$330M to -$310M, and Q1 already used -$96.8M of it. On the first day after earnings the stock fell from $52.57 to $47.68.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Full-Year Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: trapped-ion technology + a full-stack platform</h3>
<p>IonQ is built around trapped-ion quantum computers and has expanded into quantum networking, sensing and post-quantum security, with products sold in more than 30 countries. About $3.1B in cash lets it keep investing in 256-qubit systems and acquisitions, but most of its businesses are still in early commercialization.</p>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue explosion**: Q1 revenue of $64.67M (+755%), 30% above the guidance midpoint.
- **Technology milestone**: first 256-qubit system sold to the University of Cambridge, plus a fault-tolerant computing roadmap.
- **Order growth**: RPO of $470M, up 554% YoY; full-year revenue guidance raised to $260–270M.
- **GAAP distortion**: a $1.06B warrant revaluation gain produced $805M of net income, while the core business posted a $272M operating loss.
- **Plenty of cash**: about $3.1B in cash and investments, but operating cash flow was -$151M and cash burn is accelerating.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: IonQ's Q1 revenue blew past guidance and it sold its first 256-qubit system, speeding up commercialization of its quantum platform; but core losses and cash burn are both growing, GAAP net income is only a paper effect of warrant revaluation, and the stock still fell nearly 10% after earnings.
