## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Beat across the board, nearly $1B of ARR added in one quarter</h3>
<p>Q4 revenue of $3.41B, NGS ARR of $9.10B, RPO of $21.2B and non-GAAP EPS of $1.02 all beat guidance. CEO Nikesh Arora said the company <b>added nearly $1B of NGS ARR in the quarter</b>, and that AI has made security the top priority for CIOs.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: CyberArk consolidated, GAAP swings to a loss</h3>
<p>Q4 amortization of acquired intangibles was $281M, 7.6 times the prior year, and a $524M revaluation of the convertible notes assumed from CyberArk was booked below the operating line, producing a GAAP loss of $0.35 per share. CFO Dipak Golechha said all three platforms, network and AI security, Cortex and Idira, were strong, and that the full year <b>beat guidance across the board</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Non-GAAP operating margin dips, EPS up only 7%</h3>
<p>Non-GAAP operating income was $1.01B, up 32% YoY, but the operating margin slipped from 30.3% to 29.6%; non-GAAP diluted shares rose from 707M to 832M, so non-GAAP EPS grew only 7% YoY. Acquisitions brought revenue growth while diluting per-share earnings.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: FY27 revenue growth slows to 23–24%</h3>
<p>FY27 revenue guidance is $14.1–14.2B, up 23–24% YoY, and NGS ARR growth of 22–23%, below FY26's 63% (including acquisitions); non-GAAP EPS of $4.16–4.19, up about 9%, and an adjusted free cash flow margin of 38%. The CFO reiterated the FY28 target of a 40% adjusted free cash flow margin.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Console acquisition, FY30 NGS ARR target of $20B</h3>
<p>The company announced the acquisition of Console, an AI-native agentic workflow platform, expanding Cortex's role in enterprises' shift to AI agents. Arora said AI progress will be <b>a lasting tailwind</b> carrying the company toward its FY30 target of $20B in NGS ARR, 2.2 times the end-of-FY26 level.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and FY27 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: platform consolidation</h3>
<p>Palo Alto combines network security, cloud and security operations (Cortex) and identity security (Idira, from CyberArk) into one platform; the more modules customers use, the higher their switching costs. RPO of $21.2B and deferred revenue of $14.8B provide multi-year revenue visibility.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Beat across the board**: Q4 revenue of $3.41B (+34%), NGS ARR of $9.10B and RPO of $21.2B all topped guidance.
- **GAAP loss**: CyberArk acquisition amortization and convertible-note revaluation led to a GAAP loss of $0.35 per share.
- **EPS diluted**: non-GAAP operating income +32%, but a higher share count held EPS growth to 7%.
- **Slowing growth**: FY27 revenue growth of 23–24% and non-GAAP EPS of about +9%.
- **Steady cash flow**: FY26 adjusted free cash flow of $4.41B, a 38.4% margin.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Palo Alto beat guidance on every metric in FY26 Q4, and CyberArk adds identity security to the platform; but acquisitions diluted the share base and pushed GAAP into a loss, FY27 revenue and EPS growth slow sharply, and the stock fell 9% after the report.
