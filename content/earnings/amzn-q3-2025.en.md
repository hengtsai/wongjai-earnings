## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: AWS Rebounds to 20.2%, Adding 3.8 GW in a Year</h3>
<p>AWS revenue reached $33.0B, up 20.2% YoY. CEO Andy Jassy said <b>AWS is growing at a pace not seen since 2022</b>, noting strong demand in both AI and core infrastructure as the company focuses on accelerating capacity—having <b>added more than 3.8 GW over the past 12 months</b>, more than any other cloud provider according to the company. However, AWS operating margin fell from 38.1% to 34.6% on severance costs and AI equipment depreciation.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Trainium2 Up 150% QoQ, Project Rainier Goes Live</h3>
<p>Custom AI chip Trainium2 is fully subscribed, becoming a multibillion-dollar business growing 150% QoQ. The Project Rainier cluster uses nearly 500,000 Trainium2 chips for Anthropic to train and deploy Claude models, while Bedrock added OpenAI open-weight models and Claude Sonnet 4.5. Connect customer-service AI reached $1B in annualized revenue, and AgentCore became generally available.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Two Special Charges Keep Operating Income Flat</h3>
<p>Operating income was flat YoY at $17.42B, weighed down by the $2.5B FTC settlement and $1.8B in severance; excluding those, operating income would have been $21.7B (+25% YoY) at a 12.0% margin. North America operating income fell 15% YoY to $4.79B, or $7.29B (6.9% margin) ex-FTC. Q4 guidance assumes no additional acquisitions, restructurings, or legal settlements.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Net Income +38%, Over a Third of Pre-Tax Profit from Anthropic</h3>
<p>Non-operating income reached $10.75B, including a $9.5B pre-tax gain on Anthropic—about 34% of the $28.17B pre-tax profit. Net income jumped 38% YoY to $21.19B (EPS $1.95), even though operating income was flat. Advertising revenue of $17.7B (+23.5% YoY) was the fastest-growing category, expanding through Prime Video ads and partnerships with Netflix and Spotify.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Capex Up 61% YoY, TTM Free Cash Flow Drops to $14.8B</h3>
<p>Q3 net capex surged 61% YoY to $34.2B, leaving quarterly free cash flow at just $1.3B. Although TTM operating cash flow grew 16% YoY, a $50.9B increase in net capex cut TTM free cash flow from $47.7B to $14.8B. Property and equipment rose $71.8B over nine months while cash and marketable securities fell $7.0B, with spending directed primarily toward AWS data centers and AI chips.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q4 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: AWS Scale Plus Retail Logistics & Advertising</h3>
<p>AWS exceeds $130B in annualized revenue and offers both NVIDIA GPUs and custom Trainium and Graviton chips alongside multi-model access on Bedrock. On the retail side, its in-house logistics network expands same-day and next-day delivery—with same-day perishables reaching 2,300 cities and towns by year-end—and 250M shoppers have used Rufus this year. Traffic is then monetized via advertising, creating a mutually reinforcing flywheel.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **AWS rebounds**: Up 20.2% YoY, its fastest since 2022, adding 3.8 GW of capacity in a year with Trainium2 sold out.
- **Special charges weigh on profit**: $2.5B FTC settlement and $1.8B severance; excluding them, operating income was $21.7B (12.0% margin).
- **Net income boosted by non-op**: Anthropic pre-tax gain of $9.5B accounted for about one-third of pre-tax profit.
- **Ad strength**: Advertising revenue rose 24% YoY to $17.7B, the fastest-growing revenue category.
- **Cash flow softens**: TTM free cash flow dropped 69% YoY to $14.8B as capex jumped 61% YoY.

> 💡 **One-line takeaway**: Amazon answered market doubts with its fastest AWS growth since 2022 and 24% ad growth, though heavy capex compressed free cash flow to $14.8B and a third of pre-tax profit came from Anthropic.
