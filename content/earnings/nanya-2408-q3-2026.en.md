## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue up 62% QoQ and 6x YoY</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue was NT$133.65B, up 62% from Q2's NT$82.55B and 7.1 times the NT$18.78B a year earlier. Nine-month revenue of NT$265.28B is already 4 times full-year 2025's NT$66.59B. Surging memory prices are the main reason (our inference; the financial statements do not split price and volume).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: 85.7% gross margin, 80% operating margin</h3>
<p>Gross margin rose further from 79.5% in Q2 to 85.7%, versus only 18.5% a year earlier, while cost of revenue was up just 25% from last year. An operating margin of 80.0% means NT$80 of every NT$100 of revenue is operating profit, an extreme peak in the memory cycle.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Quarterly EPS of NT$25.40, nearly NT$50 for nine months</h3>
<p>Net income attributable to the parent was NT$87.63B and basic EPS NT$25.40, up 73% QoQ; diluted EPS was NT$24.38. Nine-month EPS was NT$49.28 versus a loss of NT$1.45 a year earlier. A single quarter's profit already exceeds Nanya's full-year profit in most past years.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Cash surges after a cash capital increase</h3>
<p>Period-end cash was NT$289.9B and the only borrowing left is NT$14.4B of long-term loans, for net cash of about NT$275.5B. Nine-month operating cash flow was NT$184.8B, plus a NT$78.7B cash capital increase that raised the share count by about 11%. Capex was NT$27.6B, up 147% YoY, as investment expands.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Stock falls despite peak profits</h3>
<p>After preliminary figures were released at the 10/12 investor conference, the stock fell 7.4% on 10/13 from NT$527 to NT$488. The drop on record profits reflects market worry that the memory cycle has peaked; P/E is under 5x on annualized Q3 EPS, but whether peak earnings can last is the key question.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: a Formosa Plastics Group DRAM maker with its own fabs</h3>
<p>Nanya is a DRAM manufacturer within the Formosa Plastics Group, with its own fabs and process technology. The memory industry is highly cyclical, and prices can fall quickly when supply and demand reverse; the big increase in net cash and equity strengthens its ability to weather the next downturn.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue explosion**: Q3 revenue of NT$133.65B (up 612% YoY and 62% QoQ).
- **Extremely high margins**: Gross margin of 85.7% and operating margin of 80.0%, peak levels for the memory cycle.
- **Record earnings**: EPS of NT$25.40 and NT$49.28 for nine months, far above full-year 2025's NT$2.13.
- **Ample funds**: Net cash of about NT$275.5B after a NT$78.7B cash capital increase that raised shares about 11%.
- **Stock pulls back**: Profits hit a record but the stock fell after the conference on fears that the cycle has peaked.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Riding the DRAM price surge, Nanya delivered peak Q3 results with revenue up 6x YoY and an 80% operating margin, and its balance sheet is much stronger; but memory is highly cyclical, and the market is already discounting the next downturn.
