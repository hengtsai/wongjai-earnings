## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Smart Edge surges 63% past a 58% share, ending reliance on phones alone</h3>
<p>The most historic turn in Q3 was Smart Edge revenue rising 63% YoY to NT$97.5B, lifting its share from 42% a year earlier to 58%. Growth came from AI tablets, AI PCs, new 5G Thin Modem customers and automotive wafer shipments. CEO Rick Tsai said diversified edge computing platforms have become MediaTek's main growth driver and the structural transformation is established.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: First cloud AI accelerator in volume production, contributing $2.5B in Q4</h3>
<p>The bombshell on the call: MediaTek's first custom AI accelerator ASIC for a North American cloud giant (CSP) began low-volume shipments in September and will ramp sharply in Q4, contributing about $2.5B (more than NT$79B) of revenue. In 2027 the program is headed toward the top of its target range (about $16B), aiming for 20% of the global cloud AI accelerator market and putting MediaTek alongside Broadcom and Marvell.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: A second US CSP customer signed, with next-gen accelerators coming in 2028</h3>
<p>Beyond its existing customer, Rick Tsai confirmed that MediaTek has formally signed "a second large US CSP customer," and the next-generation 2nm AI accelerator is progressing well, with volume production and meaningful revenue expected in Q1 2028. This shows MediaTek's capabilities in high-speed SerDes, CoWoS advanced packaging and large-die integration have been endorsed by two top North American cloud giants.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: NVIDIA and Google take part in a $3.9B ECB, securing capacity</h3>
<p>MediaTek completed a $3.9B euro-convertible bond (ECB) offering with strategic participation from NVIDIA and Alphabet. This billion-dollar-scale strategic funding is earmarked for securing TSMC 2nm and CoWoS capacity, ensuring supply chain bottlenecks do not hold MediaTek back in the global AI compute arms race over the next 3 years.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Q4 revenue guidance up 25%–32%, full-year USD revenue sharply higher</h3>
<p>Management gave stunning Q4 guidance: consolidated revenue of NT$210.1B to NT$221.8B (up 25% to 32% QoQ), an unprecedented quarterly record for a Taiwan IC designer. Rick Tsai said full-year USD revenue growth will far exceed the high-single-digit target set at the start of the year, and the medium- to long-term goal is to lift operating margin above 20% in 2027.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q4 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: top-tier TSMC advanced-node partner, deep ties with NVIDIA/Google and a two-engine structure</h3>
<p>MediaTek is one of very few IC design giants with 2nm tape-outs, top-tier TSMC CoWoS allocations and in-house 224G SerDes IP, and it works with NVIDIA on edge PC chips and with Google/CSPs on cloud accelerators, forming an irreplaceable end-to-end compute moat.</p>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Data center ASICs dilute blended gross margin early on</b>: Q4 gross margin is expected to fall to 45%; if cloud accelerator system assembly costs exceed plans, margin could briefly drop below 44%.</li>
<li><b>Tight CoWoS and HBM supply</b>: If TSMC advanced packaging or HBM3e capacity cannot be allocated in time, Q4 and 2027 ASIC deliveries of $16B could be affected.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Slow recovery in mid-range smartphones</b>: Mobile chip revenue fell 17% YoY, and weak upgrade demand in emerging markets would keep weighing on Dimensity 8000/7000 shipments.</li>
<li><b>Risk of US CSP capex shifts</b>: If cloud giants change the pace of their in-house ASIC programs, the ramp of second-generation products in 2028 could be affected.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Strong sales of the NVIDIA-partnered AI PC (N1X)</b>: If enterprises broadly adopt the RTX Spark AI PC after its October launch, high-margin edge computing revenue would grow quickly.</li>
<li><b>ECB oversubscribed by top-tier giants</b>: Strategic backing from NVIDIA and Google sharply lowers funding costs and plants opportunities for future custom chip projects.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue mix transformed**: Smart Edge tops 58%, leaving behind the cycle of a pure handset chip company for a new high-growth profile.
- **Cloud ASICs enter harvest season**: The first custom AI accelerator is in volume production, contributing $2.5B in Q4, with 2027 aimed at the $16B top end.
- **Deep alliances with top giants**: A second US CSP is signed, and NVIDIA and Google strategically subscribed to the $3.9B convertible bond, securing capacity and orders.
- **Q4 guidance jumps 30%**: Quarterly revenue guidance tops NT$220B, a record for Taiwan's chip industry, with strong full-year revenue growth.
- **A rerating sweet spot**: At 14x forward P/E with a shot at 20% of the global cloud ASIC market, it is Greater China's semiconductor name with the most rerating potential.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: With Smart Edge now more than half of revenue, MediaTek has completed its structural shift, and $2.5B a quarter from cloud AI accelerators marks its formal entry into the top tier of global ASIC makers—at 14 times earnings, a rare rerating opportunity.
