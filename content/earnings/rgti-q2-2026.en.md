## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue up 185% YoY, driven by system sales</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue was $5.14M, up 185% YoY and 17% QoQ, and gross margin rose from 31% to 43%. CEO Subodh Kulkarni said the quarter focused on system performance, the technology roadmap and <b>expanding on-premises system deployments</b>, and the company is delivering Novera systems and a 108-qubit system for India's C-DAC.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Commerce Department LOI, up to $100M for equity</h3>
<p>In May the company signed a letter of intent with the US Department of Commerce for up to $100M of R&D funding over three years under the CHIPS R&D program, in return for equivalent equity. Kulkarni said this <b>highlights the strategic importance of the platform</b> and can accelerate R&D on scaling superconducting quantum computing.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Building a hybrid quantum supercomputer with HPE</h3>
<p>Rigetti expanded its work with HPE and the Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center and will deliver a 9-qubit Novera system to the TangleLab testbed funded by the National Science Foundation. Kulkarni said this reflects demand for its open, modular approach, with participation growing among government, academic and commercial customers.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: 108-qubit fidelity about 99.1%</h3>
<p>Cepheus-1-108Q's median two-qubit fidelity is about 99.1% with a gate speed of about 60 ns; the smaller 9- and 36-qubit systems reach 99.8% and 99.6% respectively. The company aims for about 1,000 qubits, 99.9% fidelity and gate speeds under 50 ns within about three years.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Wider losses, GAAP dragged down by warrants</h3>
<p>The operating loss was $28.1M, wider than a year earlier, with R&D up 53% YoY. A $29.6M warrant valuation loss pushed the GAAP net loss to $52.6M; excluding it, the non-GAAP net loss was $16M. The company also plans to invest up to $100M in the UK over the coming years.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: chiplets and an in-house fab</h3>
<p>Rigetti tiles 9-qubit chiplets into a 108-qubit system and designs and makes its chips in its own Fab-1. The superconducting approach offers fast gates, but fidelity still trails some rivals; revenue is small and relies mainly on orders from governments and research institutions.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue growth**: Q2 revenue of $5.14M (+185%, +17% QoQ), with gross margin up to 43%.
- **Government funding**: the Commerce Department LOI offers up to $100M of R&D funding in exchange for equivalent equity.
- **Wider partnerships**: building hybrid quantum systems with HPE and the Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center.
- **Technical progress**: 108-qubit fidelity of about 99.1%, targeting 1,000 qubits and 99.9% within three years.
- **Wider losses**: operating loss of $28.1M, and warrant revaluation took the GAAP net loss to $52.6M.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Rigetti's Q2 revenue and gross margin improved and it won endorsements from the Commerce Department and HPE; but revenue is tiny, fidelity still needs a breakthrough, and the valuation depends heavily on long-term hopes for quantum technology.
