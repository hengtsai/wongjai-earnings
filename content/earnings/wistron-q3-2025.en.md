## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue Jumps 108% YoY</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue reached NT$567.81B (+108% YoY), up another 3% from NT$551.29B in Q2, bringing 9M revenue to NT$1.47T (+95% YoY). While the filing does not break out product lines, doubled revenue alongside low gross margins aligns with a ramp in full-rack AI server shipments (our inference). Operating expenses rose only 31%, demonstrating clear operating leverage.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Gross Margin Rebounds from 4.4% to 7.4%</h3>
<p>Gross margin was 7.39%, rebounding 3.0pp from 4.44% in Q2 though 1.0pp below 8.35% a year ago. Operating income jumped 138% YoY to NT$27.14B—2.5x Q2's level—lifting operating margin to 4.78%. R&D expense rose 44% YoY to NT$8.91B, dropping to 1.6% of revenue as top-line scale diluted fixed costs.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Minority Interests Take 55% of Net Income</h3>
<p>Total net income was NT$16.52B, but non-controlling interests claimed NT$9.11B (55%), leaving only NT$7.41B attributable to parent shareholders. Up from 47% a year ago, the minority share continues to climb. A substantial portion of Wistron's profits comes from partially owned subsidiaries, limiting what accrues to parent shareholders (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Inventory Surges NT$234.6B</h3>
<p>Inventory surged 123% from NT$190.7B at year-start to NT$425.3B, while accounts receivable grew NT$108.2B. Together they absorbed massive working capital, driving 9M operating cash flow to -NT$176.6B (vs. -NT$12.4B prior year), alongside CapEx at 3.1x last year's pace. Inventory growth far outpacing revenue signals heavy component stocking ahead of upcoming shipments (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Relying on Short-Term Debt to Fund Working Capital</h3>
<p>Short-term borrowings jumped NT$165.2B from NT$81.7B to NT$246.9B; total debt and bonds reached NT$318.1B against just NT$77.5B in cash, resulting in net debt of ~NT$240.7B. Non-operating net loss widened from -NT$1.22B to -NT$5.10B, reflecting higher interest costs from rising debt (our inference; the report summary does not itemize non-operating items).</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Server manufacturing scale and customer relationships</h3>
<p>As a major EMS/ODM manufacturer, Wistron is deeply embedded in cloud and AI server supply chains via its subsidiaries, with revenue scale compressing its OpEx ratio below 3% and order momentum reflected in doubled revenue. Key weaknesses include thin contract-manufacturing margins, heavy working capital requirements that elevate debt and inventory risks, and over half of profits going to minority interests.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue Doubled**: Q3 revenue reached NT$567.81B (+108% YoY, +3% QoQ), bringing 9M to NT$1.47T.
- **Profit Surge**: Operating income reached NT$27.14B (+138% YoY), with EPS at NT$2.36 (+61% YoY).
- **Gross Margin Recovery**: 7.39%, rebounding 3.0pp from 4.44% in Q2.
- **Working Capital**: Inventory jumped 123%, 9M operating cash flow hit -NT$176.6B, and short-term debt rose NT$165.2B.
- **Minority Interests**: Non-controlling interests claimed 55% of net income.

> 💡 **Bottom Line**: Wistron doubled revenue and operating income in Q3 on the AI server shipment wave, but surging inventory and debt, heavy cash outflows, and over half of net income going to minority shareholders make working capital pressure and profit attribution the key metrics to watch.
