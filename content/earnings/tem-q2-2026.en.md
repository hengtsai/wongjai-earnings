## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Oncology testing accelerates, revenue up 22%</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue was $382M, up 22% YoY; oncology testing volume grew 31% YoY, accelerating from 28% last quarter, but hereditary testing revenue grew only 5%. Founder Eric Lefkofsky said: <b>"Our strategy is working given the investments we have made in AI over the past several years are driving some of the best growth rates we have seen in our two largest businesses - Oncology Diagnostics and Data Licensing."</b></p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Strong data licensing, first foundation model delivered</h3>
<p>Data and services revenue was $93.2M, up 28% YoY, with Insights up 36% YoY; $200M of new contracts were signed this quarter with customers including BioNTech, Daiichi Sankyo and Incyte. Tempus also delivered the first version of its oncology foundation model to AstraZeneca. The higher-margin data business lifted overall gross margin to 64.4%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: $1.5B Personalis deal to bolster MRD</h3>
<p>On 7/20 Tempus announced it would acquire MRD testing company Personalis for $16.25 per share, an enterprise value of about $1.5B. Lefkofsky said the partnership had already proven the combination of highly sensitive MRD technology and Tempus's commercial channel, and added: <b>"With clinical adoption and reimbursement momentum building, we believe we are collectively well positioned to capture this opportunity, which makes this acquisition particularly exciting."</b> MRD tests were about 9,000 this quarter.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: GAAP profit relies on securities gains; core business still loses money</h3>
<p>GAAP net income was $5.6M but included a $98.5M unrealized gain on marketable securities; the operating loss was $75.9M, wider than a year ago, as SG&A rose 25% YoY. Adjusted EBITDA was $8M, positive again. Full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance remains about $65M, while the first half totaled only $5.2M.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Convertibles replace high-interest loans</h3>
<p>This quarter Tempus issued $460M of 0% convertibles and repaid all long-term debt and the revolver, cutting interest expense from $21.6M a year ago to $10.3M. Cash and marketable securities at quarter-end were $821M, but convertibles total $1.17B; the Personalis acquisition would raise funding needs further.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Full-Year Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: the testing data + AI model flywheel</h3>
<p>Tempus builds clinical and genomic data from a large volume of oncology tests, then licenses it to drug makers and trains foundation models. FDA approval of both the xT tumor-only and tumor-normal companion diagnostics makes it the first lab to hold both; the Personalis deal strengthens MRD.</p>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue growth**: Q2 revenue of $382M (+22%), with oncology volume up 31% YoY, faster than last quarter.
- **Data licensing**: Insights up 36% YoY, $200M of new contracts, and a foundation model delivered to AstraZeneca.
- **MRD push**: MRD tests up 38% QoQ; announced the acquisition of Personalis at an enterprise value of about $1.5B.
- **Earnings quality**: GAAP net income of $5.6M relied on a $98.5M securities gain; the operating loss was still $75.9M.
- **Capital structure**: 0% convertibles replaced high-interest loans, halving interest expense.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Tempus's oncology testing and data licensing both accelerated in Q2, and it is betting on MRD by acquiring Personalis; but the core business still loses money, GAAP profit relies on securities gains, and the full-year EBITDA target depends on a second-half sprint.
