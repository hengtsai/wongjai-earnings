## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Net New ARR Accelerates 73% YoY</h3>
<p>Q3 net new ARR surged 73% YoY to a Q3 record of $265M, lifting ending ARR to $4.92B with growth accelerating to 23%. CEO Kurtz described <b>Q3 as one of the best quarters in the company's history</b>, noting accelerating ARR growth across endpoint, cloud security, next-gen identity, and SIEM. Year-ago comparisons also benefited from a low base following the July 2024 outage (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Falcon Flex Drives Consolidation</h3>
<p>Ending ARR from customers on the Falcon Flex subscription model exceeded $1.35B, up more than 200% YoY. Flex lets customers commit budgets upfront and flexibly deploy modules, supporting 49% adoption of six or more modules. The company said its single platform combined with Flex enables vendor consolidation, positioning CrowdStrike as <b>the operating system for cybersecurity</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Profitability Metrics Top Guidance Across the Board</h3>
<p>Revenue of $1.23B, Non-GAAP operating income of $265M, and Non-GAAP EPS of $0.96 all topped the upper end of guidance, implying a 21% Non-GAAP operating margin (our estimate). The CFO noted that <b>results exceeded guidance across all guided metrics</b>. However, GAAP operating loss widened YoY to $69.4M, mainly due to stock-based compensation and outage-related costs (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Record Cash Flow</h3>
<p>Q3 operating cash flow of $398M and free cash flow of $296M both set Q3 records, yielding a 24% FCF margin (our estimate). Ending cash stood at $4.80B against $750M in long-term debt, while deferred revenue rose 9% from fiscal year-start to $4.06B. CrowdStrike also acquired AI security firm Pangea during the quarter.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Guidance Raised, FY27 Growth Target Set</h3>
<p>Full-year revenue guidance was raised from $4.750B–$4.806B to $4.797B–$4.807B, with Non-GAAP EPS seen at $3.70–$3.72. Q4 guidance calls for $1.290B–$1.300B in revenue and $1.09–$1.11 in Non-GAAP EPS. Management also raised its H2 net new ARR growth target to at least 50% and guided FY27 net new ARR to grow another 20%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: the single-agent Falcon platform</h3>
<p>CrowdStrike uses a single lightweight agent and unified cloud platform spanning endpoint, cloud, identity, and SIEM, keeping data centralized with low incremental adoption friction—further aided by Flex. Weaknesses include ongoing GAAP losses, high stock-based compensation, and continuing litigation and costs from the 2024 outage.</p>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **ARR accelerates**: Ending ARR reached $4.92B (+23% YoY), with net new ARR at $265M (+73%).
- **Guidance topped across the board**: Revenue, Non-GAAP operating income, and EPS all exceeded the top end of guidance.
- **Flex drives consolidation**: Falcon Flex customer ARR topped $1.35B, up over 200% YoY.
- **Record cash flow**: Q3 free cash flow hit $296M (+28%).
- **Guidance raised**: Full-year revenue seen at $4.797B–$4.807B, with FY27 net new ARR targeted to grow 20%.

> 💡 **One-line takeaway**: CrowdStrike moved past the outage shadow in FY26 Q3 as net new ARR jumped 73%, ARR growth re-accelerated, and all key metrics topped guidance, though ongoing GAAP losses and a >140x P/E show the stock has already priced in the re-acceleration narrative.
