## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Backlog nearly doubles to $55.6B</h3>
<p>Co-founder and CEO Michael Intrator said the quarter <b>delivered record revenue and nearly doubled backlog to over $55B</b>. Growth was anchored by a multi-year Meta contract up to ~$14.2B, an ~$6.5B expansion with OpenAI bringing cumulative commitments up to ~$22.4B, and a sixth contract with a major hyperscaler. Customer concentration has also risen accordingly (inferred here).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Revenue doubles, margins compressed</h3>
<p>Revenue grew 134% YoY to $1.36B, yet depreciation jumped from $250M to $630M and stock-based compensation rose from $7.6M to $144M, pushing operating income down 56% YoY to a 4% margin. Adjusted EBITDA margin dipped slightly from 65% to 61%. Early-stage depreciation on newly brought-online capacity weighs on profits until utilization ramps (inferred here).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Interest expense triples YoY</h3>
<p>Interest expense tripled YoY to $310M as total debt grew from $7.9B to $14.0B in one year. During the quarter, CoreWeave issued $1.75B in 9% unsecured notes, secured a $2.6B DDTL 3.0 facility at SOFR+4%, and expanded DDTL 2.1 by $3.0B, noting the latter carried rates <b>significantly below original costs</b>, signaling declining cost of capital.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Power remains the expansion bottleneck</h3>
<p>CoreWeave added ~120 MW of active power in Q3 to reach ~590 MW alongside ~2.9 GW of contracted power while diversifying power suppliers. It announced up to $6B for a data center in Lancaster, PA (100 MW initial, scalable to 300 MW) and added £1.5B in UK investment (total £2.5B). With a wide gap between active and contracted power, execution speed is crucial (inferred here).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: $6.3B NVIDIA collaboration</h3>
<p>CoreWeave signed a $6.3B strategic collaboration with NVIDIA to expand GPU infrastructure, became the first to deploy GB300 NVL72, and launched CoreWeave Ventures to invest in the AI ecosystem. Intrator said the company's <b>position as the essential cloud for AI has never been stronger</b>. Still, the stock fell 16% the day after earnings, showing a sharp disconnect between market reaction and operating metrics.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: an AI-specialized cloud and first access to new NVIDIA products</h3>
<p>Purpose-built for AI, CoreWeave is frequently first to deploy new NVIDIA platforms and locks in customers like Meta and OpenAI under multi-year contracts, with a $55.6B backlog providing multi-year visibility. Its weakness lies in its capital structure: expansion relies almost entirely on debt and leases, meaning interest and depreciation will weigh on GAAP profits for years, while power and data center delivery depend on partners.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue more than doubled**: Q3 revenue reached $1.36B (+134%), with a 61% adjusted EBITDA margin.
- **Backlog surge**: Backlog nearly doubled to $55.6B, including Meta up to ~$14.2B and OpenAI +~$6.5B.
- **Margin pressure**: $630M in depreciation and $310M in interest left operating margin at 4%.
- **Heavy expansion burn**: Quarterly CapEx of $3.28B, FCF of -$1.59B, and total debt of $14.0B.
- **Power expansion**: ~590 MW active vs. ~2.9 GW contracted, making delivery speed critical.

> 💡 **One-line takeaway**: CoreWeave posted record Q3 backlog and revenue on undeniable AI compute demand, yet surging interest, depreciation, and CapEx—alongside power and partner delivery constraints—sent shares down 16% after earnings.
