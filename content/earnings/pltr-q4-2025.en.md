## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Rule of 40 Score Reaches 127%</h3>
<p>With 70% YoY revenue growth and a 57% adjusted operating margin, Palantir's Rule of 40 score reached 127%, up from 114% last quarter. CEO Alex Karp said the company <b>is a category of one and the numbers prove it</b>, adding that Palantir is uniquely focused on turning AI model progress into operating leverage. Revenue beat the top end of guidance by $76M and adjusted operating income by ~$100M.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: US Commercial Becomes the Growth Engine</h3>
<p>US commercial revenue surged 137% YoY and 28% QoQ to $507M (full year $1.465B, +109% YoY), while US commercial RDV climbed 145% YoY to $4.38B. For 2026, management guided US commercial revenue to at least $3.144B (+115%+ YoY), signaling further acceleration. US commercial TCV rose 67% YoY to $1.34B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Government Business Accelerates in Tandem</h3>
<p>US government revenue rose 66% YoY to $570M, accelerating from 52% in Q3, bringing full-year revenue to $1.855B (+55% YoY). Quarterly TCV hit a record $4.26B (+138% YoY), including 61 deals of $10M or more. The US accounted for 76% of total revenue, leaving ~$330M outside the US with growth clearly lagging the US (our estimate).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Margins Surge as SBC Expense Drops</h3>
<p>Gross margin reached 84.6% (+5.7pp YoY) while operating expenses fell 4% YoY; stock-based compensation declined from $282M to $196M (from 34% to 14% of revenue), lifting GAAP operating margin from 1% to 41%. An unusually high $80M in employer payroll taxes in Q4 last year also amplified the YoY improvement (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: 2026 Guidance Implies 61% YoY Growth</h3>
<p>Full-year 2026 guidance calls for revenue of $7.18–7.20B (~61% YoY growth), adjusted operating income of $4.13–4.14B (~57% margin), adjusted free cash flow of $3.93–4.13B, and positive GAAP operating and net income every quarter. Q1 revenue guidance of $1.532–1.536B implies ~74% YoY growth, accelerating further from Q4's 70% (our estimate).</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Moat & Guidance Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: Ontology Platform & Government Trust</h3>
<p>Palantir's Foundry, Gotham, and AIP platforms unify enterprise and government data into operational models, creating high switching costs once embedded; decades of serving US defense and intelligence agencies give it clearances and trust that are hard to replicate. The vulnerability is valuation: at nearly 90x P/S, years of rapid growth are already priced in.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Extreme valuation</b>: At ~91x P/S and ~250x P/E, even slight growth deceleration could trigger a sharp pullback.</li>
<li><b>Government reliance</b>: US government accounts for ~41% of revenue, exposing results to budget and policy shifts.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Terminable contracts</b>: TCV and RDV assume full execution, while most contracts can be terminated for convenience.</li>
<li><b>International lag</b>: Non-US revenue of ~$330M (24% share) is growing notably slower than the US.</li>
<li><b>Rising receivables</b>: Accounts receivable jumped 81% YoY to $1.04B, outpacing revenue growth.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Solid balance sheet</b>: $7.18B in cash and marketable securities, zero debt, and $2.27B in full-year FCF.</li>
<li><b>Accelerating growth</b>: 2026 guidance of +61% overall and at least +115% US commercial both top 2025 rates.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Accelerating growth**: Q4 revenue of $1.41B (+70%) was $76M above the top of guidance; full year hit $4.48B (+56%).
- **US commercial**: Revenue hit $507M (+137%), with 2026 guidance of at least $3.14B (+115%+ YoY).
- **Margin surge**: Adjusted operating margin hit 57% and GAAP operating margin 41%, with SBC down to 14% of revenue.
- **Record bookings**: TCV reached $4.26B (+138%) and US commercial RDV reached $4.38B (+145%).
- **Strong guidance**: 2026 revenue of $7.18–7.20B (+61%), with a commitment to quarterly GAAP profitability.

> 💡 **One-line takeaway**: With Q4 revenue growth accelerating to 70%, a 127% Rule of 40 score, and 2026 guidance of 61%, Palantir's fundamentals are nearly spotless—but at ~91x P/S, years of high growth are priced in and any slowdown could trigger sharp volatility.
