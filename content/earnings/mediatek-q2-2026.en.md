## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue above the top of guidance</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue was NT$152.18B, above the top of the NT$140.2–149.2B guidance given in April, up 2.0% QoQ and 1.2% YoY. The company said growth came mainly from <b>"market share gains in smart device platforms offsetting weaker smartphone demand"</b>, showing the solid base of its diversified product lines.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Gross margin down 2.9pp YoY, operating income down 22%</h3>
<p>Gross margin was 46.2%, within the 44.5–47.5% guidance but 2.9pp below 49.1% a year ago; the company said the year-ago quarter included a one-time gain. R&D expense rose 6.6% YoY, and operating income was NT$22.87B, down 22.2% YoY, with operating margin falling from 19.5% to 15.0%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: EPS of NT$15.28, down 12.7% YoY</h3>
<p>Net income attributable to the parent was NT$24.33B and EPS NT$15.28, down from NT$17.50 a year ago and slightly up from NT$15.17 last quarter. Non-operating income of NT$5.09B, up 32% YoY, plus a tax rate that fell from 15.5% to 12.0%, made the net income decline smaller than the operating income decline (our estimate).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Inventory up 46%, cash flow down sharply</h3>
<p>Inventory rose from NT$67.2B at the start of the year to NT$98.4B, up 46%, and receivables rose 24%. First-half operating cash flow was only NT$6.59B versus NT$59.09B a year earlier; cash dividends of NT$46.29B were paid, cash fell from NT$235.3B to NT$189.0B, and short-term borrowings rose to NT$17.1B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Q3 revenue guided to NT$152.2–159.8B</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue is guided to NT$152.2–159.8B (assuming US$1 = NT$32), with the midpoint up 2.5% from Q2 and about 10% from NT$142.1B a year earlier; gross margin is guided to 46% ± 1.5% and the opex ratio to 31% ± 2%. On the first day after earnings the stock rose from NT$3,555 to NT$3,910.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q3 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: smartphone SoC scale and diversified platforms</h3>
<p>MediaTek is one of the world's main smartphone chip suppliers and has extended its R&D into smart devices, automotive and custom chips. R&D exceeds 25% of revenue; when smartphone demand is weak, share gains on other platforms keep revenue up, but they also weigh on near-term margins.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue beat**: Q2 revenue of NT$152.18B, above the top of guidance, +1.2% YoY and +2.0% QoQ.
- **Profit decline**: Operating income down 22% YoY, with operating margin falling from 19.5% to 15.0%.
- **EPS down YoY**: EPS of NT$15.28 (-12.7%); non-operating income and a lower tax rate softened the decline.
- **Inventory and cash**: Inventory up 46%; first-half operating cash flow dropped to NT$6.59B.
- **Q3 guidance**: Revenue of NT$152.2–159.8B, up about 10% YoY, with gross margin around 46%.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: MediaTek's Q2 revenue beat guidance and smart device platforms offset the smartphone decline, but higher R&D and a lower gross margin cut profit by about 20%; the market is upbeat on second-half momentum, and the stock jumped about 10% after earnings.
