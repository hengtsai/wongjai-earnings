## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue up 30%, profit beats guidance</h3>
<p>Q1 revenue was $2.65B, up 30% YoY, in the upper half of the $2.5–2.7B guidance; adjusted operating profit of $551M and adjusted EPS of $1.17 were both above the top of guidance. CEO Giordano Albertazzi said data center demand is changing, with customers <b>prioritizing optimized design, speed of deployment and operational efficiency</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Adjusted operating margin up 430bp YoY</h3>
<p>Adjusted operating margin was 20.8%, up 430 basis points YoY, which the press release attributed to operating leverage from higher volume and favorable price-cost, including tariff impacts and mitigation. Gross margin rose from 33.7% to 37.7%, and Americas adjusted operating margin rose from 21.9% to 27.0%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Americas strong, EMEA sharply down</h3>
<p>Americas revenue grew 53% YoY (44% organic), and adjusted operating profit rose 89% YoY; EMEA revenue fell 20% YoY (down 29% organic), and adjusted operating profit fell 32%. Growth is concentrated in U.S. data centers, while European demand is weak.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Customer advances surge, cash flow doubles</h3>
<p>Q1 operating cash flow was $767M and adjusted free cash flow $653M, up 153% and 147% YoY. Deferred revenue from customer advances rose from $1.81B to $2.46B. Albertazzi said investments in technology and capacity, along with acquisitions, <b>are translating into market share gains</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Full-Year Guidance Raised</h3>
<p>Full-year revenue guidance was raised from $13.25–13.75B to $13.5–14.0B, with 29–31% organic growth; adjusted EPS guidance went from 5.97–6.07 to $6.30–6.40. Q2 guidance calls for revenue of $3.25–3.45B, 20–24% organic growth and adjusted EPS of $1.37–1.43.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q2 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Integrated power and cooling solutions</h3>
<p>Vertiv provides data center power, cooling and services together. As deployment density rises and timelines compress, customers increasingly need a partner that can deliver and maintain everything in one go. The CEO said the company is ready to be the partner that helps customers execute projects at scale.</p>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Profit beat**: Q1 revenue $2.65B (+30%), with adjusted operating margin of 20.8% and adjusted EPS of $1.17 both above the top of guidance.
- **U.S.-led**: Americas organic growth of 44% and adjusted operating profit +89%; EMEA organic revenue down 29%.
- **Cash flow doubles**: Adjusted free cash flow $653M (+147%), with customer advances up $650M.
- **Guidance raised**: Full-year revenue $13.5–14.0B and adjusted EPS $6.30–6.40.
- **Rich valuation**: P/E of about 48x on full-year guidance.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Vertiv's Q1 profit beat guidance across the board and full-year guidance was raised on strong U.S. data center demand; but growth is highly concentrated in the Americas, Europe is sharply down, and at a rich valuation the stock reaction was muted.
