## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Gross margin jumps to 37%</h3>
<p>Q1 gross margin was 37.00%, 5.2pp above last year's 31.78% and 2.4pp above Q4's roughly 34.6%. Revenue fell 1.4% QoQ yet gross profit stayed high, suggesting a mix shift toward higher-margin products (our inference). The report does not break out products, so the specific sources of the margin gain would need confirmation from company earnings call materials (which we did not obtain).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Operating income doubles</h3>
<p>Operating income was NT$28.42B, up 102% YoY, and operating margin rose from 11.8% to 17.8%, also above Q4's 16.3%. Revenue grew 34% YoY while operating income grew three times as fast, a leverage effect from the big gross margin gain plus a 0.8pp drop in the expense ratio. R&D expense was NT$13.66B, up 23% YoY, or 8.6% of revenue.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: EPS NT$7.91, up 101% YoY</h3>
<p>Net income attributable to the parent was NT$20.56B, up 101% YoY, and EPS was NT$7.91, up 19% from Q4's NT$6.67. Non-operating income was NT$2.68B, more than last year's NT$1.63B, but most of the growth still came from the core business. Single-quarter EPS already equals one-third of full-year 2025's NT$23.14.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Inventory up NT$17.6B</h3>
<p>Inventory rose from NT$101.5B at the start of the year to NT$119.1B, up 17%, and the inventory build consumed about NT$17.6B of operating cash flow in the cash flow statement. Revenue fell QoQ while inventory jumped, possibly to stock materials for upcoming shipments (our inference); if demand falls short of plans, inventory write-downs would weigh on gross margin. Accounts receivable rose 6%, in line with revenue scale.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Solid cash flow, continued expansion</h3>
<p>Operating cash flow was NT$19.93B, up 23% YoY, capex was NT$11.09B, up 35%, and free cash flow was about NT$8.84B. Borrowings and corporate bonds fell from NT$59.1B to NT$56B, leaving net cash of about NT$105B after cash. Equity attributable to the parent was NT$299.5B, up 12% QoQ, a balance sheet strong enough to fund continued expansion.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: power efficiency and scale</h3>
<p>Delta is the global leader in power supplies, with years of accumulated expertise in high-efficiency power and thermal management. Scale lets its expense ratio fall as revenue grows, and R&D spending held above 8% of revenue forms a competitive barrier. Gross margin rising to 37% this quarter suggests solid pricing power (our inference). The weakness is that the report does not break out products, making it hard for outsiders to judge the actual share of AI-related business from official figures.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Gross margin jump**: Q1 gross margin 37.0% (+5.2pp); revenue fell 1.4% QoQ but profit kept growing.
- **Operating income doubles**: NT$28.42B (+102%), operating margin 17.8%.
- **EPS surge**: EPS NT$7.91 (+101%), one quarter equal to one-third of full-year 2025.
- **Inventory build**: Inventory up NT$17.6B, or 17%, from the start of the year.
- **Solid cash flow**: Operating cash flow NT$19.9B, free cash flow about NT$8.8B, net cash about NT$105B.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: With flat revenue, Delta doubled Q1 EPS on a gross margin jump, sharply improving earnings quality; but inventory is rising and the P/E is near 80x, so the question is whether high margins can last and turn into revenue growth.
