## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue and Gross Margin Both In Line with Guidance</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue of €7.52B and gross margin of 51.6% both landed within the €7.4–7.9B and 50–52% ranges guided in July. CEO Christophe Fouquet stated that the results <b>came in line with guidance and marked a good quarter for ASML</b>. System sales fell 6% YoY, while installed base management jumped 27% YoY to €1.96B, keeping total revenue flat.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Bookings Double YoY, EUV Accounts for Two-Thirds</h3>
<p>Net bookings reached €5.4B—double last year's €2.6B—with €3.6B in EUV. Fouquet noted that AI investment momentum continues <b>and is extending to more customers across both leading-edge logic and advanced DRAM</b>; lithography intensity is rising alongside EUV adoption, and High NA EUV is also progressing. Nine-month bookings totaled €14.88B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: 2026 Not Below 2025, But China Set to Drop Sharply</h3>
<p>Management provided its first 2026 directional view: revenue is not expected to be below 2025, with details due in January. However, Fouquet said he expects Chinese customer demand and 2026 China revenue <b>to decline significantly</b> compared with the very strong levels of 2024 and 2025. China has been a major growth driver over the past two years, making it critical whether AI demand can fill the gap (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Q4 Revenue Guided at €9.2–9.8B, Full Year Up ~15%</h3>
<p>Q4 revenue is guided to €9.2–9.8B with gross margin of 51–53%, R&D of ~€1.2B, and SG&A of ~€320M; full-year revenue is projected to grow ~15% with a ~52% gross margin, pointing to <b>a very strong fourth quarter</b>. The Q4 midpoint implies ~26% QoQ growth (our calculation), with full-year revenue around €32.5B (our calculation).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Advanced Packaging and AI Partnership</h3>
<p>ASML shipped its first advanced packaging tool, the TWINSCAN XT:260, an i-line scanner offering up to 4x the productivity of existing solutions to enter the 3D integration market. The Mistral AI partnership aims to embed AI across the product portfolio to boost system performance and customer yields. ASML also declared an interim dividend of €1.60 per share and repurchased ~€148M in shares during Q3.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q4 Guidance

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: EUV Monopoly</h3>
<p>ASML is the only company globally capable of mass-producing EUV lithography systems, with High NA EUV widening its technological lead for both leading-edge logic and DRAM. Its large installed base generates nearly €2B in quarterly service revenue. Key vulnerabilities are export controls restricting China sales and sensitivity to customer CapEx cycles.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **In line with guidance**: Q3 revenue of €7.52B and gross margin of 51.6% both landed within guidance.
- **Bookings doubled**: Net bookings hit €5.4B (2x prior year), including €3.6B in EUV.
- **Services growing**: Installed base management reached €1.96B (+27% YoY), while system sales fell 6% YoY.
- **Mixed outlook**: Q4 revenue guided at €9.2–9.8B and 2026 not below 2025, though China will decline significantly.
- **Shareholder returns**: 9M buybacks totaled €4.25B and dividends €1.93B, lowering cash to €5.1B.

> 💡 **Bottom Line**: ASML delivered a steady Q3 with bookings doubling YoY as AI-driven demand spreads across leading-edge logic and DRAM; however, with China revenue set to drop sharply in 2026, management has only committed to non-declining sales, leaving the growth trajectory to January's full outlook.
