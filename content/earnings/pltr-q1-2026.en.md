## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue Up 85% YoY, the Fastest Growth Ever</h3>
<p>Q1 revenue was $1.63B, up 85% YoY and 16% QoQ, about $100M above the $1.532–1.536B guidance. CEO Alex Karp said 85% is <b>the highest YoY growth rate in the company's history</b>; the US business more than doubled, and the Rule of 40 score rose to 145%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: US Commercial Up 133% YoY</h3>
<p>US commercial revenue was $595M, up 133% YoY, and US government $687M, up 84%, for total US revenue of $1.28B, or 79% of the total. US commercial remaining deal value was $4.92B, up 112% YoY; this quarter Palantir closed 47 deals worth $10M or more, with total contract value of $2.41B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: 46% GAAP Operating Margin</h3>
<p>Gross margin was 86.8%, 6.4pp higher than last year; operating expenses rose only 24%, GAAP operating income was $754M, up 328% YoY, and the operating margin was 46%. Adjusted operating income was $984M, a 60% margin, about $110M above the $870–874M guidance.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Full-Year Guidance Raised by 10 Percentage Points</h3>
<p>Full-year revenue guidance was raised from $7.18–7.20B to $7.65–7.66B, lifting growth from about 61% to about 71%; US commercial revenue is guided to at least $3.22B, growth of at least 120%. Karp said the raise reflects <b>confidence in the acceleration of the US market</b>. Adjusted free cash flow guidance is $4.2–4.4B.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Stock Falls After Earnings</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue guidance is $1.797–1.801B, with adjusted operating income of $1.063–1.067B. Although every metric beat guidance, the stock fell 6.9% on 5/5, from $146.03 to $135.91; on full-year revenue guidance, price-to-sales is still about 46x.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Full-Year Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: AIP and the Ontology platform</h3>
<p>Palantir uses its Ontology to model enterprise data and processes so AI can make decisions inside customer environments. Karp put the company alongside NVIDIA, Micron and SK hynix as AI infrastructure companies breaking through the Rule of 40. Its government and commercial engines are accelerating together.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Very rich valuation</b>: over 150x P/E on TTM EPS and nearly 67x price-to-sales.</li>
<li><b>Dependence on government budgets</b>: The U.S. government accounts for more than 40% of revenue, so budget and policy changes matter a lot.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Rising receivables</b>: up 35% in one quarter, faster than the rise in customer prepayments.</li>
<li><b>International business</b>: revenue outside the US is about $350M, and growth is concentrated in the US.</li>
<li><b>Stock reaction</b>: it fell despite the beat, a sign expectations were already high.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Contract visibility</b>: US commercial RDV of $4.92B, up 112% YoY.</li>
<li><b>Plenty of cash</b>: $8B in cash and marketable securities and no borrowings.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Big beat**: Q1 revenue of $1.63B (+85%), about $100M above guidance and the company's fastest YoY growth ever.
- **US doubles**: US revenue +104%, with US commercial +133% and US government +84%.
- **Profits soar**: 46% GAAP operating margin, 53% net margin and 60% adjusted operating margin.
- **Guidance raised**: full-year revenue growth raised from about 61% to about 71%.
- **Stock falls**: every metric beat, but the stock fell 6.9% on the first day after earnings.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: With 85% revenue growth and a 46% GAAP operating margin, Palantir's Q1 shows AI software demand exploding, and full-year guidance was raised sharply; but at over 150x earnings, the stock still slipped after a clean beat.
