## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue up 31% YoY, continuing to grow</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue was NT$197.06B, up 31.1% YoY and 7.5% QoQ, and nine-month revenue NT$539.67B, up 37.2% YoY. Operating income was NT$32.62B, up 31.5% YoY, with revenue and profit growing together. The financial statements do not break out products, so the growth drivers need to be read alongside the company's investor conference materials.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Gross margin down 1.6pp QoQ</h3>
<p>Gross margin was 34.08%, down 1.56pp from 35.64% in Q2 and below 34.87% a year earlier. But the operating expense ratio fell from 18.37% to 17.52%, keeping operating margin at 16.55%. The financial statements do not explain the sequential decline in gross margin; it may relate to product mix or costs (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Nine-month EPS already exceeds last year's full year</h3>
<p>Q3 EPS was NT$9.04, up 26.3% YoY but 6.6% below Q2's NT$9.68; nine-month EPS of NT$26.64 is already above full-year 2025's NT$23.14. In Q3 non-operating income fell from NT$2.17B to NT$1.05B, and other gains and losses swung from a NT$510M gain to a NT$250M loss, which is why net income grew more slowly than operating income.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Inventory up 66% as the company stocks up for shipments</h3>
<p>Inventory rose from NT$101.5B at the start of the year to NT$168.2B, with a NT$66.6B increase in inventory on the nine-month cash flow statement; over the same period contract liabilities (customer prepayments) rose NT$7.7B to NT$18.2B. Inventory is growing far faster than revenue, which could lead to write-down risk if demand falls short.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Strong cash flow, rising capex</h3>
<p>Nine-month operating cash flow was NT$90.16B, up 49% YoY, and capex NT$44.88B, up 52%, for free cash flow of about NT$45.28B; cash dividends paid totaled NT$30.13B. Period-end cash was NT$209B, and net cash after borrowings and corporate bonds about NT$124.9B.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue growth**: Q3 revenue of NT$197.06B (+31%, +7.5% QoQ) and operating income of NT$32.62B (+31.5%).
- **EPS at record levels**: Nine-month EPS of NT$26.64 already exceeds full-year 2025's NT$23.14.
- **Falling gross margin**: Q3 gross margin of 34.1% is down for two straight quarters, with a lower expense ratio holding operating margin steady.
- **Inventory surge**: Inventory up 66% from the start of the year to NT$168.2B, and contract liabilities up to NT$18.2B.
- **Strong cash**: Nine-month free cash flow of about NT$45.3B and net cash of about NT$124.9B.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Delta's Q3 revenue and operating income both grew about 30% YoY, and nine-month profit already exceeds last year's total; but two straight quarters of falling gross margin, a surge in inventory and a high P/E led to a clear pullback in the stock after the results.
