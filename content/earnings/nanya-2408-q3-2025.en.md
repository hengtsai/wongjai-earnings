## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue Jumps 78% QoQ</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue reached NT$18.78B, up 78% from NT$10.53B in Q2 and 2.3x last year's NT$8.13B. Cost of revenue rose only 95%, pointing to DRAM price recovery as the main growth driver (our inference; filings do not separate price and volume). Inventories fell 9% from year-start despite surging sales, indicating healthy shipments.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Gross Margin Rebounds 39pp in a Single Quarter</h3>
<p>Gross margin swung from -20.6% in Q2 to 18.5%, a 39pp improvement in one quarter. Operating income reached NT$1.12B (6.0% operating margin) versus a -NT$2.51B loss a year earlier. R&D fell 22% YoY to NT$1.67B, while the OpEx ratio dropped from 34% to 12.5% as higher revenue sharply diluted fixed costs.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Quarterly Swing to Profit, 9M Still in Loss</h3>
<p>Net income attributable to parent reached NT$1.56B (EPS NT$0.50), ending consecutive losses; however, 9M still showed a NT$4.48B loss and -NT$1.45 EPS, worse than -NT$1.13 a year earlier. Deep first-half losses have yet to be offset, making Q3 the first profitable quarter in the memory upcycle off the trough (our inference).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: NT$16.5B Added in Long-Term Borrowings</h3>
<p>Nine-month operating cash flow was only NT$3.06B against NT$11.21B in CapEx, yielding ~-NT$8.1B in free cash flow. Nanya Tech added NT$16.5B in long-term debt and repaid NT$6.9B in short-term debt, yet cash still declined from NT$61.9B at year-start to NT$52.3B. With NT$24.1B in total borrowings, net cash stood at ~NT$28.2B, keeping the balance sheet solid.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Shares Pull Back After Earlier Rally</h3>
<p>Shares fell 6.8% on the first session after the earnings call, dropping from NT$95.6 to NT$89.1, likely reflecting that the return to profit had been priced in ahead of results (our inference). Annualizing Q3 EPS of NT$0.50 implies a ~45x P/E, while TTM P/E is not meaningful due to cumulative losses. Management provides no guidance, so the outlook hinges on whether DRAM price gains continue.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: niche DRAM and Formosa Plastics Group resources</h3>
<p>Nanya Tech focuses on specialty and commodity DRAM with proprietary technology and fabs, backed by the Formosa Plastics Group with a conservative balance sheet that maintains net cash through cycles. This enables it to weather memory downturns and capture rebounds. Its weaknesses are severe industry cyclicality—making profits highly sensitive to pricing—and smaller technological scale than the big three peers (our inference).</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue surge**: Q3 revenue hit NT$18.78B (+131% YoY, +78% QoQ).
- **Gross margin turns positive**: Swung from -20.6% in Q2 to 18.5%, with a 6.0% operating margin.
- **Quarterly profit**: Net income attributable to parent NT$1.56B (EPS NT$0.50), though 9M EPS remained -NT$1.45.
- **Debt-funded cash flow**: 9M free cash flow was ~-NT$8.1B, supported by NT$16.5B in new long-term debt.
- **Solid finances**: Net cash ~NT$28.2B, P/B 1.75x.

> 💡 **Bottom Line**: Nanya Tech's Q3 marked an inflection point in the memory upcycle, with revenue up nearly 80% QoQ and gross margin and earnings swinging positive in a single quarter; with 9M still in a loss and cash flow negative, sustained DRAM price gains will be key to turning a quarterly rebound into full-year profitability.
