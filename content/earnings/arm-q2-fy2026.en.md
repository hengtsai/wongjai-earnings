## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Third Straight Quarter Over $1B</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue reached $1.135B (+34% YoY), topping the $1.01B–$1.11B guidance range for a third straight $1B+ quarter. CEO Haas noted that <b>record royalties reflected record demand for the Arm compute platform</b>. Licensing revenue rose 56% YoY, driven by the timing and scale of multiple high-value deals and backlog contributions, per the shareholder letter.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Royalties Driven by Higher Rates</h3>
<p>Royalty revenue grew 21% YoY to $620M on rising adoption of higher-rate technologies such as Armv9 and CSS, alongside increased Arm chip deployment in data centers, with growth across smartphones, data center, automotive, and IoT. Cumulative CSS licenses reached 19 across 11 companies, with 5 customers shipping and all top four Android smartphone OEMs now shipping CSS-based devices.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Record R&D Yet Margin Expands</h3>
<p>Non-GAAP R&D jumped 45% YoY to a record $466M on engineering headcount expansion. Even so, revenue growth of 34% outpaced total cost growth, lifting Non-GAAP operating margin from 38.6% to 41.1%, with Non-GAAP OpEx of $648M coming in slightly below ~$655M guidance. GAAP operating margin rose from 7.6% to 14.4%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Expanding Data Center Penetration</h3>
<p>The letter noted that Neoverse CPU deployments have surpassed 1B cores, powering NVIDIA Grace, AWS Graviton, Google Axion, and Microsoft Cobalt; Arm estimates its share of CPUs shipped to top hyperscalers will approach 50% this year. A strategic partnership with Meta was also announced in October.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Q3 Revenue Guided at $1.225B</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue is guided at $1.225B ± $50M, implying ~8% QoQ and ~25% YoY growth at the midpoint (our calculation), with Non-GAAP OpEx of ~$720M (+11% QoQ) and Non-GAAP EPS of $0.41 ± $0.04. Related-party revenue jumped to $422M (37% of revenue) from $192M a year ago—a concentration worth monitoring (our assessment).</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q3 Guidance

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core Moat: CPU Architecture & Developer Ecosystem</h3>
<p>Arm's architecture is widely adopted across mobile, data center, automotive, and IoT chips, backed by over 22M developers (>80% globally, per the letter), creating high switching costs through its software ecosystem. CSS shifts Arm from selling standalone IP to compute subsystems with higher royalty rates. Weaknesses include lumpy licensing revenue, rising R&D costs, and elevated related-party revenue concentration.</p>
</div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Revenue above guidance**: Q2 revenue of $1.135B (+34%), third straight $1B+ quarter.
- **Record royalties**: $620M (+21%), lifted by higher Armv9 and CSS rates.
- **Licensing surge**: $515M (+56%), with ACV up 28% YoY.
- **Margin expansion**: Non-GAAP operating margin climbed to 41.1% despite R&D rising 45%.
- **Q3 guidance**: Revenue of $1.225B ± $50M and EPS of $0.41 ± $0.04.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Arm topped the high end of FY26 Q2 revenue and EPS guidance as higher royalty rates and surging licensing drove operating margin expansion despite heavy R&D investment; still, elevated related-party concentration and a 200x+ P/E leave the stock sensitive to licensing lumpiness.
