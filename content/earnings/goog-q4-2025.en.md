## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Cloud up 48% YoY, operating margin jumps from 18% to 30%</h3>
<p>Google Cloud revenue reached $17.7B, up 48% YoY, with operating income of $5.3B (+154% YoY) and operating margin expanding from 17.5% to 30.1%. The press release attributed the growth to enterprise AI infrastructure and AI solutions. Pichai noted that <b>Google Cloud exited 2025 at an annualized revenue run rate exceeding $70B, with broad customer demand driven by AI products</b>. Cloud now accounts for 16% of total revenue.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Search not cannibalized by AI, accelerates to 17% instead</h3>
<p>Search & other revenue was $63.1B, up 17% YoY—nearly double YouTube ads' 9% growth. Pichai stated that <b>Search saw more usage than ever before, with AI continuing to drive an expansionary moment</b>. He also highlighted the launch of Gemini 3 as a major milestone, noting first-party models now process over 10B tokens per minute via API and the Gemini App has over 750M monthly active users. Traffic acquisition costs were $16.6B, up 12% YoY, growing slower than Search revenue.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: 2026 capex of $175–185B, roughly double this year</h3>
<p>Q4 capex was $27.9B, up 95% YoY, bringing full-year capex to $91.4B. Pichai said <b>to meet customer demand and capitalize on future opportunities, Alphabet expects 2026 capital expenditures to be in the range of $175B to $185B</b>, 91–102% higher than 2025 (our estimate). Full-year free cash flow of $73.3B rose just 0.7% YoY, so if 2026 operating cash flow growth fails to keep pace, free cash flow will decline markedly.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: One-time $2.1B Waymo charge weighs on operating margin</h3>
<p>Q4 included a $2.1B Waymo employee compensation charge, mostly booked in R&D based on estimated share valuation; the press release also noted Waymo's $16B financing round announced in February, largely funded by Alphabet. Other Bets operating loss widened from $1.17B to $3.62B, and combined with Alphabet-level expenses (shared AI R&D) doubling to $5.89B, consolidated operating margin edged down 0.5pp YoY; excluding the Waymo charge, it would have been roughly 33.4% (our estimate).</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: EPS growth boosted by valuation gains and lower tax rate</h3>
<p>Operating income rose 16% YoY, while EPS jumped 31%. The press release noted a $2.3B gain on equity securities added $0.15 to Q4 EPS, and the effective tax rate fell from 17.7% to 11.9%. Full-year securities valuation gains reached $24.6B, and non-marketable securities jumped by $30.7B over one year to $68.7B—large enough to meaningfully swing EPS. Full-year YouTube ads plus subscription revenue exceeded $60B.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Moat and 2026 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: Search traffic + proprietary models and TPU compute</h3>
<p>Search usage hit record highs with AI Overviews not eroding ad revenue; Gemini models, custom TPUs and Google Cloud form a full stack from silicon to applications. Over 325M paid consumer subscriptions and over $60B in annual YouTube revenue give Alphabet multiple cash engines to fund capex.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Capex doubling</b>: $175–185B in 2026 is roughly double 2025, which could sharply reduce free cash flow or turn it negative.</li>
<li><b>Rapidly rising depreciation</b>: Depreciation grew 38% YoY and will keep compressing operating margins after capex doubles.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>EPS includes valuation gains</b>: Full-year securities gains of $24.6B mean any pullback in portfolio valuations would reverse and hit EPS.</li>
<li><b>Widening Other Bets cash burn</b>: Operating loss tripled, and Waymo's $16B round is mostly funded by Alphabet.</li>
<li><b>YouTube slowdown</b>: YouTube ad revenue grew just 9%, the slowest among core ad segments.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Annual revenue tops $400B**: Q4 revenue of $113.8B (+18%), full-year $402.8B (+15%).
- **Cloud profitability takeoff**: Revenue up 48% YoY, 30% operating margin, annualized revenue over $70B.
- **Search acceleration**: Search revenue of $63.1B (+17%); AI did not cannibalize ads and instead drove usage.
- **EPS boosted by non-operating items**: Operating income +16% vs. EPS +31%, with the gap driven by valuation gains and a lower tax rate.
- **Capex doubling**: 2026 projected at $175–185B, while 2025 free cash flow already grew just 0.7%.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Alphabet proved AI is lifting both Search and Cloud while Cloud is now generating meaningful profit; however, with 2026 capex doubling outright, the key over the next year is whether revenue growth can keep pace with this spending.
