## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: AWS accelerates to 37%; AI and in-house chips each top $25B</h3>
<p>AWS revenue growth climbed from 17% in Q2'25 to 20%, 24%, 28% and 37%, for an annualized run rate of $169B; operating income was $16.6B, up 64% YoY, a 39.4% margin. <b>"AWS is booming, growing 36.7% year-over-year in Q2—our fastest growth in 18 quarters—and our AI and Chips businesses each eclipsed run rates of more than $25 billion,"</b> Jassy said; both are growing at triple digits. Anthropic and OpenAI have both made multi-year, multi-GW commitments to Trainium, and Bedrock customers' Q2 spending exceeded all prior quarters combined.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Two-thirds of pre-tax income is non-operating</h3>
<p>The press release said Q2 other income of $53.4B came <b>"primarily from our investments in Anthropic"</b>. Non-operating items made up 66% of pre-tax income of $80.9B. Excluding other income and applying a 21% tax rate, core EPS would be about $1.99 (about $1.44 a year ago on the same basis), still up about 38% YoY but far below the reported $5.75 (our estimate). A further $42B (after tax) of unrealized gains on available-for-sale debt securities sits in other comprehensive income and did not go through the income statement.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Trailing-12-month free cash flow turns negative for the first time</h3>
<p>Q2 purchases of property and equipment were $54.2B, up 68% YoY and above operating cash flow of $45.4B. Trailing-12-month net capex rose by $66.1B YoY, which the press release said <b>"primarily reflects investments in artificial intelligence"</b>, and free cash flow went from $18.2B a year ago to -$7.6B. Amazon issued $67B of long-term debt in the first half ($13.6B in Q2 alone), nearly doubling long-term debt in six months to $128.9B; net of cash and marketable securities, net debt is about $5.9B, excluding lease liabilities.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: Retail accelerates, North America revenue up 16% YoY</h3>
<p>North America revenue grew 16% YoY (11% a year ago), online stores 15% and third-party seller services 16%, with paid units up 17% YoY; moving Prime Day into Q2 this year (as the company noted in its Q3 guidance) also lifted revenue. Shipping costs rose 19% YoY, and North America operating margin only edged up from 7.5% to 7.9%, so the acceleration was mostly volume. Jassy said: <b>"In Stores, we again set record delivery speeds for Prime members in the first half of the year—over 40% more items delivered same-day or overnight, with Grocery and Everyday Essentials growing meaningfully faster than the rest of the business."</b> Amazon Now, with 30-minute delivery, has expanded to more than 250 towns and cities in 9 countries.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Ads accelerate to 26%, AI shopping assistant scales up</h3>
<p>Advertising revenue was $19.8B, up 26% YoY (22% in Q1). <b>"Advertising had another strong quarter with 26% year-over-year growth,"</b> Jassy said. Advertisers using Ads Agent saw 8% lower cost per impression. Rufus and Alexa+ were combined into Alexa for Shopping, with active users nearly doubling YoY and interactions up more than 5x; U.S. customers shopping with Alexa spend over 40% more per order on average. In its first season carrying the NBA, Prime Video drew a peak U.S. audience of 6.5M for Game 7 of the Eastern Conference semifinals.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q3 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: AWS scale + in-house chips + its own logistics network</h3>
<p>AWS has a $169B annualized run rate and nearly 40% operating margin; Graviton is used by 98% of the top 1,000 EC2 customers, and Trainium has locked in two major AI labs. In retail, third-party sellers account for 61% of units; its own logistics expands same-day and overnight delivery, and advertising and subscriptions monetize the traffic.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>Negative cash flow, more borrowing</b>: Trailing-12-month free cash flow is -$7.6B, $67B of debt was issued in the first half, and interest expense is already 2.5 times last year's.</li>
<li><b>EPS depends on investment marks</b>: Q2 non-operating gains of $53.4B were 66% of pre-tax income, so changes in Anthropic's valuation directly amplify EPS swings.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Faster depreciation</b>: D&A rose 31% YoY, outpacing revenue growth of 20%; ever-rising capex will squeeze AWS margins.</li>
<li><b>Q3 revenue down QoQ</b>: Guidance implies only 9–12% YoY growth, as Prime Day timing and FX make next quarter look weaker.</li>
<li><b>Component and energy costs</b>: Guidance names memory chips and energy prices for the first time, so data center build costs may rise.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>AWS acceleration</b>: Four straight quarters of acceleration to 37%, with AI and chips both growing at triple digits.</li>
<li><b>New-business optionality</b>: Zoox received an exemption for paid rides and nearly 400 Leo satellites are in orbit, long-term growth sources.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **AWS re-accelerates**: Up 37% YoY, fastest in 18 quarters, with a 39.4% operating margin; AI and chips each have annualized revenue above $25B.
- **Record core profit**: Operating income of $27.5B (+43%), a 13.7% margin, with North America and advertising both accelerating.
- **EPS inflated by non-operating items**: Net income of $62.6B includes $53.4B of Anthropic-related gains; core EPS is estimated at about $1.99.
- **Cash flow turns negative**: Q2 capex of $54.2B, trailing-12-month free cash flow of -$7.6B, and $67B of debt issued in six months.
- **Conservative Q3 guidance**: Revenue up 9–12% YoY, or nearly 4 percentage points higher excluding Prime Day timing.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: Amazon's three engines, AWS, retail and advertising, all accelerated in Q2, but most of the reported EPS came from Anthropic valuation gains, and AI capex pushed trailing-12-month free cash flow negative for the first time; judge this company by operating income, not EPS.
