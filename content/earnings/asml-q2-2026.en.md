## 2. 🔍 Five Key Insights and Management Commentary

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 1: Revenue and gross margin beat, driven by service revenue</h3>
<p>Q2 revenue was €9.33B with a gross margin of 54.0%, both above the €8.4–9.0B and 51–52% guidance. CEO Christophe Fouquet said both beats were <b>mainly driven by higher-than-expected installed base management revenue</b> (paraphrased): installed base management was €2.76B, up 32% YoY, outpacing system sales growth of 17%.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 2: Full-year guidance raised from €36–40B to €43–45B</h3>
<p>The full-year revenue guidance midpoint was raised from €38B to €44B, up €6B, and gross margin guidance from 51–53% to 54–56%. Fouquet said AI investment and advances in AI technology are lifting demand for advanced logic and memory, with customers <b>continuing to accelerate their capacity expansion plans</b> (paraphrased) and turning them into commitments across product lines.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 3: Extremely strong 1H orders; 2027 capacity to expand 30%</h3>
<p>Fouquet said <b>order intake remained extremely strong</b> (paraphrased) in 1H. The company plans to raise its low-NA EUV capacity of about 65 systems in 2026 by 30% in 2027 and is studying another 30% for 2028; DUV immersion capacity of about 130 systems in 2026 will also rise 30% in 2027. Upgrade offerings will expand significantly as well.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 4: EUV is 57% of system sales; South Korea is the largest market</h3>
<p>Q2 saw 16 EUV systems sold, at 57% of system sales, and 23 ArFi systems at 29%. Memory made up 49%, close to logic; by region South Korea led with 43%, Taiwan 30% and China only 14%. The high memory and Korea shares reflect memory makers expanding capacity for AI.</p>
</div>

<div class="sb">
<h3>🎯 Insight 5: Q3 revenue guidance of €11–12B, with gross margin rising again</h3>
<p>Q3 revenue guidance is €11–12B, with the midpoint about 53% above the €7.52B of a year earlier, and gross margin of 55–57%; R&D about €1.2B and SG&A about €400M. In Q2 the company bought back about €1.1B of shares and announced an interim dividend of €1.88 per share payable on 8/5.</p>
</div>

## 3. 🛡️ Competitive Moat and Q3 Outlook

<div class="sb">
<h3>🏰 Core moat: sole EUV supplier plus installed base services</h3>
<p>ASML is the world's only supplier of EUV lithography systems, which advanced nodes cannot do without. The larger the installed base, the steadier the upgrade and service revenue; this quarter service revenue grew faster than systems and drove the gross margin beat. Capacity expansion takes years of planning, so capacity itself is a barrier to entry.</p>
</div>

## 4. ⚠️ Risks

<h3 class="risk-red">🔴 High Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rh"><ul>
<li><b>2H delivery pressure</b>: Full-year guidance implies 2H revenue about 40% above 1H, so even slight execution delays could cause a miss.</li>
<li><b>Export controls</b>: In Q1 the company said its guidance range accounted for possible outcomes of export control discussions, but policy remains uncertain.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-gold">🟡 Medium Risk</h3>
<div class="sb rm"><ul>
<li><b>Rising receivables</b>: Up €2.85B in the quarter, and 1H operating cash flow was negative.</li>
<li><b>Customer concentration</b>: South Korea and Taiwan together account for 73% of system sales.</li>
<li><b>Expansion costs</b>: A 30% capacity expansion in 2027 requires the supply chain to keep pace.</li>
</ul></div>

<h3 class="risk-green">🟢 Low Risk / Opportunity</h3>
<div class="sb rl"><ul>
<li><b>Sharp guidance raise</b>: Full-year revenue guidance midpoint up €6B and gross margin up 3pp.</li>
<li><b>Service revenue</b>: Installed base management up 32% YoY, providing stable profits.</li>
</ul></div>

## 6. 📋 Key Takeaways

- **Double beat**: Q2 revenue of €9.33B and gross margin of 54.0%, both above guidance, driven by installed base management revenue.
- **Big guidance raise**: Full-year revenue guidance raised from €36–40B to €43–45B and gross margin to 54–56%.
- **30% expansion**: Low-NA EUV and DUV immersion capacity each up 30% in 2027, with another 30% under study for 2028.
- **Strong memory**: Memory is 49% of system sales, with South Korea at 43%.
- **Q3 accelerates again**: Q3 revenue guidance of €11–12B, with the midpoint up about 53% YoY.

> 💡 **Bottom line**: ASML's Q2 double beat was carried by service revenue; more importantly, full-year guidance was raised by €6B in one go and a 30% capacity expansion was announced, showing that AI-driven expansion in advanced logic and memory has turned into real orders.
